For this play: print the 3 Characters version for a team of three (photocopy one set per team) or the 2 Characters version for the pair; the pair can take any play's 2 Characters script. Each child gets ONLY their own role's pages (two pages each), plus the team page. The last page is the teacher's copy: do not hand it out.

The grammar and words of the month
  • have to · don't have to · has to · doesn't have to   Do you have to…? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • What would you like?   I'd like…, please.
  • How often?   every day · once a week · twice a week · once a month
  • In my opinion, fresh food is better than tinned food.
  • Garden: dig · plant · water · weed · pick
  • Vegetables: courgette · aubergine · green beans · pumpkin · cabbage · chillis · onion

Unit 2 Review Play Fresh Food

Teacher's copy full script · do not hand out
Use this to check the children's dictation. Timing for a team: hand out and read the steps (3 min) · read and write (12 min) · check together (4 min) · perform twice, swapping roles (8 min) · about 27 minutes in all.
Scene 1 · The Turia gardens
DaniWow, look at the pumpkins! We have to pick them today.
NuriaMarta, do you have to dig too?
MartaYes, I do. I dig once a week, and I plant the green beans.
NuriaPoor Marta! I don't have to dig. I have to water the chillis every day.
MartaDani, how often do you weed the onions?
DaniTwice a week. Nuria has to weed the cabbages, but Marta doesn't have to.
DaniOK, team! Now we have to cycle to the market. Get your bikes!
Guest[A new student, Guest, arrives on a bike.] Hello! I'm new. What do I have to do?
MartaWelcome, Guest! You have to pick the pumpkins with me.
Scene 2 · The Mercado de Colón
NuriaWelcome to my aunt's stall! What would you like, everyone?
DaniI'd like a big pumpkin, please. It's for my grandad's soup.
GuestAnd I'd like some green beans, please.
NuriaOf course, Guest! Here you are.
MartaIn my opinion, tinned food is better than fresh food.
NuriaDo you have to say that at my aunt's stall, Marta?
MartaNo, I don't! Sorry. Tins are easy.
DaniMarta, how often do you eat courgettes?
Marta[tasting a courgette] Once a month. But they're so good! In my opinion, fresh food is better than tinned food.
NuriaTake some aubergines and onions too, Marta. They're from our huerta!
DaniThanks! Now I have to put the pumpkin on my bike.
MartaIt's too big! It doesn't fit!
Nuria[watching the pumpkin roll away] Dani, your pumpkin! Run!
