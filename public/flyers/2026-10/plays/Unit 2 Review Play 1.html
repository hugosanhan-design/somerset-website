For this play: print the 3 Characters version for a team of three (photocopy one set per team) or the 2 Characters version for the pair; the pair can take any play's 2 Characters script. Each child gets ONLY their own role's pages (two pages each), plus the team page. The last page is the teacher's copy: do not hand it out.

Team page check, then perform
Cast: Marta, Dani and Nuria, and Guest (new student). Write your team's names on the cast line before you start.
Before you perform: put your pages together. Read the whole play aloud, once, and check that what you wrote matches what your team really said. Fix any mistakes. Then stand up and perform it for the class. Look at your team, not at the page.
★ Now perform it! ★
The grammar and words of the month
  • have to · don't have to · has to · doesn't have to   Do you have to…? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • What would you like?   I'd like…, please.
  • How often?   every day · once a week · twice a week · once a month
  • In my opinion, fresh food is better than tinned food.
  • Garden: dig · plant · water · weed · pick
  • Vegetables: courgette · aubergine · green beans · pumpkin · cabbage · chillis · onion
Then swap roles and perform again. Finished early? Change it:
The vegetable: green beans →
How often: once a month →

Unit 2 Review Play Fresh Food

Teacher's copy full script · do not hand out
Use this to check the children's dictation. Timing for a team: hand out and read the steps (3 min) · read and write (12 min) · check together (4 min) · perform twice, swapping roles (8 min) · about 27 minutes in all.
Scene 1 · The school huerta, Ruzafa
MartaQuick, everyone! We have to dig here and plant the pumpkin seeds.
DaniDo you have to dig too, Nuria?
NuriaNo, I don't. I have to weed the cabbages and the onions.
MartaHow often do you weed them, Nuria?
NuriaTwice a week. Dani has to water the aubergines every day!
DaniEvery day?! Marta doesn't have to water. Marta only picks chillis once a week!
MartaWrong! I have to dig, but you don't have to. Pick the green beans!
Guest[A new student, Guest, arrives with a spade.] Hello! I'm new. What do I have to do?
MartaWelcome, Guest! You have to water the pumpkin seeds.
Scene 2 · The Mercado Central
NuriaWelcome to our stall! Courgettes, aubergines, chillis: what would you like?
MartaI'd like a kilo of green beans, please. They're for the paella valenciana.
DaniWhy? I've got a tin. In my opinion, tinned food is better than fresh food.
Nuria[hands over a green bean] Tinned?! Try a fresh one first.
Dani[chewing] Hmm. It's... good.
MartaNuria, how often do you eat paella?
NuriaOnce a month. Mum has to cook it, but my brother doesn't have to help!
DaniOK! I'd like some fresh green beans too, please.
GuestAnd I'd like some chillis, please.
NuriaOf course, Guest! Here you are.
MartaAnd the tin?
Dani[puts the tin on the counter] In my opinion, fresh food is better than tinned food.
Nuria[smiling] Keep the tin, Dani. It's a good pot for pumpkin seeds!
