Lunch at the Mercado Central

Grammar for this play: would like / I'd like for polite orders and how often with every day, once a week, twice a week.
MarisaTom
The café at the Mercado Central in Valencia. Tom is visiting from London. He is very hungry.
MarisaGood afternoon! Welcome to the café at the Mercado Central. What would you like?
TomI'd like some pumpkin soup, please. How often do you make it?
MarisaI make it every day! The pumpkins come from L'Horta twice a week.
TomLovely. I'd like a tomato salad too, please. Are the tomatoes ripe?
MarisaYes! My brother has to pick them every morning. Would you like some courgette bread too?
TomYes, please! How often do people come here?
MarisaSome people come once a week. Some people come every day!
TomI'm only here for one week, but I'd like to come every day!
MarisaHere you are. You don't have to pay now. Pay at the end!
MarisaTomLucía
Tom is visiting Valencia from London. Lucía takes him to her favourite café.
MarisaGood afternoon! Welcome to the café at the Mercado Central. What would you like?
TomI'd like some pumpkin soup, please. How often do you make it?
MarisaI make it every day! The pumpkins come from L'Horta twice a week.
LucíaI'd like a tomato salad, please. Are the tomatoes ripe?
MarisaYes! My brother has to pick them every morning.
TomWould you like some courgette bread too, Lucía?
LucíaYes, please! I come here once a month. It's my favourite café.
MarisaHere you are. You don't have to pay now. Pay at the end!
★ Now perform it!

Two roles for a pair. Swap roles and do it twice.

Three roles for a trio. Swap roles and do it twice.
