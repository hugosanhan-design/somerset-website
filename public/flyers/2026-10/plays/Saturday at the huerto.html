The play · Lesson 2 · Wed 7 Oct · Pairs or trios · 5 minutes

Saturday at the huerto

Grammar for this play: have to / don't have to and the question Do we have to…? Words to listen for: ripe, water, weed, dig, pick.
Grandma AmparoLeo
A community garden in Benimaclet, Valencia. Saturday morning. Grandma and Leo have a busy day.
Grandma AmparoGood morning, Leo! The tomatoes are ripe. We have to pick them today.
LeoDo we have to dig too, Grandma?
Grandma AmparoNo, you don't have to dig. But you have to water the beans.
LeoAnd you, Grandma? Do you have to weed?
Grandma AmparoYes, I do! I have to weed. There are lots of weeds here!
LeoGrandma, do we have to go to the market after this?
Grandma AmparoYes! We have to sell the tomatoes at the market in Benimaclet.
LeoGreat! I don't have to go to school today. It's Saturday!
Grandma AmparoAnd I don't have to cook lunch. We can eat at the market!
LeoAnd I don't have to tidy my room today!
