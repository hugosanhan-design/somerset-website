Unit 2 Test · Listening script
Audio file: audio/test/Unit 2 Test - Listening.mp3
Introduction. "Unit 2 test. Part 1, listening. You will hear five short conversations. Listen and circle a, b or c. In questions 3, 4 and 5, also write one word. You will hear each conversation twice."
1. Woman: Do you have to tidy your room today, Nico?   Nico: No, I don't. My sister has to tidy it. I have to help my dad in the garden.  → b
2. Girl: Hello! I'd like two courgettes and an aubergine, please.   Seller: Of course. Anything else?   Girl: Yes, a big cabbage. That's all, thank you.  → a
3. Girl: We water our plants twice a week. And we pick the green beans every day. They're ready to eat!  → b + every day
4. Waiter: What would you like to drink?   Girl: I'd like an orange juice, please. My brother would like a water.  → b + orange
5. Man: Let's plant the seeds! First we dig the ground. Then we plant the seeds and water them. In October, we pick the pumpkins.   Girl: I love pumpkin soup!  → b + pumpkins

Marking rules

Part 1: items 1–2 are circle only. Items 3–5 need the circle and the written word; any readable spelling (pumkins is fine).
Part 2: the whole form is needed: has to, not has. Item 5 needs Do and have to for the point. Accept the full does not / do not.
Parts 3 and 4: one point each, spelling readable. Chilli or chillies both fine.
Part 5 (5 points): 1 point for a correct sentence with has to (or have to for plural); 1 for don't have to at the weekend; 1 for an order with I'd like; 1 for an opinion with In my opinion; 1 for capital letters and full stops on all four. Score each criterion as yes or no and quote the child's words on the marking sheet. A fluent sentence that misses the criterion gets no point.
