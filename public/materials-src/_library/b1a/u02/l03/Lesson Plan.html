Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can identify key words before listening to focus their attention, complete a multiple-choice-with-one-conversation exam task, and describe a photograph using paraphrasing when they don't know a word.
Target language. Cardinal and ordinal numbers; key-word underlining strategy; paraphrase frames ("it's something we...", "it's someone who...").
Exam link. B1 Preliminary Listening Part 2 (multiple choice) and Speaking Part 2 (photo description) — both exam tasks on this spread carry the exact exam label.
Materials: Student's Book pp.22-23. Audio: Track 2.2, 2.3, 2.4 (numbers, gapped exam-tip practice, exam task), Track 2.5 (model photo description). Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b1a-u02-l03. If Unit 2's Play 1 didn't happen at the end of l02, open it here after speaking.

2–3. Listening: numbers & key words · 25 min

Materials: Track 2.2, 2.3, 2.4. Student's Book p.22.

Ex.1 (Track 2.2): listen to five pairs of numbers, then drill pronunciation in pairs — ordinal endings (7th, 8th, 18th, 25th, 20th, 21st) are the trap. Ex.2–3 (Track 2.3): read the Exam Tip on identifying key words first (they're usually verbs, adjectives or nouns, not prepositions or articles); underline the key words in the five questions, then listen and answer using the A–C options from Ex.1.

Ex.2 answers: 1 15 · 2 €222 · 3 7th August · 4 on the 21st · 5 three.

3. Exam Task: multiple choice with one conversation · 15 min

Materials: Track 2.4. Student's Book p.22.

Underline the important words in the six questions and their A–C options before listening (Ex.3). Play Track 2.4 twice: a radio interview about the London Restaurant Festival, its Amazing Spaces restaurants, its sustainability angle, the Gourmet Odyssey and its masterclasses.

Answers: 1B (one month) · 2C (about 250) · 3B (restaurants with interesting furniture and design) · 4B (has information about environmentally-friendly restaurants) · 5A (three courses in three different restaurants) · 6B (people can learn how to make different dishes).

Valencia link: ask if students know a local food festival or event (Fira de Juliol food stalls, the Ruzafa street-food nights, or La Tomatina's neighbour towns). Would they go to a "Valencia Restaurant Festival" with a horchata masterclass?

4–7. Speaking: describing food, paraphrasing, photo description · 50 min

Materials: Student's Book p.23, Track 2.5.

Ex.1–2: pair discussion questions, then describe the kitchen photo (five guided questions: how many people, what they look like, where, what they're doing, what else is in the background). Ex.3: listen to the model (Track 2.5) and check whether he covers all five points, and what he adds. Ex.4: the Exam Tip on paraphrasing — if you don't know "barbecue", say "cooking food outside on a grill". Ex.5: the real Exam Task, new pairs, Student A describes the beach barbecue photo, Student B (turn to p.89 in their own book, or describe a second food photo you print/project) describes theirs, using the Useful Language box (This photo shows people..., In the background/foreground I can see...).

No "answer" here to reveal — this is a speaking task, not an exercise with a key. The Vista deck instead projects the Useful Language box so students can refer to it while they talk.

8. Somerset Arcade + carry-over play · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u02-l03.

Point Grab built from today's listening numbers/festival facts and the speaking Useful Language frames. If Unit 2's Play 1 (Unit2_Delicious_TasteAndPast_Play.html: taste & past tenses) hasn't run yet, open it now, after speaking, per the group's standing "carries forward, never lost" rule.

Fast finisher

Fast-finishing pairs describe a photo of their own choice (phone photo of a meal, a Valencia street) using at least two paraphrase frames from the Useful Language box.

If short of time, cut:

The pair-rotation step in block 7 (one description each is enough if time is tight). Do not cut the Exam Task listening (block 3): multiple choice with one conversation is a full exam-task type and needs the full two plays of the track.
