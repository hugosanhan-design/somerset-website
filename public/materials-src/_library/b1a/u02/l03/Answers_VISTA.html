Ex.1-2 · numbers & key words

Numbers check

🎧 Track 2.2 / 2.3
  • 1How many people came to the party? → 15
  • 2How much did the meal cost? → €222
  • 3When is Billy's birthday? → 7th August
  • 4When will the boy arrive in Mexico? → on the 21st
  • 5How many slices of pizza has the girl eaten? → three
Why: ordinal endings (7th, 21st) sound close to cardinal numbers (7, 20) — the trap is always in the ending, not the number itself. Underline key words before listening: verbs, nouns and question words, not prepositions or articles.

Speaking Ex.1-2 · useful language

Talking about food and photos

Discussion starters: Who usually prepares the food in your house? How often do you eat out? Do you prefer food cooked at home or in a restaurant?

Describing a photo: How many people are in the photo? What do the people look like? Where are they? What are they doing? What else can you see in the background?

Exam Tip · paraphrasing

Don't know the word? Describe it

Frames: it's something we... · it's someone who... · it's somewhere we (+ verb)... · The person is / people are... + -ing · It / He / She / They look(s) + adjective.

Example: don't know "barbecue"? Say "The people are cooking / grilling food outside."
Exam Task · photo description

Describe your photo

This photo shows people (at a restaurant / in a park / at home / on a beach). The woman (in the hat / with the glasses / holding the burger) is... They are outside / inside. The weather is warm / cold. They are (enjoying the food / having fun / feeling happy)... In the background / foreground, I can see (some buildings / trees / people).

Valencia link: one student describes a real photo from a Valencia meal (a Fallas paella, a Malvarrosa beach barbecue, or the Central Market) using the same frames.
