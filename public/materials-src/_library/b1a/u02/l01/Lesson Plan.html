Aims. By the end of the lesson, students see their Unit 1 test results laid out unit by unit and know what's coming next; they can discuss restaurant preferences, skim an article to find its general description, and complete a "matching people to texts" exam task.
Target language. Opener discussion vocabulary (eat out, takeaway, portion size); reading strategies (skimming for general meaning, scanning for detail, underlining key points); word focus (award, district, absolutely, stew, queue, retro).
Exam link. B1 Preliminary Reading Part 3 (matching descriptions to texts) is the exact exam task on pp.18-19.

1. Unit 1 results deck · 20 min

Materials: the Unit 1 results PowerPoint (cloned .pptx, _method/level-ladder-results-scripts/), projector.

The Unit 1 test (45 min, 38 marks: Reading /5, Vocabulary /10, Grammar /15, Listening /8) is the source data. Present the class score by section first, name what's solid and what's "not yet" (per section, whichever scored under half), then say when each weak point gets a second pass this year. Individual cards are anonymised by letter on the shared screen; open a student's own card quietly on a laptop if a one-to-one moment is useful, never project a name.

2–7. Reading: matching people to texts · 55 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.17-19, Ex.1–6.

Full running order in the table above. Word Focus items: award (a prize), district (an area of a town), absolutely (very, completely), stew (meat/veg cooked in liquid), queue (a line of people waiting), retro (from the recent past).

Ex.3: C (descriptions of restaurants in one city).
Ex.5: 1 vegetarian food → A, C, H · 2 not a lot of money → B, E, G · 3 award-winning food → C, D · 4 fish dishes recommended → A, F.
Ex.6 (Exam Task): 1 Ewa → H (Vinyanova, farm animals, children's menu) · 2 Raquel → D (Lasarte, special occasion, shops nearby) · 3 Stan → C (Xavier Pellicer, well-known, vegetable-based but adds meat) · 4 Ahmed → A (Agut, traditional, art on the walls, central) · 5 Pierre → G (Peggy Sue's, cheap, fast food, retro music).

Valencia link: the article describes restaurants in Barcelona (Plaça Reial). Ask students which Valencia square or street they'd recommend for the same kind of article (Plaça de la Reina, Ruzafa, the Turia gardens) and why.

8. Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u02-l01.

Built from today's reading vocabulary and the restaurant-matching logic, per the group's standard Day 1 pattern (revise vocab from the reading if there's time).

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write one extra sentence for a ninth "restaurant I" of their own invention, in the same style as the article, for the rest of the group to match to a description.

If short of time, cut:

The individual results-deck cards (keep the whole-class view only, save one-to-one moments for the corridor or the next writing conference). Do not cut the Exam Task itself (block 7): it is the exact B1 Preliminary Reading Part 3 format and needs the full 15 minutes.
