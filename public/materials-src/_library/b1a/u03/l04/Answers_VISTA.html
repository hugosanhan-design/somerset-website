Complete the Garbage Patch text

Plastic in the ocean breaks into millions of tiny pieces called microplastics. This rubbish destroys habitats for fish and other sea animals. Some of it prevents turtles and other animals from finding food. Most of the rubbish never succeeds in reaching the shore, because it stays in the middle of the ocean. Many young sea creatures don't survive because they eat small pieces of plastic. A young inventor called Boyan Slat set up a project called The Ocean Cleanup to design machines that collect plastic. The machine collects plastic from the surface using long floating barriers. Scientists hope The Ocean Cleanup can remove most of the patch's plastic within a few years.

Grammar Ex.1 · articles, concept check

a/an, the, or nothing?

  • 1"I bought a new house. The house has got a garden." → a = first mention, the = specific
  • 2"She's an engineer." → a/an for someone's job
  • 3"the Sahara", "the Nile" → the with deserts, mountain ranges, oceans and rivers
  • 4"the poor", "the British" → the with adjectives used as nouns
  • 5"Sugar is bad for your health" vs "Pass me the sugar" → no article for general, the for specific
  • 6"He's in hospital" vs "The new hospital is well-equipped" → no article for purpose, the for the actual building
Countable nouns in these examples: house, garden, engineer; uncountable: sugar, health.
