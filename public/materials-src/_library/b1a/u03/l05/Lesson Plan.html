1–4. Writing: an email about the environment · 40 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.38-39, Ex.1–7.
Ex.1 (lead-in): 1 an email · 2 a friend · 3 things you've done to protect the environment · 4 informal.
Ex.2 (T/F): 1F · 2F · 3T · 4T.
Ex.3 (informal expressions): Hi, How are things?, Write soon.
Ex.4 (paragraph matching): 1d · 2a · 3b · 4c.
Ex.7 (planning notes): an email … to a friend … about things you and your friends have done … look after the environment … describe the things you have done at home and … at school.

Exam Tip: underline the key points in the task first, then write short-phrase notes for opening, main news and closing — never full sentences at the planning stage.

5. Exam Task: writing an email · 30 min

Materials: Student's Book p.39, Ex.8. Written individually, in class.

Task: "Write an email (100–130 words) to a friend about things you and your friends have done to look after the environment." Remind students to use informal greetings, questions and endings, and to refer back to Speaking p35's poster and Vocabulary p36's verbs for ideas (recycle, plant trees, take public transport, clean up a beach).

Valencia link: suggest one real local example students can genuinely write about — a class beach-clean at the Malvarrosa, recycling at home, or walking instead of taking the car in a city with excellent public transport like Valencia's Metrovalencia and bike lanes.

Collect every script at the end of the lesson. They will be marked before l06, which opens with the group error-review before the Unit 3 test.

7. Live well, study well: the environment, achieving change · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.40.

Useful vocabulary: break down (phr v, become smaller and smaller until it disappears), end up (phr v, arrive somewhere unplanned), gas (n, a type of air, sometimes harmful), journey (n, a trip from one place to another), persuade (v, get someone to do something by giving good reasons). Add break down and end up to the phrasal verb notebook.

Ex.1: predict the poster's sections, then read to check and order the activities (own answers, discussion). Ex.2–3: discuss the poster's advice and personal habits (own answers). Ex.4: the Mind your Mind box on achieving goals (own answers, can be a friend or family member's habit). Ex.5: in small groups, agree on a plan for changes the class could make. Project 1 (discussion, presenting encouragement ideas to friends) and Project 2 (poster with five environment tips) can be assigned as ongoing group work if there's no time to finish both today.

Valencia link: ask students for one realistic habit change relevant to Valencia life (e.g. using the Metrovalencia or Valenbisi bikes instead of asking for a car ride, or separating rubbish correctly for the city's recycling bins).

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers who finish the email early to write two more sentences with phrasal verbs from the Live well vocabulary (break down, end up).

If short of time, cut:

The Live well projects (Ex.5, Project 1–2) — discuss and start the plan, but finish the poster or presentation another day if needed. Never cut the writing task itself (block 5): it is the graded piece corrected in l06.
