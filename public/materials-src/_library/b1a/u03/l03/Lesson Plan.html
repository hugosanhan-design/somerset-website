2–3. Listening: sharks · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book p.34, Ex.1–4. Audio: shark facts recording + the marine biologist's talk.

Ex.1: match word types (a number, a country, a noun, a part of the body, an adjective) to context. Ex.2: listen to a short factual recording about Great White Sharks and complete six facts. Ex.3: apply the Exam Tip (predict the type of answer for each of the six Exam Task gaps before listening). Ex.4: the Exam Task itself, a marine biologist explaining why sharks rarely attack humans.

Ex.1: 1c · 2b · 3c · 4a · 5d · 6e.
Ex.2 (shark facts): 1 4 to 5 (metres long) · 2 grey · 3 3,000 (teeth) · 4 Dolphins (what they eat, with seals) · 5 tail (their powerful swimming part) · 6 Smell (their best sense).
Ex.3 (predicted answer type): 1 a number · 2 a country · 3 a noun · 4 a part of the body · 5 a number · 6 an adjective.
Ex.4 (Exam Task): 1 64 (recorded shark attacks in 2019) · 2 US · 3 information · 4 bones · 5 100 million · 6 expensive.

Valencia link: the Mediterranean does have sharks, though attacks are extremely rare — ask if anyone has heard of a shark sighting off the Comunidad Valenciana coast (occasional harmless blue sharks are reported near Denia and the Costa Blanca).

4–6. Speaking: the environment · 45 min

Materials: Student's Book p.35, Ex.1, Ex.3–4.

Ex.1: complete the environmental-action poster with eight verbs. Ex.3: listen to the examiner's instructions for the Speaking Exam Task and complete them. Ex.4: the Exam Task itself — in pairs, talk together for about two minutes about a school committee deciding which environmental project to run, then agree which would be best.

Ex.1 (poster verbs): 1 Write (to your local council) · 2 Recycle (plastic and paper) · 3 Plant (a tree) · 4 Take (public transport) · 5 Reduce (how much water you use) · 6 walk (or cycle instead of driving) · 7 Talk (to your friends about the environment) · 8 clean (up your local area).
Ex.3 (instructions): 1 two (minutes) · 2 school · 3 projects · 4 protect · 5 different · 6 best.
Ex.4: open-ended speaking, no key. Useful Language box on screen: "I think the best idea is...", "What do you think about...?", "I agree, because...", "On the other hand...", "Shall we decide on...?"

Valencia link: use a real local example as one of the "projects" — e.g. a school beach-clean day at the Malvarrosa, a recycling point in Ruzafa, or a tree-planting day in the Turia gardens — so the discussion has a genuine local option to compare against the book's ideas.

7. Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b1a-u03-l03.

Question bank covers the shark facts, listening strategy, and the eight environmental-action verbs.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write three more sentences using verbs from the environmental-action poster, e.g. "I have reduced the amount of plastic I use" (practising the present perfect from l02 at the same time).

If short of time, cut:

The Arcade round (block 7) can move to the start of l04 if the speaking task overruns — speaking practice always takes priority over a review game. Do not cut the Listening Exam Task (Ex.4): it is graded exam-format practice.
