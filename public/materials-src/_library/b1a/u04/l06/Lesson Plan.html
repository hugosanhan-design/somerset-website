1–4. Writing error-review · 40 min

Materials: marked l05 scripts, board, Answers_VISTA.html, Somerset Arcade, Fix-It Sheets.

Teacher prep, done between l05 and this class: mark every script (four-criteria B1 Preliminary writing rubric: content, communicative achievement, organisation, language). List each student's errors, tag each with a category and whether the grammar involved has already been taught this year (check UNIT4_PLAN.md/the group's unit map before flagging anything as "not yet taught" — relative clauses and time-expression clauses were both taught this unit, so most Unit 4 grammar errors ARE already taught material). Choose 4–6 categories that 2+ students actually made, with one real, anonymised example each ("a student wrote…", never a name). Fill Answers_VISTA.html's placeholders with those real examples, rebuild the Arcade file from the same errors, and build one Fix-It Sheet PDF per student (3–6 items each, their own errors only) — save all of it in materials/<that class's date folder>/.

Likely category candidates for this unit's writing (the 100-word story, pp.50-51) — confirm against the real scripts rather than assuming: relative clause errors (missing commas around a non-defining clause, or wrongly using "that" after a comma); sequencing-word repetition (overusing "then" instead of the wider bank: suddenly, at first, but then, one day); paragraph/story structure (no clear beginning/middle/end, or an ending that doesn't resolve or intrigue); word count (well under or over the 100-word target); spelling of clothes/materials vocabulary if the story used it (jacket, leather, silk); missing the given first sentence exactly as printed.

6. Unit 4 Test · 45 min

Materials: Unit 4 Test.html/.pdf (student copy), Unit 4 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf (key, do not photocopy), Unit 4 Test Listening.mp3.

Adapted from the official New Close-up B1 End-of-Unit Test 4 (Cengage/National Geographic Learning, permission granted to photocopy for classroom use). Reading (Exam Task, matching sentences to gaps, the Swenkas of South Africa), Vocabulary (odd-one-out + word-bank fill), Grammar (relative pronouns + time-expression clauses), Listening (six short conversations). The writing section (20 marks in the official test) is not included — Unit 4's writing (SB pp.50-51, the 100-word story) was already written in class in l05 and marked separately, so retesting it here would repeat that assessment. Vocabulary Ex.3 and Grammar's defining/non-defining exercise are also trimmed to fit 45 minutes. Two grammar sentences are localised to a real Valencia setting (La Malvarrosa; the Turia Gardens); the tested grammar and the correct answers are unchanged from the original.

Total: 44 marks, 45 minutes. Reading /5 · Vocabulary /17 · Grammar /16 · Listening /6. Full key in Unit 4 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf.
