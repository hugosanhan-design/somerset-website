Listening Ex.1 · feelings from context

How do they feel?

  • 1"The clothes were actually very boring and old-fashioned." → disappointed
  • 2"Henrik made this jacket from some old curtains. I think it looks amazing!" → impressed
  • 3"I don't understand the washing instructions on this label." → confused
  • 4"I'm really worried about my job interview tomorrow." → anxious
  • 5"My brother borrowed my watch and now he's lost it." → annoyed
Each sentence gives a clue word or situation (boring/old-fashioned, amazing, don't understand, worried, lost it) that points to one feeling.
Listening Ex.2 · Track 4.2

Choose the right situation

  • Speaker 1b, feels sad
  • Speaker 2b, has argued with someone
  • Speaker 3a, respects someone
  • Speaker 4a, feels relieved
Speaker 3 says "I think it's amazing how she understands the people she works with" — that's respect, not pride, since it's about the boss, not the speaker.
Listening Ex.3 · Track 4.3, same meaning

Tick the paraphrase

  • 1"I like the trouser suit, but I think the silk dress is nicer." → a, I prefer the dress to the trouser suit
  • 2"I wanted to buy leather gloves, but they didn't have my size." → b, he didn't buy the gloves
  • 3"Although this shop isn't cheap, the clothes are very good quality." → a, the clothes are expensive
  • 4"We can't go out until we've finished our homework." → a, they haven't finished their homework
  • 5"I used to wear glasses when I was younger, but now I don't need to." → b, she doesn't have to wear glasses
This is the core exam skill: the correct option is always a different way of saying the same thing, never the same words repeated.
Exam Task · multiple choice, six conversations

Choose A, B or C

  • 1Online clothes shop → C, the design of the website is confusing
  • 2School project mark → B, relieved that she finished it
  • 3Birthday present → C, a silk scarf
  • 4New coat → C, it was warm
  • 5Boy's brother → B, is lazy
  • 6A film → B, wasn't very funny
Strategy: you will hear every option mentioned, but only one is actually true — the other two are red herrings, exactly as the Exam Tip warns.

Speaking Exam Task · solving a problem

Useful Language

Explaining choices and giving reasons:

My first choice would be... because...
I think... would be (more useful / better / more interesting) because...
Although... is an interesting suggestion, I don't think it would work so well because...
There are several reasons why I think that... would be the best idea.
I haven't chosen... because from what you said about... I think...
... seems to me to be a better choice than... because...

This is a speaking task with no fixed answer — project this Useful Language box while pairs work through the exam task (choosing where to take an exchange student shopping).
