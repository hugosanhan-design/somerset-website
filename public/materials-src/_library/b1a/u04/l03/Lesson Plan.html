Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can identify feelings from context, listen for synonyms rather than exact words, complete a multiple-choice-with-six-conversations exam task, and give reasons for a choice in a problem-solving speaking task.
Target language. Feeling adjectives (annoyed, anxious, confused, disappointed, impressed); listening-for-synonyms strategy; Useful Language for giving reasons and comparing two options.
Exam link. B1 Preliminary Listening Part 1 (multiple choice, six short conversations) and Speaking Part 3 (collaborative task, discussing a picture-based problem) — both exam tasks on this spread carry the exact exam label.
Materials: Student's Book pp.46-47. Audio: Track 4.2 (four speakers), 4.3 (five sentences), 4.4 (exam task), 4.5 (model example task), 4.6 (model answer), 4.7 (exam task). Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b1a-u04-l03. If Unit 4's Play 1 didn't happen at the end of l02, open it here after speaking.

2–3. Listening: feelings & synonyms · 25 min

Materials: Track 4.2, 4.3. Student's Book p.46.

Ex.1: match each quoted complaint or comment to the feeling it expresses. Ex.2 (Track 4.2): four speakers, each describing a different situation (a boring fashion show; a home-made jacket; confusing washing instructions; a job interview; a lost watch). Ex.3 (Track 4.3): five short spoken sentences, tick which written option means the same — this is the core Listening Part 1 skill, that the correct answer is a paraphrase, never the same words.

Ex.1: 1 disappointed · 2 impressed · 3 confused · 4 anxious · 5 annoyed.
Ex.2: Speaker 1 → b, feels sad · Speaker 2 → b, has argued with someone · Speaker 3 → a, respects someone · Speaker 4 → a, feels relieved.
Ex.3: 1a · 2b · 3a · 4a · 5b.

4. Exam Task: multiple choice with six conversations · 15 min

Materials: Track 4.4. Student's Book p.46.

Underline key words in the six questions and their A-C options before listening. Play Track 4.4 once (per the exam format, only one listen): an online clothes shop, a school project mark, a birthday present, a new coat, a younger brother, and a film review.

Answers: 1C (the design of the website is confusing) · 2B (relieved that she finished it) · 3C (a silk scarf) · 4C (it was warm) · 5B (is lazy) · 6B (wasn't very funny).

Valencia link: ask students to describe, using one feeling adjective from Ex.1, how they felt the last time they bought clothes at the Mercado de Colón, a Ruzafa vintage shop, or online — was it "impressed", "disappointed", or "confused" by the sizing?

5–7. Speaking: giving reasons, solving a problem · 35 min

Materials: Student's Book p.47, Track 4.5, 4.6, 4.7.

Ex.2 (Track 4.5): a student has to choose a present for her grandmother, between a silk scarf (department store) and a necklace (independent jewellery shop). Ex.4 (Track 4.6): the model answer recommends the scarf, explaining why (grandmother loves scarves, store is close, easy to return) and why not the necklace (not enough time to travel there, might not suit her taste). Ex.5 (Track 4.7, the real Exam Task): two friends choosing where to take an exchange student shopping — a small town with independent shops, or a big shopping centre.

Ex.2: 1 grandmother · 2 department store · 3 independent shop · 4 necklace · 5 scarf.
Ex.4: 1 students' own answers · 2 yes, he does give reasons for his answer.

No key for Ex.5 (the Exam Task) — this is a speaking task, not an exercise with a right answer. The Vista deck instead projects the Useful Language box (My first choice would be... because / Although... is an interesting suggestion, I don't think it would work so well because...) so students can refer to it while they talk.

Valencia link: adapt the Exam Task's situation — would students recommend an exchange student go clothes shopping in Ruzafa's small independent shops, or a big centre like Bonaire or Aqua? Use the same Useful Language frames to justify the choice.

8. Somerset Arcade + carry-over play · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u04-l03.

Point Grab built from today's feeling adjectives, listening-for-synonyms practice and the speaking Useful Language frames. If Unit 4's Play 1 (Unit4_TheFashion_ClothesAndRelativeClauses_Play.html) hasn't run yet, open it now, after speaking, per the group's standing "carries forward, never lost" rule.

Fast finisher

Fast-finishing pairs think of a second real Valencia shopping-trip problem (e.g. two clothes-shopping streets to choose between) and practise the Useful Language frames on each other.

If short of time, cut:

The role-swap in block 7 (one round is enough if time is tight). Do not cut the Exam Task listening (block 4): multiple choice with six conversations is a full exam-task type and needs the full listen plus checking time.
