Vocabulary Ex.1 · shopping words

Match the definition

  • 1credit card → c, a small piece of plastic that you use to pay for things
  • 2customer → d, a person who buys something
  • 3label → b, a piece of paper or material that gives information about an object
  • 4receipt → f, a piece of paper to show that you have paid money for something
  • 5cash → a, money in notes and coins

Vocabulary Ex.5-6 · Track 4.8, shop conversation

Choose, then check by listening

  • 1I'm looking for a shirt to go with these trousers.
  • 2It's a bargain, only €15.
  • 3I'd like to try on this jacket as well.
  • 4Can I pay for it with my credit card?
  • 5Can I pay with my credit card?
  • 6I'm sorry, we only take cash here.
  • 7Don't forget your receipt, sir!
The jacket doesn't fit ("too big"), but the shirt does — the customer only pays for the shirt, and the shop turns out to be cash-only.
Grammar Ex.1-2 · concept check

Present simple, or present perfect?

  • Rule 1Future time clauses use the present simple (not will).
  • Rule 2To emphasise one action finishes before another begins, we use the present perfect simple.
  • Ex.1 · 1Michael will call as soon as he has any news.
  • 2When we go to Italy, we'll visit our cousins.
  • 3I won't buy new shoes until these ones have worn out.
  • 4Check you have your credit card before you leave.
  • 5Please call me the moment the manager arrives.
LOOK!: "You can go out and play when you've tidied your room" emphasises the tidying finishes first — that's why it's present perfect, not present simple.
Grammar Ex.3-4 · practice

Before, until or as soon as?

  • Ex.3 · 1I'll return this jacket as soon as I've found the receipt.
  • 2When I finish university, I'll travel the world.
  • 3The moment Jenny arrives, we'll have dinner.
  • 4I'll text you as soon as the postman delivers the parcel.
  • 5I won't go home until I've found a present.
  • 6Harry will buy a jumper after he has saved enough money.
  • Ex.4 · 1He'll shop around before he decides which computer to buy.
  • 2As soon as I get home, I'll walk the dog.
  • 3Ameira will go shopping as soon as she gets her pocket money.
  • 4You can watch TV until it's time for lunch.
  • 5I'll turn off the lights before I leave the house.
  • 6Until you get another bike, I'll drive you to school.
Each of before/until/as soon as is used exactly twice in Ex.4 — a good check that you haven't repeated one and skipped another.
Exam Task · open cloze

An 18th birthday celebration

  • 1As soon as he gets home, we'll give him his presents.
  • 2It will go with his favourite trousers.
  • 3His favourite trousers, which are from a second-hand store in Paris.
  • 4He hasn't tried it on yet.
  • 5We will leave home after we've had a video call.
  • 6They will want to say happy birthday before they go to bed.
Strategy: gap 3 needs a relative pronoun (revision from p.45); gaps 1, 4 and 6 are time expressions or phrasal-verb prepositions from today's other exercises.
