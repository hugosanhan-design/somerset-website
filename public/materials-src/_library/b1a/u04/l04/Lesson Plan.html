New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 4 · That's the fashion · SB p.48-49
PET I: Wednesday, 15:45–17:15
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use shopping vocabulary and phrasal verbs (1) accurately, and can build sentences with time-expression clauses using the correct verb form (present simple / present perfect simple), then complete an open cloze exam task.
Target language. Shopping nouns (bargain, cash, credit card, customer, label, receipt); phrasal verbs (find out, go with, look at, pay for, pick up, shop around, try on, wear out); time expressions (as soon as, before, until, when, the moment) with present simple/present perfect simple, never will.
Exam link. Open cloze (one word per gap, no options) is a core B1 Preliminary Reading and Use of English task, on p.49 and again in the Unit 4 test.
Materials: Student's Book pp.48-49. Audio: Track 4.8 (shop conversation). Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck, all exercises from both pages). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u04-l04. Review play (if time): plays/Unit4_TheFashion_ShoppingAndTimeClauses_Play.html.

2–4. Vocabulary: shopping & phrasal verbs (1) · 35 min

Materials: Student's Book p.48, Ex.1–6. Track 4.8.

Ex.1–2: match and use bargain, cash, credit card, customer, label, receipt. Ex.3–4: a short text about online vs in-person shopping habits, gapped with eight phrasal verbs (find out, go with, look at, pay for, pick up, shop around, try on, wear out). Ex.5–6 (Track 4.8): a shop conversation about buying a shirt, choosing between a credit card and cash.

Ex.1: 1 credit card → c · 2 customer → d · 3 label → b · 4 receipt → f · 5 cash → a · 6 bargain → e.
Ex.2: 1 bargain · 2 credit card · 3 receipt · 4 cash · 5 customer · 6 label.
Ex.3: 1 find out · 2 go with · 3 look at · 4 pay for · 5 pick up · 6 shop around · 7 try on · 8 wear out.
Ex.4: 1 around · 2 out · 3 on · 4 out.
Ex.5–6: 1 go with · 2 bargain · 3 try on · 4 pay for · 5 credit card · 6 cash · 7 receipt.

Valencia link: ask whether market stalls at the Mercado Central or the Ruzafa Sunday flea market usually take cash only or also card, and whether students always ask for a receipt (ticket/factura) when shopping there.

5–7. Grammar: clauses with time expressions · 20 min

Materials: Student's Book p.49, Ex.1–5.

Ex.1–2: after as soon as/when/until/before/the moment, future time clauses use the present simple, or the present perfect simple to emphasise that one action finishes before the other starts — never will. Ex.4: before/until/as soon as, each used twice across six sentences.

Ex.1 (verbs underlined): 1 has · 2 go · 3 have worn out · 4 leave · 5 arrives.
Ex.2 (rule): 1 present simple · 2 present perfect simple.
Ex.3: 1 as soon as · 2 finish · 3 The moment · 4 delivers · 5 until · 6 has saved.
Ex.4: 1 before · 2 As soon as · 3 as soon as · 4 until · 5 before · 6 Until.
Ex.5: students' own answers.

Valencia link: board example — "I'll go to the Falles bonfires as soon as it gets dark" or "We won't leave the beach until the sun sets over the Malvarrosa." Each student writes one true sentence about Valencia using a time expression.

8. Exam Task: open cloze · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.49, Ex.6.

"An 18th birthday celebration" — read the Exam Tip first: understand the whole meaning before filling gaps, and remember gapped words are often part of a fixed phrase, a time word, a relative pronoun or an auxiliary verb.

Answers: 1 as · 2 with · 3 which · 4 on · 5 will · 6 before.

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u04-l04. Play: groups/2026-27/pet1/plays/Unit4_TheFashion_ShoppingAndTimeClauses_Play.html.
