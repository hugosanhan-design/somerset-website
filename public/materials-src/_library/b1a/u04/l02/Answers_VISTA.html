Grammar Ex.1 · concept check

Defining or non-defining?

  • 1My grandmother, who lives in Italy, wears glasses → a, non-defining (extra information, commas)
  • 2The woman who lives next door wears glasses → b, defining (identifies who she is)
Test: can you remove the clause and still understand who the sentence is about? If yes, it's non-defining (commas); if no, it's defining (no commas).
Grammar Ex.2-3 · relative pronoun rules

Choose the right pronoun

  • Rule 1who to refer to people
  • Rule 2which, that to refer to things
  • Rule 3whose to show possession
  • Rule 4when to refer to a period of time
  • Rule 5where to refer to a place
  • Ex.3 · 1That's the woman whose son is an artist.
  • 2Have you seen the button which I left on the table?
  • 3The shop where we saw those silk ties.
  • 4Summer is the time when everybody relaxes by the sea.
  • 5The teacher is talking to the boy that lost his watch.
  • 6Where is the girl whose glasses are broken?
REMEMBER: "that" can replace who/which in a defining clause, but never in a non-defining clause (never after a comma).
Grammar Ex.4 · build a non-defining clause

Join the two sentences

  • 1John's jacket, which is red, has got silver buttons.
  • 2This tie, which is silk, cost $40.
  • 3Yesterday I met my friend Tessa, who makes her own clothes.
  • 4Last Saturday we saw our teacher, who was buying a suit at the department store.
  • 5I've bought a new raincoat, which is black with yellow spots.
Every answer needs a comma before AND after the clause (unless the clause ends the sentence, then just one comma before it).
Grammar Ex.5 · write full sentences

Who, which or where?

  • 1A mobile phone is something which we use to call friends.
  • 2A cinema is a place where we watch films.
  • 3A zoo is a place where we see animals.
  • 4A teacher is a person who teaches people.
  • 5A backpack is a bag which we carry on our backs.
  • 6A raincoat is a coat which we wear when it rains.
"Place" always pairs with "where"; a thing pairs with "which"; a person pairs with "who".
Grammar Ex.6 · open text, fast fashion

Complete with which, where, who

  • 1Fast fashion, which means producing and buying lots of cheap clothes...
  • 2Kate Fletcher, who is a professor of fashion in the UK...
  • 3Look at the clothes which you already have.
  • 4Perhaps one of your friends who wants something new to wear...
  • 5Try to find shops where you can buy second-hand clothes.
  • 6Second-hand clothes are clothes which are not new.
  • 7Choose natural materials, like cotton or wool, which are better for the environment.
Note: gaps 1 and 2 are non-defining (Kate Fletcher and fast fashion are already named); the rest are defining.
