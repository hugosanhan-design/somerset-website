Writing Ex.1 · sequencing words

Complete Will's story

  • 1One day, Will was doing his homework, when he heard a strange noise.
  • 2Suddenly, the boy looked up at him and dropped a piece of paper onto the path.
  • 3Then he disappeared.
  • 4At first, he was confused.
  • 5But then he saw a cross marked on the map.
Other sequencing words that work the same way: once, in the beginning, one morning/afternoon/evening/night, all of a sudden, after that, next.
Writing Ex.2 · example task

Read the task carefully

  • 1Has the teacher given the title of the story? → No
  • 2How long should the story be? → about 100 words
You only get a first sentence, never a title — the rest of the story, including the ending, is entirely your own idea.
Writing Ex.3 · order the paragraphs

Emma and the bird

  • Paragraph 1b — she got up and opened the curtains, noticed a blue earring
  • Paragraph 2d — a strange bird flew in holding a pink silk scarf
  • Paragraph 3a — the bird dropped the scarf and made a loud noise
  • Paragraph 4c — Emma read a label: "take the earring and the scarf and follow the bird"
The story ends with a clear mystery, not a resolved ending — a valid, exam-style way to finish a 100-word story.
Writing Ex.4 · true or false

Check the details

  • 1Emma saw the blue earring first, then the pink scarf → True
  • 2She was very calm when the bird made a noise → False
  • 3She knew the owner of the blue earring → False
  • 4She didn't know the bird → True
Emma was "confused and a little scared" when the bird made a noise, not calm.
Writing Ex.5 · descriptive adjectives

Complete with the Useful Language

  • 1Although the exam was the next day, Harry felt very relaxed and calm.
  • 2It was a beautiful, warm afternoon and the sun was shining.
  • 3She walked along the busy / noisy street.
  • 4I was very excited about the concert.
  • 5Her grandfather lived in the same small, old / dirty / clean house for seventy years.
There's more than one possible correct adjective for items 3 and 5 — any adjective that fits the sense and comes from the Useful Language box is accepted.

Video Ex.3 · definitions

Match the word

  • 1make something dirty or dangerous → contaminate
  • 2press hard with a cloth or brush and move it backwards and forwards → scrub
  • 3lose or remove something → shed
  • 4without equipment to help you see → with the naked eye
  • 5move on the surface of the water → float
  • 6tiny animals that live in water → plankton
  • 7the blood moving around your body → bloodstream
Every one of these words appears in the video's own narration, in this order.

Complete the summary

  • 1When we wash fleece jackets and other synthetic clothes...

  • 3Acting like sponges, these microplastics pick up different chemicals.
  • 4...pick up different chemicals.
