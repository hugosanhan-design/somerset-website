New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 1 · Perfect for the job · SB p.12-13
PET I: Wed, 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use employment vocabulary and life-event phrasal verbs, and can choose correctly between countable and uncountable nouns with the right quantifiers.
Target language. Employment vocabulary (unemployed, qualifications, retired); life-event phrasal verbs (get up, apply for, retire); countable/uncountable nouns; quantifiers (a few, much, a little, a lot of).
Exam link. PET Reading/Writing Part 1-style vocabulary; grammar accuracy (quantifiers are a common PET error area).
Materials: Student's Book pp.12-13. Answers_VISTA.html (exercise key). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u01-l04. Play 2 (Unit1_PerfectForTheJob_Play.html) at the end if there is time.

2. Vocabulary p.12 · 20 min

Materials: Student's Book p.12, Ex.1-4.
Ex.1 answers: 1 e · 2 b · 3 a · 4 c · 5 f · 6 d.
Ex.2 answers: 1 get · 2 up · 3 to · 4 out · 5 up · 6 out · 7 up · 8 for.
Ex.3 answers: 1 up · 2 up · 3 to · 4 for · 5 got.
Ex.4 answers: 1 c · 2 d · 3 b · 4 f · 5 e · 6 a.

3. Grammar p.13: countable/uncountable · 20 min

Materials: Student's Book p.13, Ex.1-4.
Ex.1 answers: 1 C · 2 C · 3 U · 4 U · 5 C.
Ex.4 answers (same noun, two meanings): 1a C (a piece of chocolate) / 1b U (chocolate as a food) · 2a U (an amount of time) / 2b C (a time as a single occasion) · 3a C (two cups of coffee) / 3b U (coffee as a drink) · 4a U (the sport) / 4b C (the ball used in the sport).

4. Grammar p.13: quantifiers · 13 min

Materials: Student's Book p.13, Ex.5-7.
Ex.5: countable — qualification, candidate, people, family. Uncountable — advice, food, information, love, maths, traffic.
Ex.6: countable only — a number of, a few, many. Uncountable only — a little, much. Both — a lot of, a kilo of, any, lots of, some.
Ex.7: 1 some, a little · 2 some · 3 many, lots of · 4 a, an · 5 a kilo of, some · 6 some · 7 some · 8 some, a.

Valencia / Spain link

For quantifiers practice, build one extra sentence together about a real Valencia example: 'There isn't much traffic in Ruzafa on a Sunday morning, but there are a lot of people at the market.'

Fast finisher

Write two more sentences about your own family using a life-event phrasal verb from Ex.1-3 (e.g. 'My grandfather retired last year').
