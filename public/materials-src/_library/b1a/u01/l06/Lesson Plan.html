1-4. Writing error-review · 32 min

Teacher prep, done between l05 and this class: mark every script (four-criteria B1 Preliminary writing rubric: content, communicative achievement, organisation, language). List each student's errors, tag each with a category. Choose 4-6 categories that 2+ students actually made, with one real, anonymised example each. Fill Answers_VISTA.html's placeholders with those real examples, rebuild the Arcade file from the same errors, and build one Fix-It Sheet PDF per student (3-6 items each, their own errors only).

Likely category candidates for this unit's writing (the p.14-15 informal email reply) — confirm against the real scripts rather than assuming: register slips (a formal phrase like "With kind regards" in an informal email); forgetting to ask a question back (the exact gap flagged in the SB's own model-answer exercise); missing contractions (writing "I am" instead of "I'm" throughout); word count (well under or over ~100 words); missing a bullet point from the task.

6. Unit 1 Test · 45 min

Materials: Unit 1 Test.html/.pdf (student copy), Unit 1 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf (key, do not photocopy), Unit 1 Test Listening.mp3.

Adapted from the official New Close-up B1 End-of-Unit Assessment 1, trimmed to 38 marks and 45 minutes (Reading 5, Vocabulary 10, Grammar 15, Listening 8). The writing section is not included — Unit 1's writing (SB pp.14-15) was already written in class in l05 and is reviewed, not retested, here.

Total: 38 marks, 45 minutes. Reading /5 · Vocabulary /10 · Grammar /15 · Listening /8. Full key in Unit 1 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf.

Valencia / Spain link

Keep the error-review's practice sentences grounded in Valencia where possible (e.g. 'I'm meeting my cousins at the Turia Gardens on Saturday, do you want to come?') so the corrective examples double as real spoken content, not just abstract grammar.

Fast finisher

Students who finish the test early re-read their Fix-It Sheet items once more, silently, while waiting for the rest of the group.

If short of time, cut:

Trim the Arcade error-review round (Block 3) first if time is short — move it to the top of the Unit 2 opening class. Never trim the test itself: it is graded and feeds directly into the Unit 2 results deck.
