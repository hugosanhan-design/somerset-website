Listening Ex.3 · match answers to questions, Track 5.3

Living on a houseboat

  • 1What is the biggest problem about living on a boat? → d, probably the maintenance — we have to repair things all the time
  • 2When did you first move onto a boat? → a, around September time, three years ago
  • 3Do you miss living in a house on dry land? → e, sometimes — I'd love a bit more space
  • 4Do you think you'll always live on a boat? → b, it's difficult to say — it depends on my job
  • 5Would you recommend living on a boat? → c, yes — it's not always perfect, but most of the time I love it
Listening Ex.5 · which questions ask about opinion?

Fact or opinion?

  • AnswerQuestions 1, 4 and 6 ask about the speaker's opinion.
Strategy (Exam Tip): words like think, believe, feel, prefer and according to signal an opinion question. Check whether the opinion is current or past, and whose opinion it is (Rebecca's, or "many people's").
Exam Task · multiple choice, one conversation, Track 5.5

Rebecca and the Tiny House Movement

  • 1Rebecca believes that → B, many people think possessions make us happy
  • 2Where does Rebecca sleep? → B, above the kitchen (in a sleeping loft)
  • 3Rebecca says that it's difficult for her → C, to have enough space for her possessions
  • 4The best thing about living in a tiny house, according to Rebecca → B, she has more free time than most people
  • 5At the moment, Rebecca → B, doesn't spend a lot of money on electricity
  • 6What does Rebecca think about meeting her friends? → A, she likes going to different places with them
Why: Rebecca says her house doesn't have a second floor, but "there's a platform on top of my kitchen cupboards, and that's where my bed is" (Q2, B); on electricity, "next year I'm going to put in solar panels... so that I can go off-grid", meaning she's not fully off-grid yet but already pays very little (Q5, B).
