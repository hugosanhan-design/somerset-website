Vocabulary Ex.1 · housing, match to definitions

Match the words

  • 1accommodation → f, a place for someone to stay, live or work in
  • 2apartment block → b, a large building with flats on lots of different floors
  • 3cottage → h, a small house in the countryside
  • 4flat → c, a set of rooms to live in, usually on one floor
  • 5flatmate → g, a person who shares a flat with other people
  • 6neighbour → a, a person who lives near or next to you
  • 7property → e, a building that you own
  • 8rent → d, money that you pay every week or month to live somewhere
Note: "property" is any building/land you own; "accommodation" is any place you stay or live, including a hotel or a rented flat.

Grammar Ex.1 · will, match to uses

Why do we use will?

  • 1The architect probably won't finish the building → g, uncertain future
  • 2If you don't put the dishes away, you won't get dessert → d, threat
  • 3He will become rich one day → f, prediction
  • 4I'll have a cup of tea → h, sudden decision
  • 5You can rest, I'll do the washing up → b, offer
  • 6Her brother will be 18 tomorrow → e, future fact
  • 7Will you please open the window? → a, ask someone to do something
  • 8I'll get you a present in Cairo → c, promise
Grammar Ex.2 · write sentences with will

Form the future simple

  • 1I / think / she / start / her own company one day → I think she will start her own company one day.
  • 2we / live / underground / one day? → Will we live underground one day?
  • 3you / buy / me a coffee later? → Will you buy me a coffee later?
  • 4I / help / you with your homework → I will help you with your homework.
  • 5If you don't stop now / I / call / the police → If you don't stop now, I will call the police.
  • 6we / not / read / books in the future → We won't read books in the future.

Plans or predictions?

  • 1They're going to build an apartment block here next year → a, future plans
  • 2That wall isn't very safe. It's going to fall down → b, predictions based on present evidence

Grammar Ex.5 · complete the conversations

Will or going to

  • 1What colour ___ (you / get)? → are you going to get
  • 2Oh, ___ (you / close) the windows, please? → will you close
  • 3I promise I ___ (not / do) that again → won't do

Grammar Ex.6 · complete Charmaine's email

Moving to Singapore

  • 1I ___ (leave) home in September → 'm going to leave
  • 2I'm not sure yet where I ___ (live) → 'll live
  • 3perhaps I ___ (share) a flat → 'll share
  • 4I ___ (study) architecture → 'm going to study
  • 5I love imagining how our homes ___ (look) → will look
  • 6___ (we / live) in big apartment blocks → Will we live
  • 7or ___ (we / create) our own off-grid communities? → will we create
  • 8What ___ (you / do) when you leave college? → are you going to do
  • 9I hope you ___ (come) and visit me → 'll come
Note: Charmaine's already decided to leave and study architecture (fixed intentions, "be going to"), but many details (where she'll live, whether flats will be shared, how homes will look) are uncertain predictions, so "will".
