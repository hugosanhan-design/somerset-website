Lesson Plan · b1a-u05-l02
New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 5 · Where we live · SB p.56-57
PET I · 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can name types of housing, household objects and the people connected with a home, and form the future with will and be going to accurately, choosing between them.
Target language. Housing (accommodation, apartment block, cottage, flat, flatmate, neighbour, property, rent); household objects (1) (microwave, oven, shelf, ladder, balcony, bucket, shower, freezer, dishwasher, iron, garage, kettle, ceiling, bin, brush, kitchen, fridge, washing machine); will for decisions, promises, threats, future facts, offers/requests, uncertain predictions; be going to for plans/intentions and predictions based on present evidence.
Exam link. will vs be going to reappears in the Unit 5 test's grammar section and in the writing task on pp.62-63.
Materials: Student's Book pp.56-57. Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck, all exercises from both pages). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u05-l02. Review play (if time): plays/Unit5_WhereWeLive_HousingAndWillGoingTo_Play.html.

2–3. Vocabulary: housing & household objects (1) · 30 min

Materials: Student's Book p.56, Ex.1–4. Audio Track 5.2.

Ex.2 conversation: Erik has moved into a new flat in a big apartment block near the railway station; his flatmates are friendly; the rent is 180 euros per week; he's saving to buy his own property; Rania dreams of a cottage in the countryside.

Ex.1: 1 accommodation → f, a place for someone to stay, live or work in · 2 apartment block → b, a large building with flats on lots of different floors · 3 cottage → h, a small house in the countryside · 4 flat → c, a set of rooms to live in, usually on one floor · 5 flatmate → g, a person who shares a flat with other people · 6 neighbour → a, a person who lives near or next to you · 7 property → e, a building that you own · 8 rent → d, money that you pay every week or month to live somewhere.
Ex.2: 1 flat · 2 flatmates · 3 apartment block · 4 rent · 5 property · 6 cottage.
Ex.3 (odd one out): 1 shelf (furniture; microwave and oven are for cooking) · 2 balcony (part of a house; ladder and bucket are for cleaning) · 3 shower (in a bathroom; freezer and dishwasher are in a kitchen) · 4 garage (part of a house; iron and kettle are electrical goods) · 5 ceiling (part of a room; bin and brush are for cleaning) · 6 kitchen (a room; fridge and washing machine are appliances).
Ex.4: 1a bin · 2b freezer · 3c dishwasher · 4b balcony · 5c living room · 6c oven · 7c shower.

Valencia link: ask students to describe a typical Valencia balcony (many flats in the Ensanche and Ruzafa have narrow wrought-iron balconies over the street) and whether they'd rather have a balcony or a garden.

4–7. Grammar: will; be going to · 45 min

Materials: Student's Book p.57, Ex.1–6.

Ex.1 matches eight will sentences to their uses: offers, threats, promises, future facts, requests, uncertain predictions, sudden decisions. Ex.3 contrasts be going to for a future plan (they're going to build an apartment block here) with a prediction based on present evidence (that wall is going to fall down). Ex.6 is Charmaine's email to Anna about moving to Singapore.

Ex.1: 1g · 2d · 3f · 4h · 5b · 6e · 7a · 8c.
Ex.2: 1 I think she will start her own company one day. · 2 Will we live underground one day? · 3 Will you buy me a coffee later? · 4 I will help you with your homework. · 5 If you don't stop now, I will call the police. · 6 We won't read books in the future.
Ex.3: 1a (future plans) · 2b (predictions based on present evidence).
Ex.4: 1 won't · 2 I'll · 3 is going to · 4 will · 5 Will you · 6 I'll.
Ex.5: 1 are you going to get · 2 will you close · 3 won't do · 4 going to fall · 5 will be.
Ex.6: 1 'm going to leave · 2 'll live · 3 'll share · 4 'm going to study · 5 will look · 6 Will we live · 7 will we create · 8 are you going to do · 9 'll come.

Valencia link: each student writes one sentence about a real future plan connected to Valencia using be going to (e.g. "I'm going to visit the Ciutat de les Arts i les Ciències this weekend") and one prediction using will.

8. Somerset Arcade + Play · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u05-l02. Play: groups/2026-27/pet1/plays/Unit5_WhereWeLive_HousingAndWillGoingTo_Play.html.

Point Grab covers today's housing/household-objects vocabulary and will/be going to. If there's time, open the play (three scripts, each set in a real Valencia housing scenario); pick a 2- or 3-character version. If there's no time, it carries to l03, after speaking.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write three predictions about their home town in ten years, using will for at least one uncertain prediction and be going to for at least one based on present evidence.

If short of time, cut:

The play (block 8) — it always was optional and carries forward. Do not cut Grammar Ex.6 (p.57): Charmaine's email is the closest model to the Unit 5 test's own grammar section and the writing task on pp.62-63.
