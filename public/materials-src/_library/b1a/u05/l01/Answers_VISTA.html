Reading Ex.3 · true or false

Living off grid

  • 1Most people are very worried about getting access to electricity, gas and water. → False
  • 2Off-grid communities try not to damage the environment. → True
  • 3Everyone at Finca Bellavista lives there full-time. → False
  • 4In the future, more people will get their electricity off grid. → True
Why: the text says most of us "don't really think about" where services come from (1, false); off-grid communities "work together so that they can live in a way that doesn't harm the environment" (2, true); Finca Bellavista has "some permanent residents", but you can also "visit as a tourist and stay... for a week or two" (3, false); the International Energy Agency believes "by 2030 more than 70% of new electricity connections will be off grid" (4, true).
Reading Ex.4 · where would you find each text?

Identify the text type

  • 1To rent (flat advert) → in a newspaper, shop window or online
  • 2Tim/Helen message → on a phone
  • 3Eric's Appliances advert → in a newspaper or magazine or online
  • 4Judy's email → on a computer / phone
  • 5House & Home Exhibition notice → on a website or in a magazine
Strategy (Exam Tip): decide the text type first, then underline key words in the text and in the answer options, and look for the option closest to the text's main message.
Exam Task · multiple choice with five short texts

Choose the correct answer

  • 1The advert says that → C, you can't have animals in the flat
  • 2Tim and Helen's messages → B, Helen isn't sure if Tim will like the sofa
  • 3Eric's Appliances advert → B, you can get more information from the website
  • 4Why has Judy written the email? → B, to check if Dina is going to Dublin
  • 5House & Home Exhibition, week of 13th-19th Sept → C, the exhibition area only will be closed
Why: text 1 says "no lift, no pets" — the flat doesn't allow animals (C), the lift line is a distractor since there simply isn't one. Text 4: Judy needs to book rooms tomorrow and asks "are you still interested in going to Dublin with us?" — she's checking Dina still wants to come (B), not asking Dina to book anything herself.
