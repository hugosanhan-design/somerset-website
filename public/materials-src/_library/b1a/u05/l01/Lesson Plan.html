1. Unit 4 results deck · 20 min

Materials: the Unit 4 results PowerPoint (cloned .pptx, _method/level-ladder-results-scripts/), projector.

The Unit 4 test is the source data. Present the class score by section first, name what's solid and what's "not yet" per section, then say when each weak point gets a second pass this year. Individual cards stay anonymised on the shared screen; open a student's own card quietly on a laptop for a one-to-one moment, never project a name.

2–6. Reading: living closer to the sky · 60 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.53-55, Ex.1–6. Audio Track 5.1.

Full running order in the table above. The article describes people choosing to live "off grid" — not connected to mains electricity, water and gas — including the Finca Bellavista treehouse community in the jungle in Costa Rica, who collect rainwater, grow their own food and use solar power, connected by zip-lines and wooden bridges. Word Focus: harm (hurt), zip-line (a cable or rope stretched between two points that people can slide along), permanent (always there), vision (idea of how things might be).

Ex.3 (T/F): 1 F (the article says most of us don't really think about where services come from, not that people are "very worried") · 2 T (off-grid communities work together so they don't harm the environment) · 3 F (there are some permanent residents, but you can also visit as a tourist for a week or two) · 4 T (the International Energy Agency believes more than 70% of new electricity connections will be off grid by 2030).
Ex.4 (text type — where you'd find each one): 1 in a newspaper or shop window or online · 2 on a phone · 3 in a newspaper or magazine or online · 4 on a computer / phone · 5 on a website or in a magazine.
Ex.5 (key words, text 1): text: flat next to bus stop and close to shops, no lift, no pets · options: A lift, doesn't work; B bus stop, next to shops; C can't have animals → the correct answer is B.
Ex.6 (Exam Task): 1 C (you can't have animals in the flat) · 2 B (Helen isn't sure if Tim will like the sofa) · 3 B (you can get more information from the website) · 4 B (to check if Dina is going to Dublin) · 5 C (the exhibition area only will be closed).

Valencia link: ask students if they know any off-grid or eco-friendly housing projects near Valencia, e.g. rural fincas in L'Horta or the Sierra Calderona that use solar panels and their own wells for water. Would they want to live somewhere like Finca Bellavista, or somewhere closer to the city like Ruzafa?
