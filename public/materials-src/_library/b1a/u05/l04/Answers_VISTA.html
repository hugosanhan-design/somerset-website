Home expressions Ex.4 · which word does NOT go with the verb?

Cross out the odd word

  • 1make: a rest / a mess / your bed → a rest (you HAVE a rest)
  • 2move: house / away / dinner → dinner (you HAVE dinner)
  • 3do: the housework / a lot of noise / the washing up → a lot of noise (you MAKE a lot of noise)
  • 4take: a seat / a break / a coffee → a coffee (you HAVE a coffee)
  • 5tidy: your room / the oven / up → the oven (you CLEAN the oven)
  • 6have: lunch / a shower / the table → the table (you LAY the table)

Grammar Ex.3 & 4 · future predictions

Will, be going to or might?

  • 1I think the shop ___ deliver the fridge next week → will probably deliver
  • 2John's already painted three walls, so he ___ finish soon → 's going to finish
  • 3We ___ move to a bigger city one day → might move
  • Rule aevidence in the present situation → be going to
  • Rule bpredictions we can't be sure of → might
  • Rule ca future possibility with no evidence → will
