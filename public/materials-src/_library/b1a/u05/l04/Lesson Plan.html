New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 5 · Where we live · SB p.60-61
PET I · 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can name household objects (2) and use fixed home expressions accurately, and can talk about future plans/events and future predictions choosing correctly between present simple, present continuous, be going to, will and might.
Target language. Household objects (2) (blanket, bookcase, chest of drawers, cushion, duvet, pillow, sheet, wardrobe); home expressions (make a mess/your bed, move house, do the housework, take a break, tidy your room, have a shower); present simple for timetabled future events; present continuous for fixed future plans; be going to for intentions; will/might for predictions of different certainty.
Exam link. Choosing the right future form is directly tested in the Unit 5 test's Grammar Ex.2 and reappears in the writing task on pp.62-63.
Materials: Student's Book pp.60-61. Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck, all exercises from both pages). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u05-l04. Review play (if time): plays/Unit5_WhereWeLive_HouseholdAndFuturePlans_Play.html.

2–3. Vocabulary: household objects (2) & home expressions · 30 min

Materials: Student's Book p.60, Ex.1–5.
Ex.1: 1 duvet · 2 wardrobe · 3 blanket · 4 pillow · 5 sheet · 6 cushion · 7 chest of drawers · 8 bookcase.
Ex.2: 1 blanket · 2 chest of drawers · 3 wardrobe · 4 sheet · 5 duvet · 6 cushions · 7 pillow · 8 bookcase.
Ex.4 (cross out the word that does NOT go with the verb): 1 make: a rest (you make a mess/your bed, but have a rest) · 2 move: dinner (you move house/away, but have dinner) · 3 do: a lot of noise (you do the housework/washing up, but make a lot of noise) · 4 take: a coffee (you take a seat/a break, but have a coffee) · 5 tidy: the oven (you tidy your room/tidy up, but clean the oven) · 6 have: the table (you have lunch/a shower, but lay the table).
Ex.5: 1 your room · 2 have · 3 a mess · 4 move · 5 the housework · 6 a break · 7 the washing up · 8 lunch / dinner.

Valencia link: ask students who does the housework at home and how chores are shared — is it different from a Valencian grandparent's generation?

4–7. Grammar: future plans, events & predictions · 45 min

Materials: Student's Book p.61, Ex.1–7.

Ex.1 contrasts present continuous for fixed plans (the cleaner is coming tomorrow at 3pm), be going to for intentions without a fixed arrangement (I'm going to start looking for a new flat), and present simple for a timetabled event (my exams start on Tuesday 3rd June). Ex.4 covers predictions with will (based on personal opinion), be going to (based on present evidence) and might (a possibility with no evidence).

Ex.1: 1b · 2c · 3a.
Ex.2: 1 leaves · 2 We're having · 3 going to buy · 4 I'm staying · 5 is · 6 going to live.
Ex.3 (underline prediction forms): 1 will probably deliver · 2 's going to finish · 3 might move.
Ex.4: a be going to (evidence in the present) · b will (predictions we can't be sure of) · c might (a future possibility with no evidence).
Ex.5: 1f · 2a · 3e · 4c · 5d · 6b.
Ex.6: 1 going to change · 2 are meeting / are going to meet · 3 leaves · 4 is showing / is going to show · 5 start / starts.
Ex.7: students' own answers, using will, be going to or might as appropriate.

Valencia link: ask students to predict something about Valencia in ten years using each of the three forms: something they're sure of (will), something with present evidence (be going to, e.g. building work already happening), and something uncertain (might).

8. Somerset Arcade + Play · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u05-l04. Play: groups/2026-27/pet1/plays/Unit5_WhereWeLive_HouseholdAndFuturePlans_Play.html.

Point Grab covers today's household-objects/home-expressions vocabulary and future plans/predictions. If there's time, open the play (three scripts, each set in a real Valencia moving-house scenario); pick a 2- or 3-character version.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write three sentences about their weekend plans, one each with present continuous (fixed plan), be going to (intention) and will or might (prediction).

If short of time, cut:

The play (block 8) — it always was optional and carries forward. Do not cut Grammar Ex.6 (p.61): the paraphrase practice is the closest model to the Unit 5 test's own grammar sections.
