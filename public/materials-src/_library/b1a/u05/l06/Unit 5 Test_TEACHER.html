2 Write the words next to the correct definitions. / 8

accommodation · apartment block · cottage · flatmate · housewarming · neighbour · property · rent
  1. A building and / or a piece of land → property
  2. A person who shares an apartment with another person → flatmate
  3. The money you pay to live in a building → rent
  4. A small, old-fashioned house, often in the countryside → cottage
  5. A person who lives in a home which is very close to your home → neighbour
  6. A large building which is divided into several homes → apartment block
  7. An event people sometimes organise after they move into a new home → housewarming
  8. Any kind of place where you live or stay → accommodation

Grammar / 24

1 There is one mistake in each sentence. Find and correct the mistake. / 8

  1. I'll carry that bag for you. (not "carrying")
  2. I'm going to do some shopping this weekend. (not "for")
  3. I promise I won't eat your ice cream! (not "want")
  4. Watch out! You're going to hit me with that bag. (not "hitting")
  5. We leave for France on Friday 5th August. (not "leaves")
  6. My neighbour is moving house next month. (not "are"; or "My neighbours are moving")
  7. We aren't going to have a holiday this year. (not "don't going to")
  8. We're going to go to Hawaii next year. (not "go to go")

2 Complete the sentences with the present simple, present continuous or going to form of the verbs. / 8

  1. The train goes (go) in ten minutes.
  2. She is moving / is going to move (move) house soon.
  3. The show starts (start) at 7 p.m.
  4. It is going to rain (rain); just look at the clouds!
  5. Next year we are going to go (go) on holiday, but I don't know where yet!
  6. Tomorrow we are having / are going to have (have) lunch with my grandmother.
  7. I am meeting / am going to meet (meet) my friends at the cinema this afternoon.
  8. The painter is going to finish (finish) the kitchen first.
