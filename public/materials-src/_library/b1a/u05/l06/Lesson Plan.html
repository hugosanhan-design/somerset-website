1–4. Writing error-review · 40 min

Materials: marked l05 emails, board, Answers_VISTA.html, Somerset Arcade, Fix-It Sheets.

Teacher prep, done between the writing class and this one: mark every email (four-criteria B1 Preliminary writing rubric: content, communicative achievement, organisation, language). List each student's errors, tag each with a category and whether the grammar involved has already been taught this year (check the group's unit map before flagging anything as "not yet taught" — will/be going to and future plans/predictions were both taught this unit, so most Unit 5 grammar errors ARE already taught material). Choose 4–6 categories that 2+ students actually made, with one real, anonymised example each ("a student wrote…", never a name). Fill Answers_VISTA.html's placeholders with those real examples, rebuild the Arcade file from the same errors, and build one Fix-It Sheet PDF per student (3–6 items each, their own errors only).

Likely category candidates for this unit's writing (the ~100-word email, pp.62-63) — confirm against the real scripts rather than assuming: will/be going to confusion (using "will" for a fixed plan, or "going to" for a spontaneous decision); missing response to one of the four notes; register slips (overly formal language to a friend, e.g. "Dear Lola" instead of "Hi Lola"); word count (well under or over the 100-word target); missing or wrong greeting/sign-off; housing/household vocabulary spelling if used in the email.

6. Unit 5 Test · 45 min

Materials: Unit 5 Test.html/.pdf (student copy), Unit 5 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf (key, do not photocopy), Unit 5 Test Listening.mp3.

Adapted from the official New Close-up B1 End-of-Unit Test 5 (Cengage/National Geographic Learning, permission granted to photocopy for classroom use). Reading (Exam Task, multiple choice with five short texts), Vocabulary (odd-one-out + definitions + collocations), Grammar (will/be going to + present forms for the future + time clauses), Listening (a radio interview about student accommodation). The writing section (20 marks in the official 80-mark test) is not included — Unit 5's writing (SB pp.62-63, the ~100-word email) was already written in class and marked separately, so retesting it here would repeat that assessment. Two grammar/vocabulary sentences are localised to a real Valencia setting; the tested grammar and the correct answers are unchanged from the original.

Total: 60 marks, 45 minutes. Reading /5 · Vocabulary /25 · Grammar /24 · Listening /6. Full key in Unit 5 Test_TEACHER.html/.pdf.
