Girls playing cricket in Pakistan

  • 2What sports do you play? — student's own answer

Reading Ex.4 · word families

Find the form in the article

  • 1athlete → athletic (adj)
  • 2compete → competition (n)
  • 3confident → confidence (n)
  • 4enjoy → enjoyment (n)
  • 5instruct → instructor (n)
  • 6success → succeed (v)
Word families: one root, several forms — adjective, noun, verb. Judo's coloured belts (text C) give "confidence" its context.
Reading Ex.5 · complete the sentences

Choose the right form

  • 1The Olympic Games is one of the most popular sports competitions in the world.
  • 2My judo instructor learned the sport in Japan.
  • 3Nick can run fast, swim and play tennis — he's very athletic.
  • 4Amy's coach always encourages her and gives her the confidence to win races.
  • 5Harry only plays basketball for enjoyment, but he's very good.
  • 6Work hard, listen to your coach, don't stop trying — this will help you succeed.
Same six words as Ex.4, now used in new sentences — check the part of speech each gap needs before choosing.
