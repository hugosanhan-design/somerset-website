New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 6 · Go for it! · SB p.65-67
PET I: Wed, 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can discuss sport habits and opinions, skim a text for gist and scan for specific detail, use word-family forms (athlete/athletic, compete/competition, confident/confidence, enjoy/enjoyment, instruct/instructor, succeed/success), and complete a person-to-text matching exam task.
Target language. Sports named in the reading (paddleboarding, scuba diving, judo, cycling, ice-skating, skurfing, snowboarding, handball); word focus (challenge, equipment, coordination, ice rink); word families for athletic/competition/confidence/enjoyment/instructor/succeed.
Exam link. Person-to-text matching is a Reading exam task format (five people, eight texts, three distractors) that recurs in the Unit 6 test's own Reading section.
Opens with: Unit 5 test results PowerPoint, built after marking, in this date's materials folder — not part of this library package.
Materials: Student's Book pp.65-67. Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck, opener discussion + reading exercises). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u06-l01 (reading vocabulary).

2. Opener · p.65 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.65.

Full-page photo: girls playing cricket at a school in Zood Khun, Pakistan (one batting with a plastic bat, a chair as a wicket). Pair discussion questions: 1) Do you think it's unusual for girls to play cricket? 2) What sports do you play? 3) What's the most unusual place you've played a sport?

Valencia link: ask whether anyone has played pilota valenciana (Valencian handball, played against a wall or in a street "carrer" in villages near Valencia) — a genuinely unusual place/sport pairing, same spirit as the photo.

4-5. Reading: skim & word families · 30 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.66-67, "Time for something new?" (eight short texts, A-H, about different sports).

Ex.3: skim-read for the categories below. Ex.4-5: word families from the article (athlete/athletic, compete/competition, confident/confidence, enjoy/enjoyment, instruct/instructor, succeed/success).

Ex.3: Three water sports: paddleboarding, scuba diving, skurfing. One winter sport: snowboarding. Three texts mentioning teachers: B (scuba diving), C (judo), G (snowboarding). Three indoor sports: scuba diving, judo, ice-skating. Four outdoor sports: paddleboarding, cycling, skurfing, snowboarding. One sport playable indoors or outdoors: handball.
Ex.4: 1 athletic (adj) · 2 competition (n) · 3 confidence (n) · 4 enjoyment (n) · 5 instructor (n) · 6 succeed (v).
Ex.5: 1 competitions · 2 instructor · 3 athletic · 4 confidence · 5 enjoyment · 6 succeed.

Valencia link: ask which of the eight sports on p.67 students could actually try in Valencia this month — paddleboarding and scuba diving on the Malvarrosa/Patacona coast, ice-skating at a city rink, cycling along the Turia Gardens.

6. Exam Task: matching people to texts · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.66, Exam Tip + Exam Task.

Five people (Freddie, Rafael, Marion, Gina, Tarek) each want to try a new sport. Underline the key points of each description (Ex.6), then match each person to the most suitable of the eight texts A-H (Ex.7). Three texts are not used.

Ex.6 (key points): 1 Freddie: wants to meet people, doesn't want to buy or rent equipment · 2 Rafael: wants an organised class with a teacher, all year round, hasn't got a lot of money · 3 Marion: loves swimming in the sea, hates noisy boats, loves surfing, wants a watersport · 4 Gina: can't swim, isn't good at ball sports, wants an outdoor sport she can do on her own but occasionally meet others · 5 Tarek: good at gymnastics, doesn't want an outdoor sport, wants to enjoy himself, hates exams and tests.
Ex.7 (Exam Task): 1 Freddie → H (handball, "a fantastic way to make friends", no equipment to buy) · 2 Rafael → C (judo, "lessons are usually inexpensive", qualified instructor, dojo) · 3 Marion → A (paddleboarding, "like a combination of surfing and rowing", for a good swimmer) · 4 Gina → D (cycling, "an activity you can enjoy alone, or you can train with a team") · 5 Tarek → E (ice-skating, "an excellent way to get exercise and have some fun", no exams, indoor rink).

7. Somerset Arcade · 5 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u06-l01.

Quick Point Grab round on the reading vocabulary (sport names, word families) if there's time left after the Exam Task.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write one more "person" description (2-3 sentences, like Freddie/Rafael/Marion/Gina/Tarek) and challenge a partner to match it to one of the remaining unused texts.

If short of time, cut:

The Arcade round (block 7) — it carries to l02's own vocabulary round. Do not cut the Exam Task itself (Ex.7): it is the lesson's core exam-format practice.
