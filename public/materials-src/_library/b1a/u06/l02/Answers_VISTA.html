Grammar Ex.1 · concept check

Zero or first?

  • aLikely to happen in the present or future → first conditional
  • bFacts and general truths → zero conditional
  • cCan use "when" instead of "if" → zero conditional
  • dCan also use may, might or could → first conditional
REMEMBER: when the if/when clause comes first, use a comma before the main clause.
Grammar Ex.2-3 · practice

Complete with the correct form

  • Ex.2 · 1If you see Ken tomorrow, will you tell him about the match?
  • 2When football players score a goal, they always celebrate.
  • 3You won't pass your exam if you don't work hard.
  • 4If we practise, we might get a place on the team.
  • Ex.3 · 1If you hit the ball past the other player, you win a point.
  • 2If Tom's team doesn't win tonight, he will/could/may/might be very sad.
  • 3Your hockey skills won't/may not/might not improve if you don't practise regularly.
  • 4When you mix yellow and blue you get green.
  • 5If it rains tomorrow, we will/could/may/might have football practice in the gym.
  • 6When it's raining, we take the bus to school.
Zero conditional items (Ex.3, 1/4/6) use the present simple in both clauses; first conditional items (2/3/5) allow will/may/might/could.
Grammar Ex.4 · unless

What does unless mean?

  • Meaninga, if she doesn't feel tired, she'll be at practice tomorrow
  • Definitionunless means the same as if...not
"Unless she feels tired" = "if she doesn't feel tired" — unless already contains the negative idea.
Grammar Ex.5 · rewrite with unless

Same meaning

  • 1Unless you say sorry for shouting at the coach, you might lose your place in the team.
  • 2Unless Stella starts practising, she won't play in the match.
  • 3If you don't do exercise, you won't get any fitter.
  • 4If they don't leave right now, they'll miss the start of the competition.
Sentences 1-2 start with "unless" and rewrite an "if...don't" original; 3-4 start with "if...don't" and rewrite an "unless" original.
Grammar Ex.6 · gym phone call

Complete the dialogue

  • 1If you join today, you only pay £25 per month.
  • 2If you visit our website...
  • 3...you will find a full list of all our classes.
  • 4And if I want to bring a friend to a class...
  • 5It usually costs £2 per class, unless you pay today.
  • 6If you wait a moment, I'll ask one of our instructors to show you around.
A real customer-service call, mixing zero conditional facts (item 1's price) with first conditional offers (items 2-6).
