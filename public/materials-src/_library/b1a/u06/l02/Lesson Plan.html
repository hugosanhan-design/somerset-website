New Close-up B1 Split A, Unit 6 · Go for it! · SB p.68-69
PET I: Wed, 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can name sports and use them correctly with go/do/play, name sports equipment via word groups and correct-option sentences, and form and use zero and first conditional sentences, including unless.
Target language. Sports (athletics, basketball, cycling, gymnastics, judo, running, skiing, snowboarding, swimming, tennis, volleyball, yoga); equipment/place words (track, stick, net, team, tournament, athlete, goal, pitch, court, racket, bat, stadium); zero and first conditional; unless.
Exam link. The multiple-choice cloze on p.68 (Exam Task) and the conditional gap-fill on p.69 both recur, in a shorter form, in the Unit 6 test's Vocabulary and Grammar sections.
Materials: Student's Book pp.68-69. Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck, all exercises from both pages). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u06-l02. Review play (if time): plays/Unit6_GoForIt_SportsAndConditionals_Play.html.

2–4. Vocabulary: sports & equipment · 45 min

Materials: Student's Book p.68, Ex.1–6.

Ex.1: sort athletics, basketball, cycling, gymnastics, judo, running, skiing, snowboarding, swimming, tennis, volleyball, yoga into go/do/play. Ex.3: complete word groups (pitch/court/___, racket/bat/___, etc.) with the word bank. Ex.6 is the Exam Task: a multiple-choice cloze about table tennis.

Ex.1: go: cycling, running, skiing, snowboarding, swimming. do: athletics, gymnastics, judo, yoga. play: basketball, tennis, volleyball.
Ex.3: 1 pitch, court, track · 2 racket, bat, stick · 3 basket, goal post, net · 4 individual, indoor, team sports · 5 match, game, tournament · 6 player, rider, athlete · 7 point, run, goal.
Ex.4: 1 racket, court · 2 track · 3 pitch, goal, goals · 4 stick · 5 bat, runs · 6 stadium.
Ex.6 (Exam Task): 1 C indoor · 2 A athletic · 3 D hit · 4 A net · 5 C points · 6 B games.

Valencia link: ask which sport from Ex.1 has its own big local team — Valencia CF (football, play) at the Mestalla, or Valencia Basket (basketball, play) — and whether students go/do/play pilota valenciana in Valencia's own tradition.

5–7. Grammar: zero/first conditional & unless · 30 min

Materials: Student's Book p.69, Ex.1–6.

Ex.1: zero conditional for facts/general truths (can use "when" instead of "if"), first conditional for likely future events (can use may/might/could). Ex.4: unless means "if...not". Ex.6: complete a gym-membership phone dialogue.

Ex.1: a first · b zero · c zero · d first.
Ex.2: 1 see, will you tell · 2 score, always celebrate · 3 won't pass, don't work · 4 practise, might get.
Ex.3: 1 win · 2 will/could/may/might be · 3 won't/may not/might not improve · 4 get · 5 will/could/may/might have · 6 take.
Ex.4: a (if she doesn't feel tired, she'll be at practice tomorrow); unless means the same as if...not.
Ex.5: 1 Unless you say sorry for shouting at the coach, you might lose your place in the team · 2 Unless Stella starts practising, she won't play in the match · 3 If you don't do exercise, you won't get any fitter · 4 If they don't leave right now, they'll miss the start of the competition.
Ex.6: 1 join · 2 visit · 3 will find · 4 want · 5 pay · 6 'll ask.

Valencia link: board example for the unless pattern — "Unless you book early, you won't get tickets for the Valencia Marathon" — then each student writes one more unless sentence about a Valencia sports event.

8. Somerset Arcade + Play · 10 min

Materials: laptop + projector, unit b1a-u06-l02. Play: groups/2026-27/pet1/plays/Unit6_GoForIt_SportsAndConditionals_Play.html.

Point Grab covers today's sports/equipment vocabulary and zero/first conditional + unless. If there's time, open the play (three scripts, each set in a real Valencia sport location); pick a 2- or 3-character version. If there's no time, it carries to l03, after speaking.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write three unless sentences of their own about sport (training, matches, equipment) and read them to a partner, who has to guess if each is zero or first conditional in style.
