Grammar Ex.1-3 · concept check

How does second conditional work?

  • Ruleif-clause: past simple; main clause: would + infinitive.
  • Ex.2 · 1"If I were you, I'd eat healthier food" → b, giving advice
  • 2"If I won Wimbledon, I'd be famous" → a, imagined unlikely/impossible
  • Ex.3we can use could instead of would, to talk about ability
Second conditional talks about the present/future as imagined or unlikely — unlike first conditional, which talks about what's realistically likely.
Grammar Ex.4 · complete the sentences

Correct verb form

  • 1If I lived near the sea, I would learn how to scuba dive.
  • 2If I were richer, I would/could buy a paddleboard.
  • 3If my family didn't enjoy sport, we wouldn't go to the sports centre every weekend.
  • 4You would get into the team if you practised more.
  • 5If she didn't spend all her time skateboarding, she would/could get better grades at school.
  • 6You wouldn't feel so tired if you went to bed earlier.
Every sentence follows the pattern: past simple in one clause, would/could + infinitive in the other.
Grammar Ex.5 · rewrite as second conditional

Same idea, imagined

  • 1 (example)If I had a bike, I wouldn't get the bus to the football club.
  • 2If he did some exercise, he would get fit/fitter.
  • 3If she was/were better at running, she would get chosen for teams.
  • 4If we had some money, we could buy new team uniforms.
  • 5If we had enough players, we wouldn't have to cancel the match.
  • 6If my friends had tickets, they could come into the stadium.
Each original sentence gives a real reason ("he doesn't do exercise, so he isn't fit"); the second conditional version imagines the opposite.
Grammar Ex.6 · mixed conditional dialogue

First or second?

  • 1If I were you, I wouldn't play either.
  • 2If he gets one more point, will he win the tournament?
  • 3If you open the cupboard, you 'll/will see lots of food.
  • 4If we get Dad to drive us, we 'll make it on time.
  • 5What would you do if you won an Olympic medal? — I would run around the track for hours!
  • 6If you were taller, would you play for your school basketball team?
Items 2-4 are realistic/likely (first conditional); items 1, 5 and 6 are imagined or unlikely (second conditional).
