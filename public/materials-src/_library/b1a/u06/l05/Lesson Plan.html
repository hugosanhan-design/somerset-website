PET I: Wed, 90 min
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can give reasons and examples to support an opinion, organise an opinion essay into paragraphs, write a 100-word opinion essay about competitive sport in class, and understand a video about mounted archery.
Target language. Opinion phrases (I think that... because, I believe that... as, In my opinion/view..., For example..., A further example is...); linking phrases (Another point in favour of/against, It's also true that, Furthermore/Moreover, In conclusion/To sum up/In summary); video vocabulary (ancient, archer, arrow, bow, connection, control, horseback, hunting, protect, target).
Exam link. The opinion essay (100 words) is the Unit 6 Writing exam task and reappears, unchanged in format, as the Unit 6 test's writing task (assessed here, not on the test).
Materials: Student's Book pp.74-76. Video: "Mounted archery" (track 3, video). Answers_VISTA.html (reveal deck). Somerset Arcade: b1a-u06-l05.

1–6. Writing: opinion essay · 75 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.74-75, Ex.1–7.

Ex.1 (sentence 1 is a model in the book; students write 2-5). Ex.3: the model essay mentions being healthy, being sociable and paying attention to others, not competitiveness, stress or school work. The real Exam Task is written individually, in class, and collected for the writing error-review in l06.

Ex.1 (suggested answers, items 2-5): 2 I think that every child should learn to swim as they need to be safe in the water · 3 Individual sports are important. For example, they encourage independence · 4 I believe that playing computer games is a good idea because it improves coordination and helps you concentrate · 5 I think that walking regularly is good for your health as it keeps you fit and makes you feel calmer.
Ex.2 (language used in the model): "I think that... as", "In my opinion", "I also believe that... because", "in my view", "In conclusion, I feel that...".
Ex.3: the writer mentions points 2, 3 and 6 (being healthy, being sociable, paying attention to other people) — not competitiveness, stress or school work.
Ex.4 (possible answers): 1 For: sports stars entertain millions and are incredible at their job. Against: they don't do really important work · 2 For: if children try harder at sport, they might try harder at everything. Against: too much competition may cause stress · 3 For: academic work matters for the job world. Against: many subjects benefit a child in life, so schools should teach them all · 4 For: children might think it's OK to hit someone. Against: with a parent present to explain it's just a sport, it can be fine.
Ex.6-7: students' own essays and self-assessment — no fixed key; marked individually after class using the four PET writing criteria, feeding l06's error-review.

Valencia link: if a student needs a concrete local example for the essay, suggest Valencia CF's youth academy or the Valencia Marathon's junior race as real examples of competitive sport for young people.

7. Video: Mounted archery · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.76. Video: "Mounted archery" (a mounted archer at a competition in Aomori, Japan).

Before you watch (Ex.1): find archer, arrow, bow, target in the photo. While you watch (Ex.2): choose the correct option in eight sentences. After you watch (Ex.3): complete the nine-gap summary with the word bank, then watch again to check.

Ex.1: archer = the person on the horse · arrow = the object the person is shooting · bow = the object the person is holding · target = the black and white diamond with the red circle.
Ex.2: 1 equipment · 2 easy · 3 shoot · 4 an ancient · 5 protect · 6 training · 7 connection · 8 successful.
Ex.3: 1 ancient · 2 hunting · 3 protect · 4 arrow · 5 horseback · 6 control · 7 connection · 8 secret · 9 mind.

Valencia link: ask whether any "ancient skill" from Valencia's own traditions has become a modern sport or competition, the way mounted archery has — pilota valenciana is the obvious parallel, a centuries-old game still played competitively today.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers who finish the essay early re-read it and add one more linking phrase from the Useful Language box (Furthermore, Moreover, It's also true that) to strengthen a paragraph.

If short of time, cut:

Video Ex.1 (before you watch) can be done as a 1-minute whole-class call-out rather than pair discussion, to protect the full 30 minutes for the Exam Task essay, which must never be cut or rushed.
