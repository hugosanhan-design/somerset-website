Writing Ex.4 · for and against

Two sides of the argument

  • 1 Sports stars earn too much money.For: they entertain millions and are incredible at their job. Against: they don't do really important work.
  • 2 Competitive sports encourage children to try harder.For: if children try harder at sport, they might try harder at everything. Against: too much competition may cause stress.
  • 3 School is for academic work only.For: academic work matters for the job world. Against: many subjects benefit a child in life, so schools should teach them all.
  • 4 We shouldn't let children watch violent sports.For: children might think it's OK to hit someone. Against: with a parent present to explain, it can be fine.
Possible answers — other reasonable for/against arguments are also acceptable, as long as they're relevant and clear.
