Mixing up conditionals

A student wrote

[PLACEHOLDER — e.g. "Unless children will play sport, they won't be healthy" (mixing unless with will in the same clause)]

Fixed

Rule: after "if" or "unless", use the present simple (zero/first conditional) or past simple (second conditional) — never "will" or "would" in that clause.
