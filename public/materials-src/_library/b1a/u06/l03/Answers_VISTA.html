Listening Ex.1 · cross out the odd option

Which word doesn't fit?

  • 1The ___ must be extremely fit and strong. Cross out: limits
  • 2One of the ___ races in the world. Cross out: incredible
  • 3Competitors must ___ some very difficult conditions. Cross out: carry on
  • 4Most deserts are extremely ___ places. Cross out: windiest
  • 5Only a small number of people can enter this ___. Cross out: success
Two of the three options fit each gap grammatically or in meaning; the crossed-out word is the odd one.

Listening Exam Task Ex.4 · The Four Deserts Race

Complete the fact file

  • 1Race starts in: the Atacama Desert, in Chile.
  • 2Length of each part of the race: 250 km.
  • 3Gobi Desert: strong winds and snow in the mountains.
  • 4Sahara Desert: difficult to breathe because of sandstorms.
  • 5Antarctica: competitors see penguins, seals and whales.
  • 6They sleep on real beds.
Spelling matters: names are always spelled out loud in the recording (letter by letter, "double" for repeated letters).
Speaking Ex.2 · advantage or disadvantage?

Sort the comments

  • aIt helps you meet new people. → A
  • bIt needs special equipment. → D
  • cIt has got a lot of rules. → D
  • dIt is very challenging. → D
  • eIt isn't expensive. → A
  • fIt is quite dangerous. → D
  • gIt is very popular. → A
  • hIt is very unusual. → A
Suggested answers — a few of these could reasonably go either way depending on the sport in mind; discuss disagreements as a group.
