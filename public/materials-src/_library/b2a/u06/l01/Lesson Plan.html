Target language. Circus/leisure vocabulary from the article (treated, impress, exploit, welfare).

2. Reading lead-in: Exercises 1–2 · 11 min

Materials: Student's Book p.66, Ex.1–2.

Ex.1: pairs discuss four questions — what you can see at a circus, whether they have been to one, whether circuses are just for children, and whether any aspect of the circus might make people uncomfortable. Ex.2: find four words in the texts on p.67 (line references given: welfare line 11, impress line 12, treated line 63, exploit line 73) and use them to complete four sentences.

Ex.2 answers: 1 treated · 2 impress · 3 exploit · 4 welfare.

3. Discuss and personalise: Exercise 3 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.66, Ex.3.

Pairs discuss the four Ex.2 statements (animals used to be treated badly; juggling/unicycles don't impress me; we shouldn't exploit animals for entertainment; circuses take care of animals' welfare) and say which they agree with — genuine discussion, no fixed answer.

Valencia link: ask whether the group has seen the debate over the former Tívoli circus site or Valencia's own animal-free circus ordinances — several Spanish cities, Valencia included, have restricted animal circuses in recent years; does the group think that is the right call?

4. Exam Tip + prediction: Exercise 4 · 17 min

Materials: Student's Book p.66 (Exam Tip) and p.67, four texts A–D about circuses.

Read the Exam Tip together: decide the type of text first (story, advert, blog, essay), think about where you'd see it and who it's for, and remember a text usually has one main purpose even if it does several things. Students look only at the photo, headings and layout of the four texts on p.67 and guess what each is about — this doesn't need to finish today; l02 completes the actual matching (Ex.5) and reveals the full key (1D 2A 3C 4C 5B 6A 7D 8B).

Valencia link: before guessing starts, ask the group what kind of circus or street-performance advert they might see around Valencia (Fallas-time street performers, the Bombas Gens area's occasional circus tents) and how the layout of such an advert would look different from a blog or an essay.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write one more sentence of their own for each of the four p.66 Word Focus words (treated, impress, exploit, welfare), about a different topic (a zoo, a workplace, a pet).

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 3 (personalised discussion of the four statements) can be shortened to two statements rather than all four if the opener discussion (Block 1) ran long. Do not cut the Exam Tip or Ex.4: predicting what each text is about is the whole basis for l02's matching task.
