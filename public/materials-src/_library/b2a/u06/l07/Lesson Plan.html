3. Model report: Exercises 3-4 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.74, model cinema report + input bar charts.
Ex.3 answer: the writer left out the "selection of films" reason and the "better range of snacks" suggestion — not many people mentioned either in the survey, so probably not worth including in a short report.
Ex.4 answers (statistic phrase → real number): the vast majority of people → about 90% · almost one third → about 30% · well over half of interviewees → about 60% · the most popular suggestion → more than 50% · almost a quarter of interviewees → 24% · more than a quarter of respondents → 26% · one in ten interviewees → 10%.

6. The real Exam Task · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.75, "Writing a report" — social club, 100 former members surveyed.

Task: You are a member of a social club which organises activities for students and young people. Membership has been falling sharply. Write a report for the club leaders highlighting why people are leaving and suggesting ways to attract new members, using the input data (Q1: main reasons for leaving; Q2: suggested improvements). 140-190 words.

Valencia link: before writing, ask the group to name a real Valencia social club, sports club or youth association (e.g. a local peña fallera, a sports club, a scouting group) to picture as they write — it makes the report feel real rather than abstract.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers complete the Reflection Checklist (Ex.8) in detail, then try the Somerset Arcade Point Grab quietly while others finish writing.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 5 (extra reporting-language paragraph) can be cut if the group needs the full 15 minutes for the real Exam Task. Never cut the real task itself or set it as homework — house rule.
