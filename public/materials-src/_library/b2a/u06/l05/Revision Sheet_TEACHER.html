FCE I · Unit 6 · Grammar p72

Teacher key: question tags, indirect and negative questions

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Tags
  1. wasn't it
  2. can she
  3. have they
  4. aren't I
  5. weren't there
2 Circle
  1. the station is
  2. the match starts
  3. if
  4. if the bus stops
  5. the tickets cost
  6. whether
3 Indirect
  1. where the Palau de les Arts is
  2. if / whether the museum opens on Mondays
  3. how much a ticket costs
  4. if / whether Jordi won the race
4 Negative questions
  1. weren't
  2. hasn't
  3. didn't (last night = past)
  4. isn't
  5. can't (surprise: Everyone does!)
5 Mistakes
  1. where the station is
  2. doesn't he
  3. shall we
  4. if the museum is open on Sundays
Typical errors: *isn't it? as a universal tag (Spanish ¿no?), direct word order after Could you tell me, *does kept in indirect questions, *I'm late, am not I?.
