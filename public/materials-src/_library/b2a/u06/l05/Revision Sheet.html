Revision: question tags, indirect and negative questions

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Remember
• Tags: positive sentence + negative tag, negative sentence + positive tag, with the same auxiliary. I'm + aren't I?. Let's + shall we?.
• Indirect questions: statement word order, no do / does / did. With no question word, use if or whether.
• Negative questions show criticism, surprise, sympathy or doubt, or ask the listener to agree.
1Add a question tag.
  1. The paella was delicious, ?
  2. Irene can't swim, ?
  3. They have never been to Mestalla, ?
  4. I'm late, ?
  5. There were lots of people at the Fallas, ?
2Circle the correct option.
  1. Could you tell me where the station is / is the station?
  2. I don't know what time does the match start / the match starts.
  3. Do you know if / what Nuria is coming?
  4. Can you tell me does the bus stop / if the bus stops near the Malvarrosa?
  5. I wonder how much do the tickets cost / the tickets cost.
  6. Could you tell me whether / what the shop opens on Sundays?
