3-4. Listening Exam Task: Exercises 3-5 · 23 min

Materials: Student's Book p.70. Audio: Tracks 6.4, 6.5.
Ex.4 answers: 1 The Women's World Cup — the first was held in China in 1991 · 2 a tiny bus · 3 the victory of the US women's team · 4 four · 5 ticket sales.
Ex.5 Exam Task full key: 1 265 million / 265,000,000 · 2 nineteenth century / 19th century · 3 3,000 / three thousand · 4 rules · 5 rubber · 6 (stone) courts · 7 feet or hands · 8 high ring · 9 war · 10 team thinking.

Valencia link: ask whether the group thinks women's football gets fair coverage in the Valencian press compared with men's football (Valencia CF Femenino vs Valencia CF).

6. Speaking Exam Task: Exercises 3-6 · 8 min

Materials: Student's Book p.71. Audio: Tracks 6.6, 6.7.
Ex.3 (most/least positive): most positive about later opening hours, music concerts, half-price tickets on weekdays; least positive about more cafés/restaurants and free transport around the park.
Ex.4 (chosen as most successful): music concerts and similar events.
Ex.5 (techniques a-f used for ideas 1-5): 1 b (the big problem with amusement parks is that...) · 2 c, f · 3 a, e, f · 4 c · 5 d.

Valencia link: for the Exam Task, ask pairs to imagine the new attraction is being built near the Ciutat de les Arts i les Ciències — would a sports stadium, shopping mall, concert venue, amusement park or swimming pool fit best there?

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write two more "Justifying choices" sentences from the Useful Language box, applied to a different local development (a new metro line, a new beach facility).

If short of time, cut:

Speaking Ex.1 (general amusement-park chat) can be shortened to one question if the listening Exam Task ran long. Do not cut the collaborative Exam Task itself (Ex.6): it is the exact exam format students need to practise under time pressure (about 2 minutes discussion, 1 minute decision).
