Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can discuss robots/AI in fiction and reality, complete a video comprehension task, and consolidate the whole unit (sports vocabulary, phrasal verbs, prepositions, -ing/infinitives, question tags, indirect and negative questions) through the unit play and a whole-unit Point Grab.
Even-unit house rule: p.76 is a VIDEO page (Units 2/4/6), not "Live well, study well" (odd units).
Materials: Student's Book p.76 + Review p.82 (SB p.154 in the full edition). Answers_VISTA.html. Play: groups/2026-27/fce1/plays/Unit6_TakeItEasy_Play.html (reused as-is, not rebuilt). Somerset Arcade: b2a-u06-l08. Video 3.

Vocabulary Ex.3: 1 onto · 2 to · 3 about · 4 in · 5 of · 6 at.
Grammar Ex.4: 1 cycling, warming up · 2 Living, to go · 3 to play, to form · 4 Training, getting up · 5 swimming/to swim, to get · 6 scoring, to lose · 7 slow down, to finish.
Grammar Ex.5: 1 hasn't it · 2 didn't they · 3 aren't I · 4 weren't there · 5 shall we · 6 won't we.
Grammar Ex.6: 1 me what we are waiting for · 2 didn't you go · 3 if/whether the ticket includes · 4 Wasn't the party · 5 if/whether John has gone out.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write two sentences about whether they agree robots could ever "understand human emotion", using one -ing form and one to + infinitive.

If short of time, cut:

The unit play can run shorter (one scene rather than all three) if the Review took longer than planned. Do not cut the video Exam Task (Block 2-3): this is the only video-comprehension practice in the unit.
