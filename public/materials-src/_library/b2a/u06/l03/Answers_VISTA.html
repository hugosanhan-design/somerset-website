Exercise 3 · match sentence to rule

Why that form?

  • aattempt/promise + to-infinitive → matches sentence 7
  • benjoy/involve + -ing → matches sentence 1
  • ccontinue/start + either, no difference → matches sentence 3
  • dstop/try + either, different meaning → matches sentence 5
  • elet/make + object + infinitive (no to) → matches sentence 2
  • fwould rather + infinitive (no to) → matches sentence 4
  • ghad better + infinitive (no to) → matches sentence 6
Full key: a7 b1 c3 d5 e2 f4 g6.
Exercise 4 · complete the rules

Purpose, subject, prepositions

  • aWe use to + infinitive after some adjectives (e.g. "easy to get").
  • bWe use to + infinitive after too/enough.
  • cWe use -ing forms as the subject of a sentence.
  • dWe use to + infinitive to express purpose.
  • eWe use -ing forms after prepositions.
