Revision: -ing form and infinitives

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Remember
• enjoy, involve + -ing. attempt, promise + to + infinitive. start, continue: either.
• let, make + object + infinitive without to. would rather, had better + infinitive without to.
• -ing after prepositions and as a subject. to + infinitive after too / enough, after some adjectives and for purpose.
• Different meaning: stop, regret, go on + to or -ing.
1Circle the correct form.
  1. Raquel promised to be / being on time for the match at Mestalla.
  2. Our coach doesn't let us to leave / leave before the end.
  3. I'd rather stay / to stay at home than go to the Malvarrosa in the rain.
  4. Sergio enjoys to cycle / cycling along the Turia every morning.
  5. Laia is very good at play / playing tennis.
  6. You'd better to take / take an umbrella.
  7. Paula is old enough to drive / driving.
2Write the correct form of the verb.
  1. Jordi attempted (climb) the wall, but it was too slippery.
  2. Running a marathon involves (train) for months.
  3. Her mother made her (practise) for an hour every day.
  4. I'm tired tonight. I'd rather (watch) the match at home.
  5. We were thirsty, so we stopped (buy) a horchata.
  6. Nuria is saving money (buy) a new bike.
  7. The film is too long (watch) in one evening.

3Choose the meaning, a or b.
  1. Gemma stopped to talk to Pablo. a) She was talking and then she finished. b) She stopped what she was doing so she could talk.
  2. Tomás went on talking after the bell. a) He continued. b) He talked later.
  3. I regret saying that to Eva. a) I feel bad about a past mistake. b) I feel bad that I must say something now.
4Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.
  1. Carlos enjoys to swim in the Albufera.
  2. My mother made me to tidy my room.
  3. Learn English is not easy.
  4. Daniel went to the Plaza del Ayuntamiento for to see the mascletà.
5True for you.
  1. Write a sentence with I'd rather.
  2. Write a sentence with too and to + infinitive.
