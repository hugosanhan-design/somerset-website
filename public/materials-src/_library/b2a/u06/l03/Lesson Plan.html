Lesson Plan · b2a-u06-l03
New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 6 · Take it easy
FCE I · Friday B2
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can choose correctly between the -ing form and to + infinitive after a range of verbs, adjectives, too/enough, as a subject, to express purpose and after prepositions.
Target language. -ing form and infinitives: verbs followed by to + infinitive (attempt, promise), by -ing (enjoy, involve), by either with little difference (continue, start) or a real difference (stop, try), verb + object + infinitive without to (let, make), would rather + infinitive without to, had better + infinitive without to.
Exam link. B2 First Use of English Part 2 (open cloze) regularly tests -ing/infinitive choices after specific verbs.
Materials: Student's Book p.69. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u06-l03. Audio: Track 6.3. Grammar reference 6.1, p.91.

Valencia link: ask the group to build one sentence each about a hobby popular in Valencia (paella-cooking classes, Turia Gardens jogging, the beach) using "to express purpose" (rule d) and one using "after prepositions" (rule e).

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write three true sentences about themselves, one each using "would rather + infinitive", "had better + infinitive" and a verb from Ex.5 (regret, mean or go on) with the correct -ing/infinitive form.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 5 (meaning differences) can be done as a quick class check rather than pairwork if the gap-fill text (Block 5) needs more time. Do not cut Ex.6: it is the only exercise that drills all eleven verb patterns together in connected text, closest to the exam format.
