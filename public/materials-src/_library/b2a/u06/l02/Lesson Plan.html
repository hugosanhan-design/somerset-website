Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can complete a B2 First multiple-choice-with-four-texts Exam Task, and use eight sports words/phrases from a listening about competitive sport.

Friday B2: this is the second half of the 2-hour slot that opened with l01. FCE I runs it as its own 60-minute class two days later.

2. Reading Exam Task: Exercise 5 · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.66-67, multiple-choice with four texts.

Questions 1-8, choose the best answer A, B, C or D from the four circus texts.

Full key: 1D 2A 3C 4C 5B 6A 7D 8B.

Valencia link: after checking, ask the group whether they'd rather see a modern circus (no animals, more acrobatics and technology, like Circ del Sol-style shows sometimes touring through Valencia) or a traditional one — which is closer to Text A's "unusual" circus?

4-5. Vocabulary: sports · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.68, Ex.1-4. Audio: Track 6.2.

Ex.1: listen and match three speakers to photos of tennis, football and running. Ex.2: match Speaker 1's eight football words to their definitions. Ex.3: choose the correct option in six sentences. Ex.4: replace six bold phrases with Speaker 3's words/phrases.

Ex.2 answers: 1d 2c 3a 4b 5f 6e 7h 8g.
Ex.3 answers: 1 in · 2 lap · 3 competitive · 4 victory · 5 marathon · 6 medal.
Ex.4 answers: 1 my opponent · 2 set · 3 round · 4 semifinal · 5 trophy · 6 represent.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write three sentences about a sport they play or watch, using at least three of the new sports words.

If short of time, cut:

Vocabulary Ex.4 (replacing Speaker 3's phrases) can be set as a quick individual task with a fast class check, rather than full pairwork discussion, if the Exam Task ran long. Do not cut the Exam Task feedback (Block 3): reassure, aimed at and relevant recur in later B2 First reading texts.
