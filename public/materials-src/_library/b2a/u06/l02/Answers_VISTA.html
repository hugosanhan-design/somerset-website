Exam Task · questions 1-8 · multiple-choice with four texts

Circus texts A-D

  • 1What is unusual about the circus in Text A? → D — part of the show is created by computers
  • 2What is the main purpose of Text B? → A — to invite new members
  • 3What sort of people is Circus Club aimed at? → C — people who want to enjoy themselves
  • 4What is the job of the writer of Text C? → C — photographer
  • 5How did the writer of Text C get the performers to trust him? → B — by taking the time to get to know them
  • 6What is the main purpose of Text D? → A — to present different views
  • 7Which texts mention what it's like to be a circus performer? → D — B, C and D
  • 8What do the texts suggest about circuses? → B — there is a lot of hard work the audience doesn't see
Full key: 1D 2A 3C 4C 5B 6A 7D 8B. Also elicit: reassure (to stop someone worrying), aimed at (written for), relevant (important or connected to people's lives).

Vocabulary Ex.2 · Speaker 1's words

Football vocabulary

  • 1defender → d — a person who tries to stop attacking players
  • 2on target → c — going in the right direction
  • 3penalty → a — a free chance to score because your opponent broke the rules
  • 4pitch → b — a grass-covered field for sports such as football
  • 5referee → f — the person who makes sure people follow the rules
  • 6save → e — catch a ball to prevent somebody scoring
  • 7score → h — to get a point or goal in a competition
  • 8shot → g — an attempt to hit or kick a ball to try to get a point
Full key: 1d 2c 3a 4b 5f 6e 7h 8g.
