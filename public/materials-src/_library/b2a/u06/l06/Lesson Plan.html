New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 6 · Take it easy
FCE I · Friday B2
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use six phrasal verbs, five prepositions and six collocations from Unit 6, and can complete a B2 First key-word sentence-transformation Exam Task.
Target language. Phrasal verbs (burst out, give up, keep up with, knock over, show off, take off); prepositions (about/for/from, into/onto/out of, in/to/with, on/over/up, from/of/with); collocations (come close to, hold an important place, know the first thing about, make a big difference, put at risk, take part in).
Exam link. B2 First Use of English Part 4 (key word transformation, two marks per gap).
Materials: Student's Book p.73. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u06-l06.

Materials: Student's Book p.73, Ex.2-3.

Ex.3 answers: 1 take · 2 come · 3 putting · 4 make · 5 know · 6 hold.

4. Exam Task: Exercise 4 · 18 min

Materials: Student's Book p.73, Ex.4, sentence transformation.
Ex.4 full key: 1 tell me whether there are · 2 should make wild animals · 3 a really clever combination of · 4 when the treasure hunt starts · 5 Why didn't anyone tell · 6 doesn't (really) appeal to me.

Valencia link: for extra practice, ask pairs to build one more key-word sentence about a Valencia activity, e.g. transforming "I don't know the first thing about paella" using KNOW.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write three original sentences, each using one phrasal verb, one preposition phrase and one collocation from this page.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 3 (collocations) can be reduced to items 1-3 if the Exam Task needs more time. Do not cut the Exam Tip discussion before Ex.4: "write the part you know, even if unsure of the whole gap" is worth real marks in the actual exam.
