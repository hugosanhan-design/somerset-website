Exam Task · Ex.4 · sentence transformation

Key-word transformation

  • 1Are there any tickets left? → Could you tell me whether there are any tickets left?
  • 2Wild animals shouldn't be forced to perform tricks. → I don't think they should make wild animals perform tricks.
  • 3The game combines strength and speed really cleverly. → The game involves a really clever combination of strength and speed.
  • 4What time does the treasure hunt start? → I'd like to know when the treasure hunt starts.
  • 5I wasn't told about the party! Why was that? → Why didn't anyone tell me about the party?
  • 6I don't really like the idea of bungee jumping. → Bungee jumping doesn't (really) appeal to me.
