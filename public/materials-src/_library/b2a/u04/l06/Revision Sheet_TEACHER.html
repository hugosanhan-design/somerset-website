FCE I · Unit 4 · Use of English p49

Teacher key: city expressions and multiple-choice cloze

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Expressions
  1. 1 the talk of the town | 2 without fail | 3 all walks of life | 4 on top of each other | 5 on the increase | 6 take into consideration | 7 resist the temptation | 8 the best of both worlds
2 Circle
  1. 1 town | 2 urban | 3 town (went to town) | 4 place (all over the place) | 5 town (ghost town) | 6 lane
3 Open cloze
  1. 1 in | 2 a | 3 to | 4 since | 5 on | 6 from | 7 so | 8 of
4 Type of word
  1. 1 P | 2 A | 3 T | 4 L (since) | 5 P | 6 P | 7 D (so) | 8 P
5 Circle
  1. 1 high | 2 noise | 3 little | 4 leading
6 Own sentence
  1. Accept any correct sentence with the expression.
Typical errors: 'in the fast road' (lane), 'on the increasing' (on the increase), 'all the walks of life', 'take in consideration' (take into), 'since' vs 'for' in item 4, 'hard of finding' (hard to find). The expressions are fixed: no changes to the words.
