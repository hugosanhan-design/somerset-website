New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 4 · City living
FCE I · Friday B2 (paired with l05)
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use collocations and expressions about city life (living on top of each other, resist the temptation, the best of both worlds, all walks of life, on the increase, without fail, take into consideration) and complete a multiple-choice cloze exam task.
Target language. City-life collocations and expressions; place/town/city near-synonym pairs.
Exam link. B2 First Use of English Part 1 (multiple-choice cloze).
60-minute session (FCE I). Friday B2 pairs this with l05 in their 2-hour slot, running after it.
Materials: Student's Book p.49. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u04-l06.

2. Choose the correct word: Exercise 2 · 8 min

Materials: Student's Book p.49, Ex.2.
Answers: 1 town (talk of the town) · 2 urban (urban jungle) · 3 town (went to town) · 4 place (all over the place) · 5 town (ghost town) · 6 lane (fast lane).

4. Exam Task: Exercise 4 · 16 min

Materials: Student's Book p.49, Exam Task, eight-gap text about city stress and the ‘urban jungle’.
Answers: 1 A little · 2 C high · 3 A urban · 4 D noise · 5 C leading · 6 A quiet · 7 C fast · 8 A walks.

Valencia link: ask the group whether they think Valencia counts as an ‘urban jungle’ the way the text describes, or whether its lower population density and green spaces (the Turia Gardens) make it feel calmer than Shanghai or Mexico City, the two cities named in the text.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write one true sentence of their own using each of three Ex.1 collocations (living on top of each other, the best of both worlds, from all walks of life).

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 1 (personalised discussion of the seven collocations) can be shortened to three or four sentences rather than all seven if time is tight. Do not cut the Exam Task (Ex.4) or its full key check: this is the lesson's only Use of English Part 1-style practice in the unit.
