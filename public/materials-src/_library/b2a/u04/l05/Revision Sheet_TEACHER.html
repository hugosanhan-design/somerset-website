Teacher key: countable and uncountable nouns, quantifiers

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. 1 news is | 2 have | 3 furniture | 4 is | 5 are | 6 is
2 Quantifier
  1. 1 many | 2 little | 3 a few (countable, about six) | 4 a little | 5 much | 6 little (not enough)
3 C or U
  1. 1 U | 2 C | 3 U (room to put things) | 4 C (lights = lamps)
4 Box
  1. 1 every | 2 plenty | 3 all | 4 no | 5 many (How many) | 6 several (many does not fit: three or four is not a large number)
5 Errors
  1. 1 The police are looking for the thief. | 2 I have few friends in this city, so I feel lonely. | 3 There are too many cars in the city centre. | 4 We need more equipment for the gym.
6 Own sentence
  1. Accept any correct sentence with a suitable quantifier.
Spanish speakers pluralise equipment, furniture, news, advice (los muebles, las noticias), use much with plurals ('too much cars'), say little for 'pocos' with plurals, and treat police as singular (la policía).
