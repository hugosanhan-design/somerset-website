Revision: countable and uncountable nouns, quantifiers

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Uncountable (no plural, no a): traffic, pollution, stress, health, air, money, equipment, furniture. News, maths, politics take a singular verb; clothes, outskirts, police take a plural verb.
many, few, a few + countable plural. much, little, a little + uncountable. few / little = not enough; a few / a little = some.
Some nouns change meaning: experience, space, light, work, competition.
1Circle the correct option.
  1. The (news is / news are) very good today.
  2. The police (has / have) closed the road near the Plaza del Ayuntamiento.
  3. We need to buy new (furniture / furnitures) for the flat.
  4. There (is / are) too much traffic in the city centre at eight o'clock.
  5. The outskirts of the city (is / are) quiet at night.
  6. Politics (is / are) a difficult subject for many students.
2Circle the correct quantifier.
  1. There aren't (many / much) parking spaces near the Turia.
  2. We have very (few / little) time before the train leaves.
  3. Only (a few / a little) people came to the concert: about six.
  4. Can I have (a few / a little) milk in my coffee, please?
  5. How (many / much) pollution is there in your city?
  6. There is (few / little) entertainment in the village, so young people leave.
3Countable (C) or uncountable (U)? Write C or U.
  1. Daniel has a lot of experience as a guide in Valencia.
  2. Visiting the Albufera at sunset was a wonderful experience.
  3. There is not enough space for a sofa in my room.
  4. Please switch off the lights when you leave.
