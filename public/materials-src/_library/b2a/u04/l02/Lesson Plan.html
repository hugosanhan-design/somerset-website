FCE I · Friday B2 (paired with l01)
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can complete a ten-item multiple-matching reading task using the elimination technique from l01's Exam Tip, and can use eight city-development words (settle, urbanisation, rapid, urban, suburbs, outskirts, rural, inhabitants) plus six vocabulary-pair distinctions (bike lane/pavement, crossroads/roadworks, etc.).
Target language. City-growth vocabulary (settle, urbanisation, outskirts, suburbs, rural, inhabitants) and precise city-infrastructure pairs (junction/traffic light, crossing/zone, camera/limit, facilities/venues).
Exam link. B2 First Reading Part 7 multiple matching, completed in full with the real key.
60-minute session (FCE I). Friday B2 pairs this with l01 in their 2-hour slot, running after it.
Materials: Student's Book pp.42-44. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u04-l02.

2–3. Exam Task: Exercise 7 · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.42-43, Exam Task questions 1–10, sections A–D on p.43.
Answers: 1 C · 2 B · 3 A · 4 D · 5 B · 6 C · 7 D · 8 A · 9 B · 10 A.

Note for feedback: sections B, A and D are each used twice, section C twice as well — every section is used at least twice, so remind the group the Exam Tip's warning ("sections may be chosen more than once") is not just a formality here.

4. Vocabulary: Exercise 1 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.44, Ex.1 (discussion, no fixed answer).

Pairs read seven sentences and discuss what the bold phrases mean (the outskirts, a residential area, commercial areas, the suburbs, shopping districts, an industrial park, housing estates), describing real examples from places they know.

Valencia link: ask directly — which Valencia neighbourhood is most like a "residential area" (e.g. Ciudad Fallera), which is most "commercial" (Colon/Gran Vía), and where is the city's own industrial park (Fuente del Jarro, technically in Paterna, just outside the city)?

5. Vocabulary: Exercise 2 · 8 min

Materials: Student's Book p.44, Ex.2 (six close-pair sentences).
Answers: 1 bike lane / pavement · 2 crossroads / roadworks · 3 junction / traffic light · 4 crossing / zone · 5 limit / camera · 6 venues / facilities.

6. Vocabulary: Exercise 3 · 5 min

Materials: Student's Book p.44, Ex.3 (eight-gap text on the history of cities).
Answers: 1 settle · 2 urbanisation · 3 rapid · 4 urban · 5 suburbs · 6 outskirts · 7 rural · 8 inhabitants.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write a two-sentence description of Valencia using at least three Ex.3 words (settle, urbanisation, rapid, urban, suburbs, outskirts, rural, inhabitants).

If short of time, cut:

Vocabulary Ex.1 (discussion of the seven bold phrases) can be shortened to three items rather than all seven if the Exam Task discussion (Block 3) ran long. Do not cut Ex.7 itself or its key check: a real B2 First Reading mark only comes from completing the whole matching task properly.
