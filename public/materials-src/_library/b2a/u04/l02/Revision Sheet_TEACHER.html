Teacher key: cities

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Match
  1. 1 d outskirts | 2 g commercial area | 3 e housing estate | 4 b industrial park | 5 c roadworks | 6 a inhabitants | 7 f urbanisation
2 Gaps
  1. 1 bike lane | 2 pavement | 3 junction (crossroads is not in the box) | 4 traffic light | 5 speed limit | 6 speed camera | 7 commute | 8 venues (places to watch events) | 9 facilities (for people who do sports)
3 Circle
  1. 1 urban | 2 rapid | 3 settle | 4 zone | 5 roadworks | 6 residential
4 Cloze
  1. 1 rural | 2 rapid | 3 urbanisation | 4 settle | 5 outskirts | 6 inhabitants
5 Own sentences
  1. Accept any true, correct sentence that uses the word in a natural collocation (e.g. I live in the suburbs; I commute by Metrovalencia).
Typical errors: suburb/outskirts mixed up (suburbs are residential areas, outskirts = the outer edge); sidewalk (US) for pavement; 'make a commute'; inhabitants confused with habitants. Spanish urbanización is a housing estate, not urbanisation.
