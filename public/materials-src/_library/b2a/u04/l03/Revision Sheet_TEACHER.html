Teacher key: future forms

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. 1 is going to (evidence: black clouds; will is weaker here) | 2 will (decision now) | 3 are going to (plan already made) | 4 Shall (suggestion with we) | 5 get (present simple after as soon as) | 6 comes (present simple after until) | 7 will be lying (in progress at a future time) | 8 will have finished (before a future time)
2 Verbs
  1. 1 will be sitting ('ll be sitting; are going to be sitting also fine) | 2 will have lived (will have been living also accepted) | 3 will not tell / won't tell | 4 will get / 'll get | 5 arrives | 6 am visiting / am going to visit (both accepted: arrangement)
3 Errors
  1. 1 When I arrive in Madrid, I will call you. | 2 I think it will rain tomorrow. (I think it is going to rain is acceptable) | 3 We will not go out until it stops raining. | 4 By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner. | 5 Shall we go for a coffee after class?
4 True to you
  1. Accept any logical sentence: will be + -ing in sentence 1, will have + past participle in sentence 2.
Spanish speakers use will after when / until (Spanish future subjunctive logic: 'cuando llegue'), the present simple for predictions ('creo que llueve'), and Will we...? for suggestions. Future perfect is often replaced by will + simple verb.
