Exercise 3 · complete the rules

When to use each future form

  • aSure predictions (opinion) → will; predictions based on evidence → going to.
  • bA decision made now → will; a plan made earlier → going to.
  • cOffers and promises → will.
  • dSuggestions with I/we → shall.
  • eA less certain prediction, instead of will → should.
Exercise 4 · match to the rule

Continuous or perfect?

  • 1We'll all be wearing yellow T-shirts → c, future continuous: in progress around a future time.
  • 2Let's go shopping after it finishes → d, no will/going to after before/after/when/until.
  • 3I'm sure it will have finished by midday → a, future perfect simple: completed before a future time.
  • 4I'll have been cycling all morning, so I'll be tired → b, future perfect continuous: over a period, with a future result.
Exercise 5 · choose the correct option

Eight short exchanges

  • 1What time shall we arrive? — It's up to you.
  • 2What time will we arrive? — Hopefully around ten.
  • 3I do not know how much you'll need, but £20 should be enough.
  • 4I take taxis all the time, so I'm sure £20 will be enough.
  • 5Do not phone at six. We'll be eating dinner then.
  • 6You can phone after seven. We'll have eaten dinner by then.
  • 7I'll have been studying all day, so I'll be exhausted.
  • 8I'll have finished all my exams by then.
Exercise 6 · rewrite with the word given

Six transformations

  • 1I'll message you when we finish shopping — hopefully soon.
  • 2By the time we get to the station, the train will have left.
  • 3You can't go until the light changes to green.
  • 4There are lots of maps, so you shouldn't get lost.
  • 5I'll be waiting for you outside the train station when you arrive.
  • 6By the time they finish that skyscraper, they will have been working on it for four years.
Rule reminder: never will/going to straight after before, after, when or until — use a present tense instead (when we finish, not when we will finish).
