Revision: technology and compound nouns

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Compound nouns = two words working as one: hard drive, headset, password.
Collocations: make a backup, access a website, upload photos, upgrade a phone, undo a mistake.
1Match each word with its definition.
1  backup
2  attachment
3  bookmark
4  headset
5  database
6  password
7  anti-virus program
8  hard drive
a  something you wear over your ears, often with a microphone
b  the part inside a computer where files are stored
c  a copy of your files in case you lose the originals
d  a secret mix of letters and symbols that lets you log in
e  a saved link so you can find a website again quickly
f  software that protects a computer from harmful programs
g  a large collection of information stored on a computer
h  a file sent together with an email
2Circle the correct option.
  1. Jordi's laptop is very slow, so he is thinking of (upgrading / undoing) it.
  2. Oh no, I deleted the wrong paragraph! Quick, (undo / display) it!
  3. You need a (password / headset) to log in to your school email.
  4. Daniel (bookmarked / browsed) the Mestalla ticket page so he could find it again easily.
  5. Before you open an email from a stranger, check the (attachment / backup) carefully. It may carry a virus.
  6. Only teachers can (access / bookmark) the school database.
