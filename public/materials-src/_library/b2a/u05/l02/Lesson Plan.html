1. Finish Exam Task: Exercise 5 · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.54-55, Exam Task Ex.5 (8 questions, A-D options, two parts).
Answers: 1b · 2c · 3b · 4a · 5c · 6d · 7a · 8d.

Valencia link: after checking, ask which drone use (question 5–8, from delivering aid to monitoring wildlife) students think would be most useful for the Albufera Natural Park near Valencia — monitoring wildlife (herons, the Albufera's birdlife) is the closest real-world match to the article's Rwanda/Mongolia examples.

2. Vocabulary lead-in: Exercise 1 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.56, Ex.1 (ten sentences with technology words in bold).

Pairs discuss what each bold word means (access, apps, attachments, backup, bookmark, browsing, display, undo, upgrading, virtually) and to what extent the sentences are true for them. Open discussion, no fixed answer, but every word must be used correctly in the follow-up talk.

3. Compound nouns: Exercise 2 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.56, Ex.2 (match 1–10 with a–j).
Answers: 1b (anti-virus program → protects a computer against harmful programs) · 2e (broadband → quickly download information) · 3g (database → which we can access easily) · 4a (desktop → computer around with us easily [can't carry]) · 5f (headset → worn over our ears, microphone attached) · 6h (hard drive → where we store a large amount of data) · 7c (password → which keeps our information private) · 8i (network → group of computers connected, sharing information) · 9d (wireless → computers communicate without a physical connection) · 10j (virtual reality → seems real to the person experiencing it).

4. Complete the texts: Exercise 3 · 8 min

Materials: Student's Book p.56, Ex.3 (three short texts, ten gaps, use the correct form of the compound nouns from Ex.2).
Answers: 1 virtual reality · 2 headset · 3 desktop · 4 hard drive · 5 databases · 6 broadband · 7 passwords · 8 wireless · 9 network · 10 anti-virus.

Fast finisher

Ask fast finishers to write a sentence of their own for three of the compound nouns not yet used in the class discussion.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 1 (vocabulary lead-in personalisation) can be shortened to five of the ten sentences if the exam task review ran long. Do not cut Ex.3: the compound-noun forms (singular/plural, e.g. databases vs database) are the exact point the exam repeatedly tests.
