1–3. Listening: Exercises 1–5 · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book p.58, Ex.1–5, Tracks 5.3–5.4.
Ex.2 answer: c (the boy is now using his games console).
Ex.3 (suggested answers): a, laptop: he says he was using it "before that" (before the console); his homework took two hours on the laptop, then he started the console ten minutes ago. b, phone: his mum thinks he was using it earlier, but he says he hasn't touched his phone all day.
Ex.5 (Exam Task, full key): 1B · 2C · 3C · 4B · 5C · 6A · 7C · 8C.

Valencia link: before listening, ask which of the eight picture sets (Q1: washing machine/vending machine/fridge; Q7: desktop/radio+books/printer) is closest to something in the classroom itself — a quick grounding exercise before the recording starts.

4. Discussion tasks: Speaking Exercises 1–3 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.59, Ex.1–3, Track 5.5.
Ex.1 answers: 1C (organise their own conversation, examiner silent) · 2D (examiner makes clear who each question is for) · 3D (follow-up questions like "Why not?") · 4C (main purpose: prove you work well together) · 5D (main purpose: final decision on level/grade).
Ex.3 (complete the extracts): 1 question · 2 thought · 3 example · 4 question · 5 question, truth, idea, thoughts · 6 example · 7 sense.

5. Exam Task: Speaking discussion · 13 min

Materials: Student's Book p.59, Exam Task (eight discussion questions) + Useful Language box.

Groups of three take turns being the examiner and asking the eight questions (most useful device, how technology creates problems, computers in classrooms, hours per day, new technology to invent, are we too dependent, why some fear new technology, advice for a young teenager). Use the Useful Language for thinking time, asking for repetition and interacting with a partner.

Valencia link: add one extra discussion question: "Should Valencia's schools give every student a tablet, the way some regional programmes have tried?" — genuine local debate, good B2 First discussion practice.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write their own ninth discussion question to add to the Exam Task list, and answer it using at least one phrase from each Useful Language category.

If short of time, cut:

The Speaking discussion round (Block 5) can run with six questions instead of eight if the listening review (Block 1) took longer than planned. Do not cut Ex.3 (Useful Language extracts): students need this language active before the discussion round, not just recognised.
