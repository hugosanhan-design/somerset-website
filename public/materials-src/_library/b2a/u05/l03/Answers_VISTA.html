Exercises 1–2 · listen and complete the rules

Whose drone is it?

  • 1Who does the drone belong to? → Rebecca, Chloe's sister.
  • 2What does Shazad do wrong? → He flies it too far away and loses control of it, so it crashes.
  • aUse mustn't when creating rules about what not to do.
  • bUse be not allowed to when reporting other people's rules.
  • cUse ought to when making recommendations.
  • dUse be supposed to when reporting other people's requests.
  • eUse could when asking for permission (more formal).
  • fUse can when giving permission.
Exercise 4 · match to the rule

Three kinds of ability

  • 1I managed to convince her! → c, a past achievement.
  • 2Let's see how high this drone is capable of going. → a, general ability / maximum limit.
  • 3How will you be able to control it if it's too high? → b, future ability.
Exercise 6 · probability

Sure, or just possible?

  • 1She might let me use it. → not sure.
  • 2You may remember we met last year. → more formal than might.
  • 3It can't be difficult. → sure (negative).
  • 4It must be too far away for the signal. → sure (positive).
Key: the speaker sounds sure in extracts 3 and 4; extract 2 sounds more formal than extract 1.
Exercise 7 · rewrite with the word given

Eight transformations

  • 1My phone must be in the house somewhere.
  • 2This computer is only a week old, so it can't be broken already.
  • 3Lisa is capable of performing amazing calculations in her head.
  • 4I don't understand how I'm supposed to install this software.
  • 5We finally managed to fix the computer after some difficulty.
  • 6My test results can't be right.
  • 7You ought to check the engine.
  • 8I'm sorry, but you're not allowed to use your phone in here.
Rule reminder: can't for a confident negative deduction, must for a confident positive one — the pattern the B2 First transformation task tests most.
