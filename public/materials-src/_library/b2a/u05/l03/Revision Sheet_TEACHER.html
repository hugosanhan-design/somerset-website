FCE I · Unit 5 · Grammar p57

Teacher key: modals of ability, permission, obligation and probability

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. mustn't (forbidden)
  2. ought to (advice)
  3. managed to (past achievement: not could)
  4. will be able to
  5. capable (capable of + -ing; able of is impossible)
  6. must (he wears the scarf, so we are sure)
  7. might ("I'm not sure")
  8. may (giving permission, formal)
2 Rewrite
  1. might be
  2. can't be
  3. must be
  4. are supposed to be
  5. managed to finish
  6. aren't allowed to (also: are not allowed to, 're not allowed to; aren't supposed to accepted)
3 Error hunt
  1. In the exam, you mustn't use a dictionary. (also: don't have to if the meaning is 'not necessary'; accept, but point out the difference)
  2. Jordi managed to fix the problem.
  3. Clara is capable of speaking four languages. (also: able to speak)
  4. After many attempts, I managed to / was able to pass my driving test. (could is wrong for one past achievement)
  5. You ought to phone your sister.
4 Own sentences
  1. Check: not allowed to / mustn't + base verb; ought to + base verb.
Typical Spanish-speaker errors: don't must (no tengo que / no debo), capable to (capaz de), managed to fixing, could for a single past success (pude), and ought without to. Mustn't is prohibition, not 'no hace falta'.
