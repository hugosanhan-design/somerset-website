FCE I · Unit 5 · Grammar p57

Revision: modals of ability, permission, obligation and probability

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Ability: can / be able to + infinitive, be capable of + -ing. Past achievement: was able to, managed to (not could).
Permission: Could I...? (formal ask), can / may (give), be allowed to.
Rules: mustn't = forbidden; be supposed to = reporting a rule; ought to = advice.
Probability: must be (sure), can't be (sure not), may / might be (possible).
1Circle the correct option.
  1. In the exam you (mustn't / ought to) use your phone. It is forbidden.
  2. You look exhausted, Nuria. You (ought to / mustn't) go to bed earlier.
  3. Jordi (could / managed to) fix the router after two hours of trying.
  4. By next June, Eva (was able to / will be able to) run 10 km without stopping.
  5. This new drone is (capable / able) of flying 40 km.
  6. Pablo is wearing a Valencia CF scarf. He (must / can't) be a fan.
  7. Raquel is not answering. I'm not sure, but she (might / can't) be at the Malvarrosa.
  8. The librarian says: "Yes, you (may / mustn't) use the computers now."
2Rewrite the sentence using the word in brackets.Write the missing words only.
  1. Perhaps Gemma is at the Malvarrosa. (might)
    Gemma at the Malvarrosa.
  2. I'm sure this isn't Carlos's bag. It's too small. (can't)
    This Carlos's bag.
  3. I'm sure Eva is exhausted after the trip. (must)
    Eva exhausted after the trip.
  4. The rule says we have to be quiet in the library. (supposed)
    We quiet in the library.
  5. It was difficult, but Daniel finished the course in the end. (managed)
    Daniel the course in the end.
  6. I'm sorry, but we can't let you use phones here. (allowed)
    I'm sorry, but you use phones here.
