Exercise 1 · underline the modal

Eight perfect modals

  • 1Divya should have phoned me by now. → should have phoned.
  • 2You shouldn't have tried to fix the computer. → shouldn't have tried.
  • 3She might have missed the bus. → might have missed.
  • 4She could have become a top scientist. → could have become.
  • 5Tibor can't have cooked it. → can't have cooked.
  • 6They must have been very happy. → must have been.
  • 7We would have bought a new car. → would have bought.
  • 8He needn't have told me. → needn't have told.
Exercise 2 · match to the explanation

What does each modal mean?

  • 1should have phoned → b, expecting something that hasn't happened yet.
  • 2shouldn't have tried → h, it was a mistake to do it.
  • 3might have missed → d, not sure it happened, but it's a possibility.
  • 4could have become → a, it was possible, but it didn't happen.
  • 5can't have cooked → g, fairly sure this didn't happen.
  • 6must have been → c, sure this is true about the past.
  • 7would have bought → e, wanted to, but it wasn't possible.
  • 8needn't have told → f, it happened, but it wasn't necessary.
Exercise 3 · choose the option

Eight sentences

  • 1They emailed yesterday, so they could have read it by now.
  • 2Why isn't Carlos here? He may have forgotten about our meeting.
  • 3Why did you climb that ladder? You could have fallen.
  • 4You needn't have washed the car. I was taking it to the car wash.
  • 5Hiro is brilliant at maths. He could have become a professor.
  • 6It must have been an interesting lecture — everyone stayed.
  • 7Laura failed her exams. She shouldn't have been so lazy.
  • 8I might have told you the answer, but the teacher was watching me.
Exercise 4 · rewrite with the word given

Eight transformations

  • 1She should have finished the experiment by now.
  • 2They might have missed the bus.
  • 3I would have told you how to use the device, but you didn't ask.
  • 4They ought to have updated their anti-virus software.
  • 5It can't have been easy to learn to use that app.
  • 6You shouldn't have given him another computer game.
  • 7You needn't have paid for that software.
  • 8The first people on the moon must have been both excited and terrified.
Rule reminder: perfect modals always use have + past participle — never have + past simple.
