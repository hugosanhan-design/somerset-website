Teacher key: perfect modals

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. should (expected by now)
  2. might
  3. must (the evidence is clear)
  4. can't (he was elsewhere)
  5. shouldn't (a mistake)
  6. needn't (unnecessary)
  7. could (possible, but it didn't happen)
  8. would (I would have gone, but...)
2 Rewrite
  1. might have been
  2. must have forgotten
  3. shouldn't have told
  4. can't have seen
  5. needn't have bought
3 Match
  1. 1 = d
  2. 2 = a
  3. 3 = b
  4. 4 = c
4 Error hunt
  1. She must have forgotten the meeting.
  2. I should have called you yesterday.
  3. They might have gone to Alicante.
  4. We needn't have waited so long.
  5. You could have broken your leg climbing that wall!
5 Own sentences
  1. Check: modal + have + past participle (ought to have done, needn't have done).
Typical errors: must had / should to have (the pattern is always modal + have + participle), past simple instead of participle (have went), and confusing can't have (sure it didn't happen) with needn't have (it happened but wasn't necessary).
