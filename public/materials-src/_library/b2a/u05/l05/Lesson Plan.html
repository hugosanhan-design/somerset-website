Aims. students can use perfect modals (should/shouldn't have, might/could/can't/must have, would have, needn't have, ought to have) to talk about past possibility, obligation and criticism.
Target language. perfect modals: should have, shouldn't have, might have, could have, can't have, must have, would have, needn't have, ought to have.
Exam link. B2 First Use of English Part 4 (key-word transformation) and Reading/Listening inference of past events.
60-minute session (FCE I). One grammar page, four exercises, full hour. Friday B2 teaches this in the class after l03–l04, at the start of their next 2-hour slot (paired with l06).
Materials: Student's Book p.60. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u05-l05.

1–2. Underline + match: Exercises 1–2 · 22 min

Materials: Student's Book p.60, Ex.1–2 (eight sentences with perfect modals).
Ex.1 (modal verbs to underline): 1 should have phoned · 2 shouldn't have tried · 3 might have missed · 4 could have become · 5 can't have cooked · 6 must have been · 7 would have bought · 8 needn't have told.
Ex.2 (match to explanation a–h): 1b · 2h · 3d · 4a · 5g · 6c · 7e · 8f.

3. Choose the option: Exercise 3 · 13 min

Materials: Student's Book p.60, Ex.3 (eight sentences, choose the correct option).
Answers: 1 could · 2 may · 3 could · 4 needn't · 5 could · 6 must · 7 shouldn't · 8 might.

4. Rewrite: Exercise 4 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.60, Ex.4 (rewrite eight sentences with the words in brackets).
Answers:
1 She should have finished the experiment by now.
2 They might have missed the bus.
3 I would have told you how to use the device, but you didn't ask me.
4 They ought to have updated their anti-virus software.
5 It can't have been easy to learn to use that app.
6 You shouldn't have given him another computer game. He's got loads already!
7 You needn't have paid for that software.
8 The first people on the moon must have been both excited and terrified.

Valencia link: ask the group to make one true sentence with a perfect modal about something they now regret not doing during Las Fallas this year (e.g. "I should have gone to see the mascletà on the first day").

Fast finisher

Write two more sentences about the Neil Armstrong moon-landing photo on p.60, one with must have and one with can't have.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 3 can be reduced to five items if Ex.1–2 ran long. Do not cut Ex.4: the eight transformations are the exact exam-task format this grammar point is tested in.
