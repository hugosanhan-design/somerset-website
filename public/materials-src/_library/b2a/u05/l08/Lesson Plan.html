1. Study-tech tips: Exercises 1–2 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.64, Ex.1–2 (Top Tech Tips infographic: A work in the cloud, B use planning apps, C phone as reading device, D watch/read in English).

Pairs discuss which technology they use for taking notes, writing essays, revising and presenting, then which of the four tech tips (A–D) they already follow and which they think are most useful. Open discussion, no fixed answer.

Valencia link: ask which tip would help most with a very local problem — unreliable wifi during a power cut in a storm (common in autumn in the Comunitat Valenciana): tip A, cloud backup, is the direct answer.

2. Mind your Mind: Exercises 3–4 · 8 min

Materials: Student's Book p.64, Mind your Mind box + Ex.3–4.

Read the two tips (be kind online; be aware of screen time). Discuss: is the internet generally a positive place? Do you set your own screen-time limits?

3. Unit review: Review p.153 · 17 min

Materials: Student's Book Review p.153 (printed p.81 in this Split A edition), Ex.1–6.
Vocabulary Ex.1: 1 browsing, bookmark · 2 database, undo · 3 attachment, display · 4 access, upgrade.
Vocabulary Ex.2: 1 key · 2 speak · 3 do · 4 hold.
Vocabulary Ex.3: 1 at · 2 to · 3 with · 4 for · 5 at · 6 with · 7 in.
Grammar Ex.4: 1 Could · 2 ought · 3 supposed · 4 mustn't · 5 allowed · 6 must.
Grammar Ex.5 (correct the mistake): 1 succeeded in building (not "to build") · 2 manage to get (not "getting") · 3 was able to open (not "could open", for a single past achievement) · 4 capable of taking (not "capable to take").
Grammar Ex.6: 1 should have asked · 2 must have been · 3 could have applied · 4 needn't have bought · 5 can't have seen.

4. Unit play · 15 min

Materials: groups/2026-27/fce1/plays/Unit5_EmergingTechnologies_Play.html (existing file, reused as-is, not rebuilt).

Students take character roles and read through the play, which reviews Unit 5's vocabulary (technology, compound nouns) and grammar (modals of permission/obligation/ability/probability, perfect modals) in a Valencia setting.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write one more Review-style sentence of their own for any grammar point in Ex.4–6 that they still find tricky.

If short of time, cut:

The unit play (Block 4) can be shortened to one of its scripts rather than all three if the Review page (Block 3) ran long. Do not cut the Review exercises: they are the most efficient whole-unit consolidation before the test.
