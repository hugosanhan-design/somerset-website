1. Phrasal verbs: Exercises 1–2 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.61, Ex.1–2 (match seven phrasal verbs to definitions a–g, then discuss).
Answers: 1f (be taken in → be fooled or cheated by someone) · 2d (come up → appear on a screen) · 3b (do without → not use) · 4g (key in → put information into a computer or machine) · 5a (speak up → talk more loudly) · 6c (get hold of → find) · 7e (watch out for → pay close attention to something to avoid anything bad happening).

2. Prepositions: Exercise 3 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.61, Ex.3 (choose the correct preposition in seven sentences).
Answers: 1 with (equipped with) · 2 at (at risk) · 3 with (experimenting with) · 4 at, for (staring at … bad for) · 5 to (responding to) · 6 in (result in) · 7 for (arrange for).

3–4. Exam Tip + Exam Task: Exercise 4 · 23 min

Materials: Student's Book p.61, Exam Tip (noticing grammar structures) + Exam Task (open cloze, 8 gaps, text about tiredness after video calls).

Read the Exam Tip: some gaps need a whole grammar structure (will have been, could have done), made of several words — look around the gap before deciding. Then complete all 8 gaps, one word each.

Answers: 1 have (may have noticed) · 2 in (plug in) · 3 should (should have tidied) · 4 out (watch out for) · 5 for (competing for attention) · 6 any / much (show any/much emotion) · 7 out (work out) · 8 at (staring at you).

Valencia link: ask students whether video-call tiredness ("Zoom fatigue") has changed how their own online Spanish/English classes are taught — a genuine discussion link to their own experience of remote classes during and after the pandemic.

Fast finisher

Fast finishers write one original sentence for each of the seven phrasal verbs from Ex.1, about their own use of technology.

If short of time, cut:

Exercise 3 (prepositions) can be reduced to five sentences if Ex.1–2 ran long. Do not cut the Exam Tip: recognising multi-word structures is the single most useful open-cloze strategy in the whole unit.
