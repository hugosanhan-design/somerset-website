Exercise 1 · formal or informal?

Read the tone

  • 1It is with regret that we cannot attend the event. — F
  • 2Hope to hear from you soon. — I
  • 3However, spending more money is not recommended. — F
  • 4Oh well, you can't have everything! — I
  • 5Anyway, how about visiting us? — I
  • 6Unfortunately, it seems that all the tickets have been sold. — F
  • 7I am very grateful for your assistance. — F
  • 8I'm a bit stressed at the moment. — I
  • 9It was great to hear from you! — I
  • 10Got any advice for me? — I
Look for the marker in each: full forms and formal vocabulary (regret, recommended, unfortunately, grateful, assistance) signal formal; contractions, ellipsis and chatty connectors (oh well, anyway, got any) signal informal.
Exercises 4–5 · reading Emma's reply

Which paragraphs do what?

  • 4.1Paragraphs that answer the question → the 2nd ("As for your friend David...") and 3rd ("If I were you...")
  • 4.2Paragraphs that are more about being friendly → the 1st and the last
  • 5Three phrases for giving advice → I don't think you should… / If I were you, I'd… / Why don't you…?
A good informal reply always has both: paragraphs that genuinely answer every question asked, and paragraphs that build rapport. Both count toward Content and Communicative Achievement.
Exercise 6 · complete Amy's email

One word, each gap

1Hi Sam,

2Thanks for your email. It was really great to 3hear from you.

I'm so 4sorry to hear that you've fallen out with your best friend over something that wasn't your fault! It 5sounds awful and it 6must be really annoying for you.

If I 7were you, I wouldn't give up on your friend. Why 8don't you wait a few days for everyone to chill out a bit and then try to talk? You could 9even invite your friend over to your house for a peace-keeping meeting!

Anyway, I 10hope that helps. Let me 11know how you get on.

All the 12best,
Amy
Fast finisher: underline the phrasal verbs in this email — fall out, give up on, chill out, invite over, get on. All five are exactly the phrasal verbs from l06's Use your English page.
