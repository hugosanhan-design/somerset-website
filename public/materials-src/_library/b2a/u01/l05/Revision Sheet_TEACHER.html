FCE I · Unit 1 · Grammar p12

Teacher key: present perfect revision

Student sheet is 1 A4 sheet, double-sided. Source: New Close-up B2, p12.
1Circle
  1. has been studying (activity, how long)
  2. have read (how many)
  3. have you known (stative verb)
  4. has been raining (recent activity, evidence)
  5. haven't finished (result)
  6. have visited (first time = simple)
  7. has broken (completed result)
  8. have been cooking (activity, evidence)
2Complete
  1. Have / eaten
  2. has been waiting
  3. haven't finished
  4. have / been learning
  5. has lost
  6. have known
3Adverbs
  1. She hasn't replied to my message yet.
  2. I still haven't bought the tickets.
  3. I've already seen this film. (or: I've seen this film already.)
  4. It's the best paella I've ever eaten.
  5. I've been so tired lately. (or: Lately I've been so tired.)
  6. The bus has just arrived.
4Mistakes
  1. I have lived / have been living in Valencia since 2019. (Spanish: vivo desde)
  2. She has been to Rome three times. (gone = still there)
  3. I saw him yesterday. (finished time word = past simple)
  4. How long have you been studying / have you studied English?
  5. I have known her for years. (know is stative)
5. Open answer. Check the form: I have never + past participle; I have been + -ing. Item 3 of exercise 1 and item 5 of exercise 4 are the two most common Spanish-speaker traps: stative verbs and a finished time word.
