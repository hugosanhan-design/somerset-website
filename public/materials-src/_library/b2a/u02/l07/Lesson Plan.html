1–3. Learning Focus, model, practice · 22 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.26-27, Ex.1–7.

Learning Focus box: paragraphs (usually five: intro + one per point + conclusion, but not a rigid rule); topic sentences (introduce the paragraph's main idea, usually but not always first); strong introduction and conclusion, using set phrases.

Ex.7 answers (linking words): 1 mind · 2 view · 3 Apart · 4 addition · 5 Personally.
Ex.4/5 notes: extra idea in the model — cultural awareness (it improves your cultural knowledge). Every paragraph's first sentence is its topic sentence, introducing that paragraph's main idea.

4–5. The real Exam Task, written in class · 33 min

Materials: Exam Tip ("Planning before you write"), Exam Task (p.27).

Task: "Some students think it's better to study abroad. Do you agree?" Write about: (1) studying in a different language, (2) experiencing a different culture, (3) your own idea. 140–190 words.

This is written for real, individually, in silence, in class — never set as homework. Give 2 minutes for planning (choose the third idea, jot one sentence per paragraph) before writing starts. Collect every script at the end; do not return or mark in front of students today — that happens before l09.

Valencia link: a strong "your own idea" option many students will reach for naturally — studying abroad forces you to navigate a new city and its customs, the way an exchange student in Valencia has to learn Fallas etiquette, siesta-adjusted opening hours, or how the Turia riverbed park works as a shared public space.
