Exam Task · which section?

Match the questions to A–D

  • 1Finding a fashion object that is extremely old → A (oldest bead, 13,000 years)
  • 2An object used to pay for a specific event → D (earring paid for a funeral)
  • 3A fashion item that's been beyond our planet → B (plaid went to the Moon)
  • 4An event that happens once a year → C (Hola Mohalla, each spring)
  • 5A fashion that used to come with something to help the wearer stay safe → D (wax on earrings, before firing a cannon)
  • 6An attempt to stop someone copying things → A (Maasai trademarking their bead art)
  • 7Wearing something as a record of an achievement → D (sailors' earrings, crossing the equator)
  • 8People unaware of a fashion's origins → C (women in New York might not know)
  • 9Older/more important people wear larger items → C (Xhosa head wrap size reflects age)
  • 10Something mainly worn for special occasions in its home country → B (kilts, mostly weddings/New Year)
Almost-right trap: Q5 tempts students toward section A (pirates hanging wax on earrings appears there in some drafts of the reasoning) but the actual detail — wax protecting hearing before firing a cannon — is in section D, alongside the funeral-payment detail. Read the whole sentence, not just the keyword "earring".
Vocabulary Exercise 1 · definitions

Match the bold words

  • 1actions/beliefs followed for a long time → tradition
  • 2a set of actions performed regularly → ritual
  • 3positions/purposes people have → roles
  • 4something exciting to watch → spectacle
  • 5the beginning of something → origins
  • 6parties/social activities for a special occasion → festivities
  • 7a very old story, maybe not true → legend
  • 8a formal event on special occasions → ceremony
  • 9clothes worn on special occasions → costume
  • 10things worn to hide the face → masks
  • 11walking/riding to show what you're wearing → parade
  • 12people who watch but don't take part → spectators
All from "Old Man Winter": the Slovenian Pust festival article on p.20, about masked figures chasing away "Old Man Winter" to clear the way for spring.
Vocabulary Exercise 3 · discuss the words

What do these words mean?

  • 1witness → someone who sees an event
  • 2reception → a formal event (e.g. a meal) to celebrate something, like a wedding
  • 3ancestors → relatives from the distant past
  • 4bonds → connections or links between people
  • 5speech → talking in front of an audience
Discuss, don't just define: do you agree that festivals strengthen "bonds" between people in the same society?
