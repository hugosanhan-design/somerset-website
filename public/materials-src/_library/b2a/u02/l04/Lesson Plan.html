New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 2 · Global culture? · SB p.22-23
FCE I: Tuesday · Friday B2: Friday (with l03)
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can predict what type of information is missing in a sentence-completion listening task and complete it accurately; and can carry out a two-minute collaborative speaking task, using question tags and turn-taking language.
Target language. Listening sub-skill: predicting content by word type (noun/verb/adjective/number). Speaking: collaborating, encouraging a partner to speak, question tags.
Exam link. Listening Part 2-style sentence completion (Track 2.2/2.3); Speaking Part 3-style collaborative task (Track 2.4).
60-minute session (FCE I). Both pages fit in one hour if the speaking task is kept to its stated 2+1 minutes per pair, not stretched. Friday B2 pairs this with l03 (Grammar p.21) in their 2-hour slot; run l03 first, then this plan.
Materials: Student's Book pp.22-23. Audio: Track 2.2, 2.3 (listening, museum island Berlin), Track 2.4 (speaking model). Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u02-l04.

2–5. Listening: Exercises 1–5 · 27 min

Materials: Student's Book p.22, Track 2.2 (short extract) and Track 2.3 (full Exam Task, "The Long Night of the Museums").

Prediction is the actual skill being taught here — push students to say out loud what kind of word (noun, adjective, date, number, verb) is missing in each gap before they hear anything.

Ex.3 (Track 2.2): 1 cultural centre · 2 5 / five · 3 1930 · 4 was named.
Ex.5 (Track 2.3, Exam Task): 1 modern technology · 2 1997 · 3 (special) pass · 4 buses · 5 over 120 · 6 dance performances · 7 take part in · 8 City Nature · 9 White Nights · 10 10 / ten in the evening.

Valencia link: the "your ideas" prompt asks about events like the Long Night of the Museums locally — mention Valencia's own Nit de l'Art and La Nit dels Museus, when galleries and museums across the city open free, late, exactly like the Berlin event described.

6–8. Speaking: Exercises 1–4 · 20 min

Materials: Student's Book p.23, Track 2.4.

Ex.2 gapped task (one word per gap) then Ex.3 discussion of the Exam Tip (talking together, encouraging your partner, question tags, disagreeing politely). Ex.4 is the real collaborative task: 2 minutes on the five prompts (films/literature, local festivals/customs, food/cooking, clothes/jewellery/costumes, music/dance), then 1 minute deciding which matters most.

Ex.2 answers: 1 people · 2 culture · 3 theatre · 4 plays · 5 galleries · 6 modern · 7 popular · 8 festivals · 9 historical.

FCE I (3 students): run as a three, with the teacher timing and one student stepping in as "examiner" for a round if needed. Friday B2: standard pairs.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 8 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u02-l04.

Fast finisher

Write two more collaborating phrases of your own, in the style of the Useful Language box (e.g. "I hadn't thought of that, but...").

If short of time, cut:

Ex.1 lead-in discussions (both pages) can be shortened to one question each if time is tight. Do not cut the Exam Task itself on either page (Ex.5 listening, Ex.4 speaking) — both are marked, exam-format practice.
