2. Concept check: Exercises 1–2 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.21, Ex.1–2.

Six example sentences: underline past simple verbs, circle past continuous verbs. Then complete six rules with PS or PC.

Ex.1: Past simple — finished, jumped, raced, took, was, loved, wanted, stopped, didn't answer, was, heard. Past continuous — were watching, was eating, was shining, were singing, were chasing, were wearing.
Ex.2: a PS (series of completed events, one after another) · b PS (past habits/routines) · c PS (past states) · d PC...and the PS (interrupted/unfinished situation, and the event that interrupted it) · e PC (action in progress at a particular moment) · f PC...and PS (background information, and the main events).

3. used to / would: Exercise 3 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.21, Ex.3 (three sentences taken from the p.19 reading).

Look at sentences using used to wear, used to have, and would hang. Choose the correct option to complete four rules.

Answers: a were true for a long period of time · b no longer true · c used to / would · d action (not stative) verbs, when describing past habits.

Flag clearly: used to works with BOTH action and stative verbs (used to have, used to wear); would only works with ACTION verbs for habits (would hang), never with statives like have, like, be.

4. Practice: Exercise 4 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.21, Ex.4 (choose ALL correct options).
Answers:
1 a, b, d (celebrated / used to celebrate / would celebrate). Option c wrong: can't use past continuous for past routines.
2 a, b (was / used to be). Options c, d wrong: can't use past continuous or would for past states (being a rock guitarist).
3 a only (got). Options b, d wrong: can't use used to/would for single events. Option c (was getting) sounds strange — getting married is likely a main event, not background.

5. Gapped article: Exercise 5 · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.21, "As flat as a pancake?" (archaeology/pancake-history article, ten gaps).
Answers: 1 were examining · 2 found · 3 used to mix (would sounds unnatural before "probably"; past simple doesn't fit alongside the infinitives make/bake) · 4 would/used to add · 5 used to like · 6 used to/would describe · 7 were driving · 8 decided · 9 came · 10 scored.

Valencia link: ask the group: what did people in Valencia use to eat before some now-typical foods arrived (e.g. paella's rice was itself an import, brought via Al-Andalus centuries ago) — a real "used to" discussion parallel to the pancake article's point that flatbreads existed in every culture, long before any one country claimed them.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 8 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u02-l03.

Fast finisher

Write three true sentences about your own childhood: one with past simple (single event), one with used to (state or habit), one with would (repeated action only).
