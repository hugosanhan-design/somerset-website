Teacher key: past tenses, used to and would

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. was crossing (interrupted by 'went off')
  2. opened (series of finished events)
  3. was watching (interrupted by 'rang')
  4. used to go (were going is wrong for a repeated habit)
  5. used to be (state: would is not possible)
  6. would swim (repeated habit; was swimming is wrong)
  7. was cooking (in progress when guests arrived)
  8. didn't use to (after did/didn't the verb is 'use')
2 Complete
  1. went out / were watching
  2. took / posted
  3. were / doing
  4. didn't hear / was sleeping
  5. was walking / saw
  6. didn't go / was raining
3 U / W / B
  1. B · U (state: be) · U (state: have) · B · U (state: be)
4 Corrected
  1. I didn't use to like olives.
  2. Yesterday evening I went to the cinema with Carlos. (finished event)
  3. When I was small, I used to be / was very shy. (state; would is not possible)
  4. Jordi broke his arm while he was playing football. (interrupting event is past simple)
5 Your turn
  1. Open answers (used to + infinitive; was/were + -ing).
Typical errors: 'didn't used to'; 'used to going'; would with states; past continuous for a finished event ('I was going yesterday').
