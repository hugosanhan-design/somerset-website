Exam Tip · before the task

Eliminating "almost right" answers

  • Read the whole article quickly first — don't worry about understanding everything.
  • Underline the key words in each matching question before searching the text.
  • Each question matches only one section — other sections may look "almost right" but a specific detail won't fit.
  • Search for all sections that could match, then decide which is completely right.
