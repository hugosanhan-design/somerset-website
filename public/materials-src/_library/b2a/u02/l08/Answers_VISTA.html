Review · vocabulary

Whole-unit vocabulary recap

  • People → an ancestor, a spectator, a witness
  • Things to attend → a ceremony, a festival, a parade (a speech also possible)
  • Things to wear → a costume, a mask
  • Things to listen to → a speech
  • Phrasal verbs → dates back · talk into · identify with · spread out · go on/ahead
Review · grammar

Whole-unit grammar recap

  • 1 was cycling, fell, didn't hurt
  • 2 didn't answer, rang, was washing
  • 3 went, gave up, didn't have, wanted
  • Find the mistake → "used to go" should be went (a single occasion, "once"); "were visiting" should be used to / would visit; "would own" should be used to own (stative); "did you used" should be did you use to get up.
