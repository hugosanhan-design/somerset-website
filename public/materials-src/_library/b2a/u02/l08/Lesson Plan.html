2–3. The video: Exercises 2–3 · 20 min

Materials: Student's Book p.28, video "Intercultural skills for turbulent times". Answers checked against the printed SB answer key, never guessed from the audioscript/summary alone.
Ex.2 (which skills each speaker mentions): 1 Emma Dodwell-Groves — self-awareness · 2 Elmer Dixon — cross-cultural communication · 3 Patricia Coleman — courage · 4 Moudlay — understanding · 5 Ivonne van der Pul — openness, self-awareness · 6 Abby Beckley — education · 7 Pari Nemazie — openness.
Ex.3 (summary gaps): 1 bubble · 2 assumptions · 3 gaps · 4 divided · 5 shoes · 6 take · 7 bitter · 8 open.

Valencia link: "your ideas" (do you live in a bubble of similar people?) — ask directly whether an international student arriving in Valencia with no Spanish quickly ends up in an English-speaking "bubble," and what "putting yourself in someone else's shoes" would mean for helping them out of it.

5–6. Review p.150 (SB p.78) · 12 min

Materials: Review, Unit 2 page (vocabulary + grammar recap, printed on p.78 of this Split A edition; the official SB numbering is p.150).
Vocabulary Ex.1 (categories): People — an ancestor, a spectator, a witness. Things to attend — a ceremony, a festival, a parade (a speech also possible). Things to wear — a costume, a mask. Things to listen to — a speech.
Ex.2 (phrasal verbs): 1 back · 2 into · 3 with · 4 out · 5 on / ahead.
Ex.3 (matching): 1e 2a 3b 4f 5d 6c.
Grammar Ex.4: 1 was cycling, fell, didn't hurt · 2 didn't answer, rang, was washing · 3 went, gave up, didn't have, wanted.
Ex.5 (find the mistake): 1 "I used to go" (not "would go" — single occasion "once") · 2 "we used to visit / would visit" (not "were visiting") · 3 "My parents used to own / owned" (not "would own" — stative) · 4 "did you use to get up" (not "used").
Ex.6 (present perfect/past simple): 1 have been · 2 have put on · 3 joined · 4 put on · 5 have never played · 6 have always wanted · 7 played · 8 only said · 9 have just found out · 10 have chosen.
Ex.7 (used to/get used to): 1 get used to waking · 2 not used to running · 3 get used to not having.

7. Class play

Materials: groups/2026-27/fce1/plays/Unit2_GlobalCulture_Play.html (already built, reused as-is — do not rebuild).

Pick one of the play's three scripts (2/3-character toggle). Assign parts, read through once for meaning, then again with more expression. Covers the unit's vocabulary and grammar in a Valencia/Spain setting of its own.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 8 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u02-l08.

Samples across the whole unit (pp.17-28), since this is the review lesson — not retested content specifically, general consolidation before the Unit 2 test.

If short of time, cut:

The Review page's grammar recap (Block 6) is the first thing to shorten to a quick spot-check of 2–3 items rather than all seven exercises, since l10's test itself is the real, marked check. Never cut the video Exam Task (Ex.2–3): a real video-comprehension mark only comes from that.
