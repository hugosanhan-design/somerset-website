Revision: present perfect, past simple, used to

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Past simple: a finished time (last year, in 2019, ago, when I was ten). Present perfect: life so far, things that continue to now (for, since, ever, never, yet), or a result now.
be used to + noun / -ing = it is familiar. get used to + noun / -ing = it is becoming familiar.
1Circle the correct option.
  1. I (lived / have lived) in Valencia since 2015.
  2. Raquel (went / has gone) to Mestalla last Saturday.
  3. (Did you try / Have you tried) the new paella place yesterday?
  4. We (haven't finished / didn't finish) the exam yet.
  5. Paula (has lost / lost) her keys at the beach last week.
  6. How long (did you know / have you known) Gemma?
  7. Tomás (was / has been) to London three times.
  8. I (didn't see / haven't seen) Clara since last summer.
2Complete with the past simple or present perfect of the verb.
  1. Nuria (never / eat) sushi, but she (try) paella in the Albufera last week.
  2. Daniel (work) at the same bank since 2019.
  3. How many cups of horchata you (drink) today?
  4. Laia (finish) school in 2021 and (study) architecture since then.
