Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use seven culture/tradition-related phrasal verbs, seven collocation verbs (build/give/have/hold/know/play/use), and complete a full B2 First sentence-transformation Exam Task.
Target language. Phrasal verbs: date back, identify with, look back at, spread out over, call off, go ahead, talk into. Collocations: give a speech, play a role in, festival is held, build a reputation, have its roots in, use without permission, widely known as.
Exam link. Use of English Part 4, word-given transformations — today's Exam Task is the real exam format, six items.
60-minute session (FCE I). The transformation task (Ex.3) needs real time to think, not just check — don't rush to reveal. Friday B2 pairs this with l05 (Grammar p.24) in their 2-hour slot; run l05 first.
Materials: Student's Book p.25. Answers_VISTA.html. Somerset Arcade: b2a-u02-l06.

1. Phrasal verbs: Exercise 1 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.25, Ex.1 (seven sentences, seven meanings).
Answers: 1 f (dates back — exist since a particular time in the past) · 2 g (identify with — understand, feel the same as) · 3 e (look back at — remember, think about the past) · 4 c (spread out over — happening over a long period of time) · 5 b (called off — cancel) · 6 a (go ahead — start to do something) · 7 d (talked…into — persuade somebody to do something).

2. Collocations: Exercise 2 · 15 min

Materials: Student's Book p.25, Ex.2 (word box: build, give, have, hold, know, play, use).
Answers: 1 give (a speech) · 2 plays (an important role in) · 3 held (the festival is held) · 4 built (a reputation as) · 5 has (its roots in) · 6 using (traditional designs without permission) · 7 known (widely known as).

4. Exam Task: Exercise 3 · 19 min

Materials: Student's Book p.25, Exam Task (six sentence transformations, given words: WHILE, USED, ROLE, INTO, BACK, TO).
Answers:
1 I couldn't remember what to say while I was giving my speech.
2 I didn't / couldn't get used to my new way of life for a long time.
3 Storytelling plays an important role in the festival.
4 My friends talked me into taking part in the parade.
5 The tradition of wearing earrings dates back (over) 5,000 years.
6 I used to spend hours reading when I was younger.

Valencia link: ask the group to make one true sentence about Valencia using "dates back" — e.g. "the Fallas tradition dates back over a hundred years" or "the Silk Exchange (Llotja) dates back to the 15th century."

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 8 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u02-l06.

Fast finisher

Write one more true sentence for three of today's phrasal verbs (dates back, identify with, talked…into) about your own life.
