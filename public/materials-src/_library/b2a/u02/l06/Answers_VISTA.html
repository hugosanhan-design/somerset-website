Exercise 1 · phrasal verbs

Match to the meaning

  • 1dates back → f, exist since a particular time in the past
  • 2identify with → g, understand/feel the same as
  • 3look back at → e, remember, think about the past
  • 4spread out over → c, happening over a long period
  • 5called off → b, cancel
  • 6go ahead → a, start to do something
  • 7talked…into → d, persuade somebody to do something

Exam Task · sentence transformation

Complete the second sentence

  • 1WHILE → I couldn't remember what to say while I was giving my speech.
  • 2USED → I didn't / couldn't get used to my new way of life for a long time.
  • 3ROLE → Storytelling plays an important role in the festival.
  • 4INTO → My friends talked me into taking part in the parade.
  • 5BACK → The tradition of wearing earrings dates back (over) 5,000 years.
  • 6TO → I used to spend hours reading when I was younger.
Exam Tip reminder: tense usually matches the original, but not always; both contractions and full forms are correct; you must use between 2 and 5 words including the given word.
