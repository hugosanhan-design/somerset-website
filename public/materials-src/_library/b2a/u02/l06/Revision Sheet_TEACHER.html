FCE I · Unit 2 · Use your English p25

Teacher key: phrasal verbs and transformations

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Match
  1. call off d · identify with f · date back a · look back at c · talk into b · go ahead e
2 Collocations
  1. give / plays / held / known / built / using
3 Open cloze
  1. back (date back)
  2. from (from 15 to 19)
  3. of (one of the biggest)
  4. has (present perfect)
  5. into (talk someone into)
  6. used (get used to)
  7. who (non-defining relative pronoun)
  8. an (before the vowel in unforgettable)
4 Word types
  1. P, P, P, A, P, V, R, D
5 Transformations
  1. called off
  2. is not used to / isn't used to / has not got used to / hasn't got used to
  3. talked me into taking
  4. used to spend
Typical errors: 'known like' for 'known as'; 'used to spending' for 'used to spend'; forgetting the word given must appear in the answer. Item 6 (own sentence): open answer.
