Past perfect simple & continuous

Tap "Show answer" next to each question.

Exercises 1–2 · noticing the difference

PPS or PPC?

  • 1"I didn't go... because I had seen the film before." → PPS
  • 2"The contestants had been living in the house for six months..." → PPC
  • 3"Victor had just arrived at the restaurant..." → PPS
  • 4"...I could see that she had been crying." → PPC
  • 5Which describe a single completed action before another? → 1, 3
  • 6Which describes how long a situation lasted? → 2
  • 7Which describes a process with an immediate result? → 4
  • 8Which talks about the result of something completed some time ago? → 1
Rule: past perfect SIMPLE for completed actions/results; past perfect CONTINUOUS for duration or an activity with visible/immediate evidence (red eyes = had been crying).
Exercise 3 · the celebrity chef story

Choose the correct form

  • 1"I'd been sitting there for a few minutes when he came and sat next to me."
  • 2"He'd just been running (out of breath)."
  • 3"I realised I'd left my pen at home."
  • 4"The battery had run out."
  • 5"A friend had asked him to take her dog for a walk."
  • 6"He'd been looking for the dog for half an hour."
  • 7"It had gone home by itself."
  • 8"It had probably been waiting there the whole time."
Pattern: completed single actions (left, run out, asked, gone) take the simple form; ongoing/duration actions (been sitting, been running, been looking, been waiting) take the continuous form.
Exercise 4 · Valentina Tereshkova, the first woman in space

Complete the text

  • 1Yuri Gagarin had become the first man in space.
  • 2Valentina had volunteered to join her country's space programme.
  • 3By that time, she had been skydiving for two years.
  • 4She had done more than a hundred jumps.
  • 5Although she hadn't told her parents about her dangerous hobby.
  • 6Valentina had been training to travel into space for 15 months.
  • 7Local villagers had watched / had been watching her falling to Earth.
  • 8She had travelled around our planet 48 times.
  • 9When she had finished her mission, she became a role model.
Valencia/Spain link: Spain's own (so far only) astronaut, Pedro Duque, had been training for over a decade before his 1998 Space Shuttle mission — a real Spanish example of the same grammar point.
