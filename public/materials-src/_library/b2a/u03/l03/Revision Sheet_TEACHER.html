Teacher key: past perfect simple and continuous

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Circle
  1. 1 had just parked (single completed action)
  2. 2 had been growing (how long)
  3. 3 had been repairing (visible result)
  4. 4 had won
  5. 5 had been training (since six o'clock)
  6. 6 had left
2 Complete
  1. 1 had been shouting (all afternoon, visible result)
  2. 2 had lost
  3. 3 had been baking
  4. 4 sat
  5. 5 hadn't had
  6. 6 had never taken
3 Mistakes
  1. 1 had been living
  2. 2 had arrived
  3. 3 it had already started to rain
  4. 4 he had been running
  5. 5 had been waiting
4 True for you
  1. 1 past participle after had
  2. 2 -ing form after had been
Typical errors: 'had been + past participle' ('had been arrived'); 'had been + base form'; present perfect instead of past perfect ('has been running'); forgetting that already and just go between had and the participle. Spanish speakers often use the pretérito pluscuamperfecto for any earlier action: in English past simple is fine when the order is clear.
