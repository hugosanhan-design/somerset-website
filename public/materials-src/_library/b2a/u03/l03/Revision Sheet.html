Revision: past perfect simple and continuous

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Past perfect simple (had + past participle): a single action finished before another past moment: I had just arrived when she walked in.
Past perfect continuous (had been + -ing): how long a situation lasted before something happened, or an activity with a visible result: Her eyes were red: she had been crying.
1Circle the correct form.
  1. Jordi (had just parked / had just been parking) his car when the singer came out.
  2. The queue (had been growing / had grown) for hours when the shop finally opened.
  3. Her hands were black with oil: she (had repaired / had been repairing) the bike all morning.
  4. Clara (had won / had been winning) the prize before she turned twenty.
  5. Eva was exhausted because she (had been training / had trained) in the heat of El Cabanyal since six o'clock.
  6. When I opened my bag, I realised I (had left / had been leaving) my phone on the Malvarrosa beach.
