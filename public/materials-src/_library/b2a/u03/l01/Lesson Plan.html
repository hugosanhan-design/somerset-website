1. Unit 2 test results · 15 min

Materials: the Unit 2 results PowerPoint (cloned .pptx, _method/level-ladder-results-scripts/), in this date's materials folder; it is built after the test is marked.

Before class: mark the test, send the marked scans to Claude, who builds the results PowerPoint by cloning the latest FCE I Level Ladder deck (per-question breakdown, anonymised A/B/C cards).

2. Opener p.29 · 5 min

Show the photo of the famous Cuban ballerina Alicia Alonso at the Gran Teatro de la Habana. Pairs discuss: what she was doing in the photo; whether there are respected older people in their own country; what we can learn from people much older than us.

Valencia link: ask if students know a Valencian figure widely respected in their own field regardless of age — e.g. a veteran Falla artist still designing monuments, or a long-serving paella chef in a village near Valencia known across the region.

3. Reading lead-in: Exercises 1–2 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.30, Ex.1–2.

Students match seven bold words (role models, potential, mission, impact, motivate, inspire, expectations) to their definitions, then discuss in pairs whether they agree with each of the seven statements and why, using real examples.

Ex.1 answers: 1 b (role models) · 2 e (potential) · 3 f (mission) · 4 c (impact) · 5 g (motivate) · 6 a (inspire) · 7 d (expectations).

4. Exercise 3: predicting · 6 min

Show the photo of Mira Rai training in Chamonix, France (p.31). Pairs discuss: what does trail running involve (long-distance running across hills/mountains, often for many hours); in what ways might she be a role model? Don't confirm yet — the article checks this.

5. Exercises 4–5: first read · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.31, "Mira Rai – a trail-racing phenomenon" (Track 3.1 available for a read-along if useful, not required today).

Students read the article quickly to check their Ex.3 predictions (a Nepali trail-running world champion who grew up doing hard physical work in a remote village, and now uses her fame to support other young women). Then go through the Exam Tip together and underline referring words (she, this, her mission, etc.) in sentences A–G before attempting the task.

6. Exercise 6: start the Exam Task · 7 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.30-31, Exam Task (sentences A–G into gaps 1–6, one extra sentence).

Students start matching sentences A–G to gaps 1–6 in pairs, using the underlined referring words as evidence. Don't reveal the key today — l02 finishes and marks this task in full.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 3 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l01.

FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write two sentences of your own using "role model" and "make an impact" together, about someone real you admire.

If short of time, cut:

Ex.2's pair discussion of the seven statements can be shortened to two or three statements chosen by the teacher if the results deck ran long. Do not cut the first read (Block 5) or starting the exam task (Block 6) — l02 needs somewhere to pick up from.
