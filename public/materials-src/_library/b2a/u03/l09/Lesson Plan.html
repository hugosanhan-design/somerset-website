The six categories (this unit's own content)

  1. Story-writing conventions — not making it clear the story is fiction, or stopping in the middle of the action instead of a clear ending.
  2. Past perfect simple vs continuous — this unit's grammar point; mixing up a completed action with an ongoing one, e.g. using "had gone" where "had been going" (duration) was needed, or vice versa.
  3. Missing a required element — the task asked for a celebrity AND a mistake; a story missing either loses Content marks even if the language is good.
  4. Weak or cliché ending — "it was all a dream", or simply stopping without resolving the problem set up earlier in the story.
  5. Word count — target is 140–190 words; noticeably short stories usually mean under-developed content.
  6. Spelling & British English — standard spelling check, British forms throughout.

Grammar-not-yet-taught check

If a student's error involves a grammar point not yet covered this year (check groups/2026-27/fce1/2026-27_COURSE_PLAN.md or the Friday B2 equivalent), do not drill it now — name on their sheet when it will be taught and tell them to hold the question until then.
