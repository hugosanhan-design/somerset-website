Get your marked Unit 3 story back out. For each category below: if the teacher marked this kind of mistake in your writing, copy your sentence, fix it, then write ONE new sentence of your own using the same fix. If you didn't make this mistake, write "n/a" and move on — you don't need to invent an error you didn't make.

1. Story conventions — a clear beginning, middle and end

Reminder: your story must clearly be fiction, with a proper ending that solves the problem — never just stop, and avoid "it was all a dream".

2. Past perfect simple vs continuous

Reminder: had been + -ing for duration or a process with a result; had + past participle for a single completed action.

3. Missing a required element (a celebrity / a mistake)

Reminder: check both required elements against your plan before you start writing.

4. Weak or predictable endings

Reminder: use a concluding phrase (Finally..., At long last..., In the end...) and a genuine result, ideally with a small surprise.

5. Word count (target: 140-190 words)

Reminder: plan four paragraphs so the length comes naturally, rather than padding a finished draft.

6. Spelling & British English

Reminder: British spelling throughout — colour, favourite, realise, organise.
