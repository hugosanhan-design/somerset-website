New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 3 · Role models · SB p.37
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can use eight celebrity-related phrasal verbs (get away with, live up to, catch up on, come up with, look up to, miss out on, look down on, make up for) and five fixed expressions, and can complete a B2 First open-cloze task, thinking about missing grammar words, fixed expressions and phrasal verbs.
Target language. Phrasal verbs and fixed expressions about fame and role models.
Exam link. Use of English Part 2-style open cloze (today's exam task, marked).
Materials: Student's Book p.37. Answers_VISTA.html (full 3-exercise key). Somerset Arcade: b2a-u03-l06.

1. Phrasal verbs: Exercise 1 · 18 min

Materials: Student's Book p.37, eight sentences.
Answers: 1 get away with → h (not be punished) · 2 live up to → e (be as good as) · 3 catch up on → c (find out what's been happening) · 4 come up with → f (think of) · 5 look up to → b (respect and admire) · 6 missed out on → d (not have the opportunity to enjoy something) · 7 look down on → a (think you are better than someone else) · 8 make up for → g (when something good replaces something bad).

2. Expressions: Exercise 2 · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.37, five sentence halves.
Answers: 1 draw → d (attention to herself) · 2 led → c (the way in promoting women's rights — Mira Rai) · 3 lost → e (my passion for science) · 4 see → a (the funny side) · 5 set → b (a good example for others to follow).

3–4. Exam Tip + Exercise 3: open cloze · 25 min

Materials: Student's Book p.37, 8-gap text about a nurse called Eva.

Go through the Exam Tip: missing words are often small grammar words (prepositions, parts of fixed structures) or part of a phrasal verb/collocation from today's lesson. Students complete the Exam Task individually, then compare in pairs before the class check.

Answers: 1 up · 2 name · 3 been · 4 by · 5 for · 6 soon · 7 had · 8 follow.

Valencia link: ask students to name a real nurse, teacher or emergency worker from their own town near Valencia who they look up to, the way the text's writer looks up to Eva — not a celebrity, just someone who inspired them.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 4 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l06.

FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write two original sentences using "look up to" and "make up for" together, about someone real.

If short of time, cut:

The closing "your ideas" prompt (Block 5) can be dropped. Do not cut the open cloze Exam Task (Block 4) — it's directly marked, examinable practice.
