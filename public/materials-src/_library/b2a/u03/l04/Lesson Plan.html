New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 3 · Role models · SB p.34-35
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can complete a B2 First Listening multiple-matching task (scanning statements while listening) and can compare two photos in a structured way for the Speaking Part 2 task, using comparison language.
Target language. Making comparisons: "Both photos show...", "While the first..., the second...", "One thing they both have in common is...".
Exam link. Listening Part 4-style multiple matching; Speaking Part 2 comparing photos.
Materials: Student's Book p.34-35. Track 3.2 (listening exam task) and Track 3.3 (speaking model). Answers_VISTA.html (full key + audioscript excerpts). Somerset Arcade: b2a-u03-l04.

1–3. Listening lead-in and Exam Task · 24 min

Materials: Student's Book p.34, Track 3.2. Photo of a modern train station (Liège-Guillemins, Belgium).

Students discuss meeting someone famous, then underline key words in the four statements before reading the architect anecdote and choosing the best fit (statement 4: the meeting changed how the speaker felt about something they loved — they lost their passion for architecture). Then play Track 3.2 twice for the real Exam Task: five speakers, eight possible statements, three unused.

Answers: Speaker 1 = E · Speaker 2 = G · Speaker 3 = A · Speaker 4 = F · Speaker 5 = D (statements B, C, H are the three unused options).

The full audioscript for all five speakers is quoted on Answers_VISTA.html so the teacher can justify each answer live if students push back on a choice.

4–6. Speaking: comparing photos · 32 min

Materials: Student's Book p.35, photos a/b (media photographing celebrities); Track 3.3; Exam Task photos on pp.93-94.

Ex.1: pairs discuss the two photos (red-carpet interview vs paparazzi at a car window) — similarities, how the people might feel, would they want to be in either situation. Ex.2–3: play Track 3.3 (Eva and Carl's model answer); check they compared rather than just described, and used about half their time on each part. Ex.4 Exam Task: Student A compares photos of people learning skills (Task 1) and gives a follow-up answer about working alone (Task 2); Student B compares photos of people working together (Task 2) and answers whether they admire people who learn difficult skills (Task 1).

Valencia link: use a real local example for the follow-up question — would you admire more a person who taught themselves flamenco guitar, or someone from the Valencian Community who learned traditional bunyols-making from a grandmother?

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 4 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l04.

FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write two more "Useful Language" comparison sentences of your own about the two speaking photos, using phrases not already used in the pair discussion.

If short of time, cut:

Speaking Ex.1's open discussion (Block 4) can be shortened to two quick questions. Do not cut the Listening Exam Task (Block 3) or the Speaking Exam Task itself (Block 6) — both are marked, examinable practice.
