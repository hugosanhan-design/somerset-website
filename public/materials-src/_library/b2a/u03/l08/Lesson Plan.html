2. Exercises 2–3: the Four Boxes · 13 min

Materials: Student's Book p.40, "Successful time management" box + nine activities.

Students sort activities 1–9 into boxes A (urgent + important), B (not urgent + important), C (urgent-feeling but not important) or D (not urgent + not important). The book gives no fixed answer key for this exercise — it's a judgement task — so use the suggested classification on Answers_VISTA.html as a discussion-starter, not a "correct answer" to dictate.

Then Ex.3: discuss whether Box C/D activities have any real benefit, and whether students should stop doing them completely (probably not entirely — some rest/social activity is healthy; the problem is only doing too much of it).

3. Exercise 4: Mind your Mind · 10 min

Materials: Student's Book p.40, "Procrastination" info box.

Read the three bullet points together: procrastination = delaying something important because you don't feel like doing it now; it often makes us feel worse (still stressed + guilty); beating it means dealing with the negative emotions (stop feeling guilty, think of something positive, give yourself a reward when done).

Valencia link: ask if "voy a hacerlo mañana" (a very common Spanish procrastination phrase) has an English equivalent students already use — "I'll do it tomorrow" — and whether it's said more in Spain than students think is normal elsewhere.

Somerset Arcade: whole-unit Point Grab · 7 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l08.

Covers success/fame vocabulary, past perfect grammar, and the Mira Rai reading text from across the whole unit. FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write your own "Box B" activity you've been putting off, and one sentence saying how you'll start it this week, using "I've been meaning to..." or "I've been putting off...".

If short of time, cut:

The Four Boxes discussion (Block 2) can be shortened to 3–4 activities instead of all nine. Do not cut the Unit Review (Block 5) — it's the group's last full check of Unit 3 grammar and vocabulary before l09/l10.
