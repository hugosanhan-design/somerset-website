Revision: past simple and past perfect in stories

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Story order: you do not have to tell events in the order they happened.
Past simple for the main events; past perfect (simple or continuous) for what happened before them.
After after, before, until, as soon as you can often choose past simple or past perfect: the meaning is almost the same.
1Circle the correct verb forms.
  1. Irene (opened / had opened) the letter and (felt / had felt) sick: she (failed / had failed) her driving test again.
  2. Adrián (took / had taken) a taxi to the Albufera because he (missed / had missed) the last bus.
  3. When we arrived at El Saler, the sand (was / had been) wet because it (rained / had been raining) all night.
2Complete with the correct form of the verb.Sometimes two answers are possible.
  1. Daniel (leave) the café before the storm began.
  2. After the rain (stop), we walked along the Turia.
  3. Pablo was exhausted because he (work) in the restaurant since six that morning.
  4. Laia was upset because someone (break) her guitar.
  5. Irene opened the door, (see) the mess and (call) her landlord.
  6. My phone was dead because I (forget) to charge it.
