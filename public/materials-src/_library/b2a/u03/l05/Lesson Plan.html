Lesson Plan · b2a-u03-l05
New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 3 · Role models · SB p.36
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can choose correctly between the past simple, past perfect simple and past perfect continuous when narrating a sequence of past events, including after time expressions like "after" and "by the time".
Target language. Past simple for main story events in order; past perfect (simple/continuous) for things that happened earlier; "after"/"before" with no meaning difference between past simple and past perfect.
Exam link. Directly feeds the Unit 3 Writing task (a story, pp.38-39) which requires exactly this range of past tenses.
Materials: Student's Book p.36 (+ grammar reference 3.2, p.87). Answers_VISTA.html (full 4-exercise key). Somerset Arcade: b2a-u03-l05.

1. Exercise 1: the rules · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.36, three example sentences (uncle's award, photographers leaving, party arrivals).

Students choose the correct option to complete each rule.

Answers: a don't have to (tell events in the same order they happened) · b before (past perfect for things that happened before the main events) · c no important (difference in meaning after time expressions like after/before/until).

2. Exercise 2: four mini-texts · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.36, texts 1–4 (Harry's passport, chocolates, exhausted after carrying boxes, the airport).
Answers: 1 spoke, hadn't found, had been looking, had last seen · 2 picked up, opened, was, had eaten · 3 felt, had been carrying, needed, sat, watched · 4 arrived, ran, were, had already taken off.

3. Exercise 3: gap-fill sentences · 12 min

Materials: Student's Book p.36, six sentences (nurse, house, homework, cinema, violin, Lucy).
Answers: 1 had finished · 2 entered / had entered · 3 had cleaned · 4 arrived · 5 had been playing · 6 was.

Point out items 2 and 4 accept the past simple too, since "as soon as" and "by the time" already make the order of events clear (Ex.1's rule c).

4. Exercise 4: "How Flamingo Bob became a local celebrity" · 18 min

Materials: Student's Book p.36, text about a rescued flamingo in Curaçao (verbs to put into the correct form: crash, notice, clearly be, suffer, rescue, recover, become, never think, die, become, decide, fish).
Answers: 1 had crashed · 2 noticed · 3 had clearly been · 4 had been suffering · 5 had rescued · 6 had recovered (also possible: recovered) · 7 became · 8 had never thought · 9 died · 10 had become (also possible: became) · 11 decided · 12 had been fishing.

Valencia link: the Albufera natural park near Valencia is home to real wild flamingos — ask if anyone has seen them there, a good bridge into a "have you ever seen an injured or rescued animal" discussion.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 4 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l05.

FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write a 3-sentence mini-story using the past simple for the main events and the past perfect (simple or continuous) at least once for something that happened earlier.

If short of time, cut:

The closing "your ideas" prompt (Block 5) can be dropped. Do not cut Exercise 4 (Block 4) — it's the fullest connected-text practice and prepares directly for the Unit 3 story-writing task.
