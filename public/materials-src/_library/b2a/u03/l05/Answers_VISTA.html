Past simple, past perfect simple & continuous

Tap "Show answer" next to each question.

Exercise 1 · the rules

How do we tell a story?

  • aWhen telling a story, you don't have to tell events in the order they happened.
  • bUse the past simple for main events, and the past perfect for things that happened before them.
  • cAfter time expressions like after/before/until, past simple and past perfect make no important difference in meaning.
Exercise 2 · four mini-texts

Choose the correct form

  • 1Harry's passport: spoke, hadn't found, had been looking, had last seen
  • 2The chocolates: picked up, opened, was, had eaten
  • 3Exhausted from carrying boxes: felt, had been carrying, needed, sat, watched
  • 4At the airport: arrived, ran, were, had already taken off
Exercise 3 · complete the sentences

Nurse, house, homework, cinema, violin, Lucy

  • 1I knew I wanted to be a nurse before I had finished school.
  • 2As soon as she entered / had entered the house, she realised something was wrong.
  • 3I wasn't allowed to leave until I had cleaned my room.
  • 4By the time I arrived at the cinema, the film had already started.
  • 5My hand was in agony after I had been playing the violin for an hour.
  • 6When I was with Lucy, we had a long chat.
Note: items 2 and 4 also accept the past simple ("entered", already there for 4) — "as soon as" and "by the time" already make the order of events clear.
Exercise 4 · "How Flamingo Bob became a local celebrity"

The rescued flamingo

  • 1It had crashed into a hotel window.
  • 2Odette noticed the flamingo was confident around people.
  • 3It had clearly been kept as a pet or in a zoo.
  • 4It had been suffering from a bad foot.
  • 5...all the other animals that she had rescued over the years.
  • 6After the bird had recovered (also possible: recovered) from its injuries.
  • 7The flamingo soon became a local celebrity.
  • 8She had never thought about a name before.
  • 9Another flamingo died after getting tangled in fishing line.
  • 10...after it had become (also possible: became) tangled.
  • 11Odette decided to bring the line to a school.
  • 12...because someone had been fishing and had left the line out.
