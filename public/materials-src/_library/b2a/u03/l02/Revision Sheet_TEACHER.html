FCE I · Unit 3 · Vocabulary p32

Teacher key: success and fame

Student sheet is one A4 sheet, double-sided.
1 Match
  1. 1 gossip: d; 2 rumour: f; 3 scandal: a; 4 breakthrough: e; 5 comeback: c; 6 household name: b
2 Circle
  1. 1 overcame (coped needs 'with'); 2 cope; 3 rumour; 4 gossip (uncountable); 5 disgrace (in disgrace); 6 scandal; 7 breakthrough; 8 comeback
3 Idioms
  1. 1 footsteps; 2 nowhere; 3 doors; 4 fame; 5 chance; 6 anywhere (never got anywhere); 7 secret
4 Word forms
  1. 1 ambitious; 2 determination; 3 failure (failures also accepted); 4 sponsor; 5 inspirational (inspiring accepted); 6 realistic; 7 succeed
5 Own sentence
  1. Check that overcome has no preposition, or household name is a noun phrase.
Typical errors: 'cope the pressure' (needs with); 'a gossip' / 'gossips' for the uncountable noun; confusing rumour and gossip; 'realist' or 'realistical'; 'succes' (one s too few) and 'sucess'.
