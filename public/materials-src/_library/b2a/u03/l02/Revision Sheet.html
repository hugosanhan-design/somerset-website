Revision: success and fame

About 15 minutes. Do it in pencil, then check with your teacher.
Remember
cope with something, but overcome something (no preposition).
rumour: a story that may not be true. gossip: talk about people's private lives (uncountable).
scandal: a shocking event. in disgrace: having lost other people's respect.
