New Close-up B2 Split A, Unit 3 · Role models · SB p.30-32
Aims. By the end of the lesson, students can complete a full B2 First Reading sentence-matching task with reasonable accuracy, and can use ten success-and-fame vocabulary items from p.32 (overcome, cope, rumour, gossip, scandal, disgrace, breakthrough, comeback, plus five word-formation families: ambition/ambitious, determination/determined, encouragement/encourage, failure/fail, fame/famous, influence/influential, inspiration/inspirational, reality/real, sponsorship/sponsor, success/successful).
Target language. Success-and-fame vocabulary and word formation (p.32).
Exam link. Reading Part 7-style sentence matching (today's exam task, marked); word-formation groundwork for the Use of English open-cloze/word-formation tasks later in the unit.
60-minute session (FCE I). This finishes what l01 started, so recap fast (2 min) rather than re-explaining the Exam Tip. Friday B2 runs this straight after l01 in the same 2-hour slot (their own plan covers the pairing).
Materials: Student's Book pp.30-32. Answers_VISTA.html (full exam task key + vocabulary). Somerset Arcade: b2a-u03-l02.

2. Finish the exam task: Exercises 6–7 · 22 min

Materials: Student's Book pp.30-31, Exam Task (6 gaps, 7 sentence options).

Students finish placing sentences A–G into gaps 1–6, then commit to final answers. Reveal on Answers_VISTA.html. Go through the unused sentence C together — "When that happens, she meets a lot of young people who ask her how they can live their lives differently" sounds plausible near several gaps, but check that no surrounding sentence actually sets up "when that happens".

Answers: 1 D · 2 G · 3 B · 4 F · 5 A · 6 E · (sentence C is the extra, unused option).

3. Your ideas · 8 min

"Your ideas": does a good role model always have to be someone who is very successful? Push for a real opinion with a reason, not just yes/no.

Valencia link: ask whether a Valencian firefighter, teacher or nurse known locally for years of quiet good work is as much a role model as an international athlete like Mira Rai — success measured differently.

4. Vocabulary p.32: Exercises 1–2 · 9 min

Materials: Student's Book p.32.

Ex.1: choose the correct word in each pair of near-synonyms. Ex.2: complete eight fixed idioms (follow in someone's footsteps, come out of nowhere, seize the chance, the secret of your success, open doors for, claim to fame, never got anywhere, a household name) using the word bank.

Ex.1 answers: 1a overcome, b cope · 2a rumour, b gossip · 3a scandal, b disgrace · 4a breakthrough, b comeback.
Ex.2 answers: 1 footsteps · 2 nowhere · 3 chance · 4 secret · 5 doors · 6 fame · 7 anywhere · 8 name.

5. Vocabulary Exercises 3–4 · 11 min

Materials: Student's Book p.32, word-formation table + "How to be more influential" article.

Pairs complete the noun/verb/adjective table, then use the correct forms to fill the gaps in the article about becoming a social-media influencer.

Ex.3 answers: 1 ambition · 2 determination · 3 encourage · 4 fail · 5 famous · 6 influence · 7 inspire · 8 real · 9 sponsor · 10 succeed.
Ex.4 answers: 1 reality · 2 influence · 3 encouragement · 4 sponsor · 5 determination · 6 failure · 7 succeed.

Somerset Arcade: Point Grab · 4 min

Materials: laptop + projector, somerset-arcade/index.html, unit b2a-u03-l02.

FCE I: free-for-all with three players. Friday B2: teams as usual.

Fast finisher

Write one original sentence using three of today's vocabulary words together (e.g. overcome, breakthrough, determination) about a real person's success story.

If short of time, cut:

The final "your ideas" discussion (Block 6) can be shortened to a single quick show-of-hands question. Do not cut the exam task reveal (Block 2) or the word-formation table and article (Block 5) — both are directly examinable content.
