D · Reading - the mascleta problem

Marina lives near Plaza del Ayuntamiento. Every day at 14:00 during Fallas, the mascleta goes off and the whole building shakes. Most of her neighbours love it - they wait for it all year and take it completely for granted, like the sun coming out in June. But Marina has misophonia, so the noise doesn't just annoy her, it's actually harmful to how her day feels. She has learned to put up with the big bangs at the end, but the smell of gunpowder for an hour afterwards really gets on her nerves. "It drives me crazy," she says, "but I would never tell anyone to stop. It's Valencia. You put up with the things you love, too."

True or false? Write T or F.

1. All of Marina's neighbours find the mascleta annoying.T / F
2. Marina has a medical reason for reacting strongly to the noise.T / F
3. Marina wants the mascleta to stop happening.T / F
4. The smell after the mascleta bothers her more than the noise itself.T / F

Answers (check your own work)

A: confidence→confident · despair→desperate · embarrassment→embarrassed · anxiety→anxious · sympathy→sympathetic.
B: 1 on the edge of your seat · 2 get under your skin · 3 lost for words · 4 in agony · 5 with regret.
D: 1 F (Marina reacts differently from most of her neighbours) · 2 T (misophonia) · 3 F (she says she would never ask for it to stop) · 4 T (she "puts up with" the bangs, but the smell "really gets on her nerves").
