A bus stop on Avenida del Cid. The number 99 to Malvarrosa was due eighteen minutes ago.

Emotions & personality adjectives · annoying things · two exam idioms

Eighteen minutes. This bus drives me crazy. Every single Friday it's the same.
You always say that, and then it comes two minutes later. You're so impatient.
I'm not impatient, I'm just tired of it getting on my nerves every week. We're going to miss the good part of the afternoon at the beach.
Relax, both of you. I'm in agony just listening to this argument, and my leg hurts more than my ears.
What happened to your leg?
Football. Someone pushed into me instead of the ball. I feel guilty for losing my temper with him, actually.
I know, it surprised me too. But he kept pushing into the queue for the ball, over and over, and something in me just snapped.
Honestly, that guy would put anyone in a bad mood. He's the definition of a pet peeve.
Sorry I'm late! Is this the queue for the 99? I've had the most stressful morning.
At least I'm not the only one running behind, then. Misery loves company, I suppose.
Nothing. No bus, no message, nothing. This is exactly why I take nothing for granted with public transport.
You're being dramatic now. You always get anxious when we're running late.
I do get anxious, yes, but this time it's a fair reaction. My cousin from Alicante is already waiting for us there.
Is she the one who's always so stubborn about being on time?
Exactly. If we're one minute late, she gets moody about it for the whole afternoon.
Then you two are perfect for each other, because you're both impossible to argue with once you've decided something.
Rude. But maybe true.
A bus finally turns the corner.
There it is! Look at that, I'm actually relieved. I was starting to lose hope.
Twenty-one minutes late and you're relieved. That tells you everything about this bus line.
I'm not relieved, I'm furious. But fine, let's put up with it and go.
Finally! I was starting to think I'd be late for nothing.
Malvarrosa, here we are.
