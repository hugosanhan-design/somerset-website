The Level Ladder, key and diagnosis

What this is measuring

Six parts, one per unit of New Close-up B2 Split A. The class has not studied any of it yet, so a low total is expected and is not the point. Each part is four grammar items and two vocabulary items from the unit's own topic set. The grammar scores tell you what you can move through quickly. The vocabulary scores are a rough signal only, since the book's glossary is new to them. Several items are deliberate B2 discriminators rather than B1 revision: 9 and 10 separate used to from be used to, 14 and 15 separate past perfect simple from continuous, 27 is a perfect modal, and 32 turns on stop plus infinitive of purpose. Those four are the ones worth looking at first.

A student who gets a high total but misses all four of these is a B1 student with good exam instincts, not a B2 student. That is the distinction this sheet exists to make.
