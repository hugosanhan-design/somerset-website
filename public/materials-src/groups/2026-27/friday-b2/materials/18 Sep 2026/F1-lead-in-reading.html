Reading
02 "Aitana, in four feelings" FCE Reading · Part 5 20 min  ·  solo then pairs

Read the text once for gist. On your second read, underline every word that describes how someone feels.

Watercolour illustration — Aitana on stage at Roig Arena, Valencia, autumn light
F1 of 34
Unit 1 of 6

Aitana Ocaña is one of Spain's biggest pop stars, but she insists she is also "the most ordinary person in the world." She grew up in Sant Climent de Llobregat, near Barcelona, and still goes back there when she needs to think. She is currently touring with Cuarto Azul, her fourth album, which came out in May 2025 and won Best Recording Package at the Latin Grammys.

What makes the album interesting isn't the music — it's the range of feelings on it. One song is restless and a little anxious. The next is calm, almost shy. A third is thrilled, full of energy. In an interview with a Madrid newspaper, she said she wrote most of the album in a single autumn month, working late into the night while her city went to sleep.

"I usually write at night because I think more clearly then. But honestly? Tonight I just want to sleep."

For her fans in Valencia, the question is simple: which Aitana is performing when she comes to Roig Arena next spring? The anxious one who worries about every note, or the thrilled one who forgets the audience is there?

  1. Where does Aitana go when she needs to think?
  2. Name two emotions the article uses to describe different songs on the album.
  3. In your own words, what does she mean by "the most ordinary person in the world"?
  4. Compare your mood-map prediction (Section 1) with a partner. Did you agree on which three words describe her?
