Unit 1 Test · Listening script
Audio file: Unit 1 Test - Listening.mp3
Introduction. "Unit 1 test. Part 1, listening. You will hear five short conversations. Listen and circle a, b or c. You will hear each conversation twice."
1. Lucía: Marc, how old is your brother?   Marc: He's fourteen. I'm eleven.  → b (Marc is younger)
2. Boy: This is my cousin Ana. She's got long, curly hair, and she wears glasses.  → c
3. Woman: What are you doing on Saturday, Pablo?   Pablo: I'm having lunch at my grandma's house. We're eating paella!  → b
4. Girl: Look at this photo from Turia Park! My uncle is wearing a bow tie, and he's smiling. My aunt isn't smiling. She's frowning. She's cold!  → b
5. Dad: How old are your friends, Nora?   Nora: Carlos is nine, Elena is twelve, and Sara is ten.  → b (Elena is 12, Sara is 10)

Marking rules

Part 2: the spelling counts (hotter needs two t's, funnier needs -ier). Item 2: accept shorter alone. Item 5 needs cleverer and than for the point.
Part 3: one point only if both am/is/are and the -ing form are right. Item 3 needs are and doing. (The 23 Sep sheet showed am/is/are left out in 33 of 88 answers, so mark this strictly and write down who still drops it.)
Part 1: items 1–2 are circle only. Items 3–5 need the circle and the written word; any readable spelling (paela, 10).
Part 4: one point each, spelling must be readable.
Part 5 (5 points): 1 point for a correct comparison with than; 1 point for a second one; 1 point for a hair description (curly, straight, wavy, blonde, long, short); 1 point for a plan with am/is/are + -ing; 1 point for capital letters and full stops on all four sentences. Score each criterion as yes or no, and quote the child's words on the marking sheet. A fluent sentence that misses the criterion gets no point.
