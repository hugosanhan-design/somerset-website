GROUP 1 · WHO'S TALLER?
Enzo, Ona & Vera O. — together
Both of you use this page once your scripts are complete and checked.
Two cousins meet at a family lunch and can't stop comparing hair and height.
Before you perform: Sit together with your finished pages. Read the whole play out loud once, checking that what you wrote on your blanks matches what your partner actually said. Fix anything that's wrong. Then stand up and perform it for the class — remember to swap who reads first the second time!
★ Now perform it! ★
  • Speak clearly and look at your partner, not just your page.
  • Remember the grammar of the month: comparatives (taller/faster/older than) and plans (What are you doing...? I'm ...ing + tomorrow/later/this afternoon).
  • Use the stage directions in [brackets] — act them out!
  • When you finish, swap roles and read it again from the top.
