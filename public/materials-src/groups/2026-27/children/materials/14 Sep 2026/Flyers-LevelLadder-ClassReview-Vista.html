RUNG 6
Mission 1 · Have you ever…?

Did you ever been to Peñíscola?

HAVE you ever been to Peñíscola?

Asking about life experience — things that ever happened to you — needs Have/Has + ever + a past participle (been, eaten, seen…). Never Did.

Try it out loud: Have you ever eaten paella? Have you ever seen the Fallas?

Mission 2 · Right now! (1/2)

Look! The children play in the park right now.

The children ARE PLAYING in the park right now.

Something happening right now, as we speak = am/is/are + verb-ing. Not the short, everyday form.

Mission 2 · Right now! (2/2)

What are you doing this afternoon? — I do my homework.

I AM DOING my homework.

Same rule, every time: right now = am/is/are + -ing. 10 out of 11 Agents wrote "do" or "does" here — you are not alone, and now you know the fix.

Mission 3 · The past (1/3)

Yesterday is very hot in Valencia.

Yesterday it WAS very hot in Valencia.

"Yesterday" is a past time word. Past time word → past tense verb. is becomes was.

Mission 3 · The past (2/3)

Last year we go to Albacete to visit our family.

Last year we WENT to Albacete.

"Last year" → past tense. go becomes went — a completely different word, so it's an easy one to forget. Say it three times: went, went, went.

RUNG 5
Mission 3 · The past (3/3)

In the year 1800, is/are no cars in Valencia.

In the year 1800, there WERE no cars in Valencia.

Past + a plural word (cars) → were, not was. Past + a plural noun always takes were.

Mission 4 · Giving advice

You look tired. You can go to bed early tonight.

You SHOULD go to bed early tonight.

can = ability or permission ("I can swim"). should = advice, a good idea. Tired → that's advice → should.

Bonus round · four quick fixes

have got
I has got → I have got two brothers.
-s for he/she/it
She go → She goes to school every day.
kind, not kindly
She's very kindly → She's very kind. (describing a person = adjective)
less, not little
She has little homework than me → less homework than me.

Rung 3 → done.
Rung 4 → cracked today.

Five mistakes. Every one of them fixable.
Next time we climb, we climb higher.
