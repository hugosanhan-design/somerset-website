## Marking rules (so the next worksheet is marked the same way)
- **Part A — Continuous (/8):** 1 = am/is/are + verb-ing of the right verb. ½ = verb-ing with no am/is/are. 0 = wrong verb, base form, past, or blank. Spelling not penalised (plaing, coking, waching = fine).
- **Part B — My plan (/8):** 1 = am/is/are + -ing + a time expression. ½ = only one of the two (time word but no am/is/are, or full form but no time word). 0 = neither, Spanish, or blank.
  Both readings of "My plan" accepted: re-writing the sentence (Nico is playing football tomorrow) OR a real personal plan (I'm eating bananas tonight). Time expressions outside the box accepted (in 20 minutes, on Friday).

Madison's sheet has whole items crossed where her Part A was right. Use these scores for the progress report, not the sheet ticks.

## Class-wide findings
1. **The am/is/are gets dropped, not the -ing.** 33 of 88 Part A answers had -ing with no am/is/are. Bruno did it on all 8. Every student except Gael and Madison did it at least once. The -ing ending is learnt; the auxiliary is not.
2. **It gets worse as the page goes on.** Lucas writes "I am" in plans 1–2, then "I visiting / I doing / I cooking" for the rest. Vera Dolz writes "is/are" for A1–3, then drops it for A4–8. Attention, not knowledge, at least for these two.
3. **Item 4 "I do my homework" was the hardest.** Only 3/11 got "I am doing" (Gael, Enzo, Ona). Carla and Vera de la Ossa changed it to "play", Martin to "like", Madison left "do". They probably see "do" as a question word (Do you…?), not a verb that can take -ing.
4. **"My plan" was read two ways.** Some re-wrote the sentence, others wrote real plans (Carla, Gael, Vera Dolz). Gael refused prompts that aren't true for him ("I don't have sister", "I don't have dog"). The label invited that, so both readings were accepted. For the next worksheet: either write "Make it a plan for Nico" or give personal prompts.
5. **Part B ran out of time.** 18 plans left blank across the class, all at the end of the page. Enzo stopped after 2 plans, Madison after 3.
