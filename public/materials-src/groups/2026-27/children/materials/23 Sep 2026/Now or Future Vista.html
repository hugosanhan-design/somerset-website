Right now

Pepe is cleaning the car.
No time word. It's happening right now — look, he's doing it!
Lesson 6
Add one small word…

A plan for later

Pepe is cleaning the car tomorrow.
Same grammar — am/is/are + verb-ing. But "tomorrow" tells us it hasn't happened yet. It's a plan.
