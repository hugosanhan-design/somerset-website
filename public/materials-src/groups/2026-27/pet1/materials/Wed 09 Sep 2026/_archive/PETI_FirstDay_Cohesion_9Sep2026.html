First Day

Nobody in this room knows everybody yet. By the end of the class, everybody will.
TASK 1Find someone whostand up, ask, write
How it works. Stand up and ask your classmates. Ask the full question, do not just show them the paper. When someone says yes, write their name and one extra detail. You cannot use the same name more than three times, so you have to talk to everybody.

Task 3

Now build your six lines

  1. My name's ______ and everyone calls me ______.
  2. I live in ______ with ______.
  3. Every day after school I ______, and at the moment I'm ______.
  4. This summer I ______. I'm quite good at ______.
  5. People say I'm ______ and ______. One day I'd like to be ______.
  6. People are usually surprised that I ______.
Then add a seventh line. Look at your Find Someone Who sheet and finish this: I found out that ______ and I both ______.

TASK 4Practise, then stand uppartner first, class second
Step 1. Say your seven lines to the person next to you. They tell you one thing to improve. Step 2. Say them again to the class, standing. About one minute. You can hold the paper, but look up at least twice.
