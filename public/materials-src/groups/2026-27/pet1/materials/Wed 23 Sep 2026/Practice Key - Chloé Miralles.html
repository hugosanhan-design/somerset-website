Practice key

Answers to the eight exercises in the First Day report. Not for the student.

Exercise 1 · Spelling and capitals

  1. I probably go to the Albufera in summer.
  2. The first thing I see is the beach.
  3. My favourite food is paella.
  4. We usually meet in the centre of Valencia.
  5. My sister wants to be an architect.
  6. I live near the Central Market.

Exercise 2 · Subject and verb

  1. I think it's Valencia CF.
  2. It is very hot in Valencia in August.
  3. They cooked / made / ate a big paella at the beach (any sensible past verb).
  4. I like going (or to go) to Ruzafa on Saturdays.
  5. The Turia is a park. It is very long.
