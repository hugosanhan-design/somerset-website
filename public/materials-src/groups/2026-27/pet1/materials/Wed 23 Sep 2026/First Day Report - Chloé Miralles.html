Part A · Language points

What to remember from your First Day answers.
Pattern 1 · A sentence needs a subject and a verb
You wrote I think is Tanzania, I probally think is arquitec and They a mini tour. Each one misses a word. Say I think it's Tanzania, I'd like to be an architect and They gave me a mini tour.
Pattern 2 · Spelling and capitals
You spelled firts, probally, ussually, arquitec, favorite, center in different ways. Write first, probably, usually, architect, favourite, centre. At Somerset we use British spelling. Write I with a capital letter every time, and give names and titles capitals too.
You wroteBetter versionWhy
I live im Ruaya streetI live on Ruaya Street.Use on with a street. Street names take capitals.
