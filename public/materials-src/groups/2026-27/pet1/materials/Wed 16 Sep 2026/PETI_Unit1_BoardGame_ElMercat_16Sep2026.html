A Unit 1 board game · PET I · 16 September 2026
How to play. Groups of 2–3, each group is one team. Everyone starts at square 1, the Cabanyal fish stalls, with 0 points. Roll a die and move that many squares. Land on a card square: draw the top card from the matching pile and answer it, correct answer earns the points shown. Land on a coloured squares: follow the instruction, no card needed. Wrong answers earn nothing, the group corrects together, no teacher marking mid-game. The game ends the moment a team reaches square 32, the Central Market clock tower, which is worth a bonus. The winner is whichever team has the most points at that moment, not necessarily the team that arrived first, so a well-timed STEAL or DOUBLE near the end can still flip the game.
