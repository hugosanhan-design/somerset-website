Grammar spotlight · teaching note

This is first exposure, not review: 4 of 5 missed diagnostic item 4 (only Álvaro got it). Read the Level Ladder sentence aloud, get them to vote, then build the two columns with them before showing the table. Ask "why not is having in I have a great bike?" to set up the stative box. Teacher's book p9 grammar guide: I'm studying to be a vet is its own temporary-situation example (Alejandra wants to be a vet).

C · Usually… but this week

  1. 1 Álvaro usually plays football on Saturdays, but this week he is watching Dragon Ball because it's raining.
  2. 2 Valeria always does her homework first when she gets home.
  3. 3 Alejandra usually goes to the shopping centre after school, but this week she is studying for a History exam.
  4. 4 Look! Sergio is wearing his basketball shirt today.
  5. 5 Maya usually paints at the weekend, but these days she is helping her brothers with their maths.
  6. 6 The Mercado Central opens at 7:30 every morning.
  7. 7 My cousin usually cycles to work, but this week she is taking the metro because her bike is broken. (the diagnostic sentence again, check who gets it now)
  8. 8 Right now, the firefighters are checking the streets of Ruzafa before the mascletà.
  9. 9 I don't usually eat sushi, but tonight I 'm having / am having sushi with my family. (have = eat is an action, so -ing is fine here; contrast with "I have a bike")
  10. 10 This month, the Valencia CF players are training twice a day.

The sentences use the students' own Task 1 answers (Dragon Ball, homework first, shopping centre, History, basketball, painting). If a detail is wrong, let them correct it, that is extra speaking.

D · Stative verbs: find and fix

  1. 1 I'm knowing the answer already. I know the answer already.
  2. 2 My uncle is owning two flats in Ruzafa. My uncle owns two flats in Ruzafa.
  3. 3 We're walking to the Mercado Central right now. (correct as is)
  4. 4 He's believing every word the politician says. He believes every word the politician says.
  5. 5 They're cooking paella for twenty people today. (correct as is)
  6. 6 I'm wanting to be an architect when I finish school. I want to be an architect when I finish school.
  7. 7 This soup is tasting amazing. This soup tastes amazing.

E · About you, then tell your group

Free answers. Check the form, not the content: habit in present simple, "this week" in present continuous, stative verbs (want, love) with no -ing. Speaking: trio + pair. The report line forces third person: "My partner usually plays… but this week she's …ing". Listen for the missing -s and the missing is, these were Sergio's and Álvaro's patterns on the diagnostic.

Notes for the play

Watch for these on the run-through, don't stop the scene to correct, note them for after: Marina's line uses present simple (work, love, know, read) mixed with a stative verb (love, know) correctly with no -ing. Bruno's line has a real present continuous (I'm filming this week) next to two stative verbs (understand, explain as simple, not continuous). Aitana's line has three stative verbs in a row (love, believe, am patient/cheerful as state adjectives) which is the hardest spot in the script, flag it if a student tries to add -ing there.
