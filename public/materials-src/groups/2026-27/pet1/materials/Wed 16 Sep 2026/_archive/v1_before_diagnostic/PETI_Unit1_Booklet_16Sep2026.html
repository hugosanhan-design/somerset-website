The interview at the Ciutat

A scene at the Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias. Five roles: Narrator, Interviewer, Marina, Bruno, Aitana. Read it through once silently, then in role.

NARRATOR: It's nine o'clock on a Tuesday morning. Inside the Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias, a small office is getting ready for three job interviews. The job is English-speaking guide.

INTERVIEWER: Good morning. Please, sit down. So, Marina, what do you do at the moment?

MARINA: I work as a hairdresser in Ruzafa, but I want a change. I love talking to people, and I know a lot about science, I read about it every night.

INTERVIEWER: Interesting. Are you a confident person?

MARINA: Very. I'm never nervous in front of a group.

NARRATOR: Bruno, the second candidate, walks in. He's carrying a large camera.

INTERVIEWER: Bruno, I see you're holding a camera. What's that for?

BRUNO: Sorry, I'm filming the planetarium this week for another project, I'm a camera operator there on Fridays. But I understand the exhibits really well, I explain them to my little cousins every visit.

INTERVIEWER: And what kind of person are you, Bruno?

BRUNO: Honestly, quite shy at first, but reliable. I never miss a shift.

NARRATOR: Finally, Aitana, the third candidate, sits down. She's smiling.

INTERVIEWER: Aitana, tell me about yourself.

AITANA: I'm an astronaut, well, not a real one! I work in the astronaut exhibit already, I demonstrate the space suits. I love this museum, and I believe I'm the right person for this job.

INTERVIEWER: What makes you a good guide?

AITANA: I'm patient with children, and I'm cheerful even on a long, tiring day.

NARRATOR: The interviewer looks at the three candidates and smiles.

INTERVIEWER: Thank you, all three of you. We're deciding by Friday. Good luck!
