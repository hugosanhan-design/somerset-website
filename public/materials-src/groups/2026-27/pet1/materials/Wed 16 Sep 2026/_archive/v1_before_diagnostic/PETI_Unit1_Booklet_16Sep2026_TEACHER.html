E · About you

Free answers. Check for the correct form, not the content: one present simple habit, one present continuous (this week), one stative verb with no -ing.

Notes for the play

Watch for these on the run-through, don't stop the scene to correct, note them for after: Marina's line uses present simple (work, love, know, read) mixed with a stative verb (love, know) correctly with no -ing. Bruno's line has a real present continuous (I'm filming this week) next to two stative verbs (understand, explain as simple, not continuous). Aitana's line has three stative verbs in a row (love, believe, am patient/cheerful as state adjectives) which is the hardest spot in the script, flag it if a student tries to add -ing there.
