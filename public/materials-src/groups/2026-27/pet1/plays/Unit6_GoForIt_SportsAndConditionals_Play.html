A sports centre in Campanar, Valencia, on sign-up day for new clubs, including pilota valenciana.

Sports & equipment vocabulary · zero & first conditional, unless

Student A
If you join the pilota valenciana club today, you get a free tracksuit.
Student B
Really? Unless I try a class first, I never know if I'll like a new sport.
Student A
That's fair. If you come on Saturday, you can watch a match on the court first.
Student B
Good idea. When people play pilota well, do they always use the whole hand, or a stick too?
Student A
Just the hand, usually. Unless you play a modalitat with a bat, which is a bit different.
Student B
If I don't like pilota, could I try athletics instead?
Student A
Of course. If you enter the athletics tournament in June, you'll run on the new track here.
Student B
Unless I improve a lot, I probably won't win, but I'd still like to try.
Student A
That's the right attitude! If you practise every week, you'll definitely get fitter.
