An estate agent's office near the Plaza del Ayuntamiento, Valencia, where a client is looking for a new property.

Housing & household objects (1) vocabulary · will; be going to

Client
I'm looking for a small flat, maybe with a balcony. Are you going to have anything new this week?
Agent
Actually, yes. A cottage-style property near the port is going to come onto the market on Friday.
Client
That sounds interesting. Will it have a dishwasher and a decent kitchen?
Agent
I'll check the details for you now, one moment.
Client
Thank you. I promise I won't take too much of your time today.
Agent
No problem at all. Yes, it has a dishwasher, a freezer and a nice balcony.
Client
Perfect. I'm going to visit it on Saturday morning, if that's possible.
Agent
Of course. I'll arrange the visit and call you tomorrow to confirm the time.
Client
And what about the rent? Will it be similar to other flats in the area?
Agent
Yes, I think so. I'll send you the exact figure by email this afternoon.
