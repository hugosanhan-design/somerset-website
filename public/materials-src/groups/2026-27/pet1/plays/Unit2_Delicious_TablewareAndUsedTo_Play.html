A video call. Student B, an exchange student in Valencia, is talking to their mum back home.

Mum
Hi love, how's Valencia? Are you getting used to the food yet?
Student B
Slowly! Back home I used to eat dinner at six, here everyone eats at nine or ten.
Mum
That's so late! Are you used to it now?
Student B
Getting there. My flatmate gave me a jug of horchata yesterday, I'd never tried it before.
Mum
What did you drink it from, a glass or a mug?
Student B
A glass, always a glass here, I don't think I've seen a mug since I arrived.
Mum
Interesting. And what about breakfast, is it different?
Student B
Completely. I used to have cereal in a bowl, but here people just have a small coffee and toast on a plate.
Mum
Are you missing your old breakfast?
Student B
A bit, but I'm getting used to the new way. Actually, I bought a jar of local honey for the toast, it's delicious.
Mum
That sounds lovely. I'm glad you're settling in.
