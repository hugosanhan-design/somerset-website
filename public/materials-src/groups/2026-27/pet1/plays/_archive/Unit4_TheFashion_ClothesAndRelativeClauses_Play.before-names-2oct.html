A stall at the Mercado de Colón, Valencia, that sells antique jewellery and vintage accessories.

Clothes & materials vocabulary · relative clauses

Nora
Look at this bracelet, the one which has the little glass beads! It's beautiful.
Seller
Ah, that's an antique piece, from a family whose grandmother made jewellery for fifty years.
Nora
Is it silver, or is it a different metal?
Seller
It's silver with glass, actually. And look at this handkerchief, which is silk, from the same collection.
Nora
I love it. Do you have anything more casual, something that isn't so old-fashioned?
Seller
Of course. This scarf, which is cotton, is much more modern and fashionable.
Nora
That's the one! Is this the stall where you found the leather gloves last month, too?
Seller
Yes, exactly, the stall where I find most of my best pieces. I know a tailor whose workshop is just behind the market, who can fix anything.
Nora
Perfect. I'll take the bracelet and the scarf, please.
Seller
Excellent choice. That's a bargain, by the way, for two original pieces like these.

A vintage clothes boutique on a narrow street in Ruzafa, Valencia, on a Saturday afternoon.

This shop is amazing, it's the place where I always find the best second-hand jackets.

Let me try it on. Actually, it's quite loose, but I like that.
Paula
It suits you. My cousin, who works here on Saturdays, told me it's from the 1980s.
Diego
Really? That's the moment when fashion was at its best, according to my dad.
Paula
Ha! Look, there's a silk blouse too, which would go with your sister's new skirt.
Diego
She'd love that. Is there a changing room where I could try the jacket on properly?

Yes, it's over there, the door which has the curtain.
Diego
Perfect. I think I've found my new fashionable jacket for the whole winter.

A fabric shop near the Plaza del Ayuntamiento, Valencia, a few weeks before Fallas, where a fallera's dress is being made.

Clothes & materials vocabulary · relative clauses

Marisol
This is the shop where my mother always buys the silk for my Fallas dress.
Tailor
Welcome back! Is this the dress whose sleeves needed to be fixed last year?
Marisol
Yes, exactly that one. I need a new fabric, something which is elegant but not too tight.
Tailor
I have this silk, which comes from a small workshop whose owner I've known for years.
Marisol
It's beautiful. Is it the same silk which you used for my cousin's dress?
Tailor
A similar one, yes, from the day when the new collection arrived.
Marisol
I'll take three metres, please, and some antique buttons if you have them.
Tailor
Of course, I have a box of buttons which are exactly the right colour.
Marisol
Perfect. This is going to be the most original dress of the whole cabalgata.
Tailor
I'm sure it will. You're the customer whose dresses I enjoy making the most.
Marisol
This is the shop where my mother always buys the silk for my Fallas dress.
Tailor
Welcome back! Is this the dress whose sleeves needed to be fixed last year?
Marisol's grandmother, whose own Fallas dress is fifty years old, arrives with a bag of buttons.
Iaia
I brought some antique buttons, the ones which I saved from my own dress.
Marisol
Iaia, they're perfect! I need a new fabric too, something which is elegant but not too tight.
Tailor
I have this silk, which comes from a small workshop whose owner I've known for years.
Iaia
That's the same silk which they used for my dress, all those years ago.
Marisol
I'll take three metres, please, and I'll use Iaia's antique buttons.
Tailor
A wonderful choice. That's the moment when a dress becomes a family tradition.
Iaia
This is going to be the most original dress of the whole cabalgata, without a doubt.
Marisol
Thanks to both of you, whose help I couldn't have done this without.
