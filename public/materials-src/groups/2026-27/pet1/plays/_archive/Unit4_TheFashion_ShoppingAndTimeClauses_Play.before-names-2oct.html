A clothes shop near the Colón market, Valencia, a customer trying on a shirt before a job interview.

Shopping vocabulary · phrasal verbs (1) · clauses with time expressions

Assistant
Can I help you find anything today?
Customer
Yes, I'm looking for a shirt to go with these trousers. I have an interview as soon as I finish shopping.
Assistant
This blue one is a real bargain, only twenty euros. Would you like to try it on?
Customer
Yes, please. I'll pick up a tie too, until I decide which shirt is best.
Assistant
Of course, the changing room is over there.
The customer tries on the shirt.
Customer
How is it? It feels a little tight around the shoulders.
Assistant
It looks great, actually. We can find a bigger size before you leave, if you prefer.
Customer
Good idea. Can I pay for it with my credit card?
Assistant
Of course. Don't forget your receipt, and good luck with the interview the moment it starts!
Customer
Thank you, I'll shop around here again as soon as I get my first pay cheque.
Assistant

Customer
Yes, I'm looking for a shirt to go with these trousers. I have an interview as soon as I finish shopping.

The Sunday mercadillo (flea market) in Ruzafa, Valencia, two friends shopping for second-hand clothes.

Thanks for the warning. I'll shop around a bit more once I've tried this on.

Alba
