A padel club in Campanar, Valencia, on a Saturday morning.

Sports clothes & collocations vocabulary · second conditional

Lucas
If I had better trainers, I would move much faster on this court.
Marina
If you practised more, you could beat me without new trainers at all!
Lucas
Maybe. If I joined a club here, would I get free lessons?
Marina
I think so. If I were you, I'd ask at reception before we start.
Lucas
Good idea. If we scored more points today, we'd finally win a match against Diego.
Marina
If we didn't wear a tracksuit in this heat, we'd feel much cooler, honestly.
Lucas
True. If I had my sunglasses, I could see the ball better against the sky.
Marina
If you hit the ball harder, you would win more points from the back of the court.
Lucas
OK, let's try that. If we enter the summer competition, we'd need to practise every week.
Marina
Agreed. If we won the competition, I'd be so proud of us!
Lucas
If I had better trainers, I would move much faster on this court.
Marina
If you practised more, you could beat me without new trainers at all!
Diego, their regular padel partner, arrives with his racket bag.
Diego
If I joined this club properly, would I get free lessons too?
Marina
I think so. If I were you, I'd ask at reception before we start.
Lucas
Good idea. If we scored more points today, we'd finally win a match against you, Diego!
Diego
If you didn't wear a tracksuit in this heat, you'd feel much cooler, both of you.
Marina
True. If I had my sunglasses, I could see the ball better against the sky.
Lucas
If you hit the ball harder, Diego, you would win more points from the back.
Diego
If we entered the summer competition together, we'd need to practise every week.

The seasonal ice rink in the city centre of Valencia, at Christmas time.

Sports clothes & collocations vocabulary · second conditional

Alba

Hugo
Of course! If I were braver, I'd try skating backwards like that instructor.
Alba
If we practised every weekend, we could enter the beginners' competition in January.
Hugo
If I scored well in that competition, I'd want to join a real club after.
Alba
If we didn't wear a helmet here, would they let us skate at all?
Hugo
No, definitely not. If you took your helmet off, they'd stop you immediately.
Alba
Fair enough. If I had a warmer tracksuit, I'd stay out here for hours.
Hugo
Same. If this rink were open all year, I'd come every single week.
Alba
If I had gloves like yours, my hands wouldn't be so cold right now.
Hugo

On the sand at La Malvarrosa beach, Valencia, before an early-morning swim.

Sports clothes & collocations vocabulary · second conditional

Iris
If I had a proper swimming costume like yours, I'd feel much more confident.
Teo
If you practised holding your breath longer, you could try free diving with me.
Iris
If I could swim down five metres, would that be enough to start?
Teo
Almost. If you rode a boat further out with an instructor, it would be much safer.
Iris
If I joined a diving club here, would they teach me properly?
Teo
Definitely. If you entered a beginners' course, you'd learn all the safety rules first.
Iris
If the water were warmer today, I'd stay in for an hour, easily.
Teo
If we did a workout on the sand first, we'd warm up before going in.
Iris
Good plan. If I got really good at this, I'd love to dive professionally one day.
Teo
If you kept practising all summer, I really think you could.
Iris
If I had a proper swimming costume like yours, I'd feel much more confident.
Teo
If you practised holding your breath longer, you could try free diving with me.
Pau, a qualified diving instructor, walks down to the water with his equipment.
Pau
If you rode the boat further out with me, it would be much safer for a first try.
Iris
If I joined a diving club here, would you teach me properly?
Pau
Definitely. If you entered a beginners' course, you'd learn all the safety rules first.
