A small boat on the Albufera, the coastal lagoon just south of Valencia, at sunset.

Geographical features · present perfect simple & continuous

Guide
Welcome aboard! Have you ever been on the Albufera before?
Marc
No, never. I've heard it's not really an ocean, more like a lagoon between the coast and the rice fields.
Guide
Exactly right. This whole area used to be a much bigger stretch of water, but people have been growing rice here for centuries, so it's got smaller.
Marc
Has it changed a lot recently?
Guide
Yes, actually. Scientists have been studying the water quality for years, and it's improved a lot since the 1990s.
Marc
That's good news. I've never seen so many flamingos before, look at the coast over there!
Guide
The flamingos have been coming back every winter since the water improved. Some visitors have even seen them from the cliff path on the north side.
The boat drifts closer to a small island.
Marc
Have you worked here long?
Guide
I've been working on these boats for about eight years now. I've never got tired of this view.
Marc
I completely understand why.

A morning run along the Turia gardens, the long park built where a river used to flow through Valencia.

Geographical features · present perfect simple & continuous

Cova
I've been running here every morning this week. It's such a nice valley of green space through the whole city.
Dani
I know! Have you ever seen a photo of what this looked like before it became a park?
Cova
No, what was it like?
Dani
It used to be a real river, like a stream that grew huge after storms. In 1957 it flooded badly, so the city has diverted it outside Valencia ever since.
Cova
Wow, so this whole path has been a riverbed, not a natural valley?
Dani
Exactly. The city has planted thousands of trees here since then, and it's become one of the biggest urban parks in Spain.
Cova
Have you always lived so close to it?
Dani
Yes, I've lived near here my whole life. I've never got tired of running through it.
They slow down near a bridge.
Cova
