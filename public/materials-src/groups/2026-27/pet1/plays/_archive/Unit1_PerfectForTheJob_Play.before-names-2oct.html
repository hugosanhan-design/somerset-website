A small office at the Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias, during job interviews for an English-speaking guide.

Present simple & present continuous · stative verbs · personality adjectives

Interviewer
Good afternoon, Aitana. Please, sit down. So, what do you do at the moment?
Aitana
At the moment I'm training as a science guide here at the Ciutat, but I usually work as a primary school teacher in Ruzafa.
Interviewer
Interesting. Are you a confident person?
Aitana
Very. I'm not usually nervous, but I have to admit, right now I'm feeling a little nervous.
Interviewer
That's understandable. What kind of person are you, generally?
Aitana
I'm sociable and hard-working. My students say I'm patient too, even on a long day.
Interviewer
Good qualities for a guide. Do you know much about the museum?
Aitana
I do. I love science, and I read about the exhibits every evening. I think the Hemisferic is the most interesting building here.
Interviewer
I agree, it's beautiful. What's that you're holding?
Aitana
Oh, this is my notebook. I'm writing some notes for my speech right now, but I usually keep it for lesson planning.
Interviewer
Very organised. One more question, are you a careful person?
Aitana
Extremely. I'm always careful with the children in my class, and I never lose things. Actually, I have three years of experience working with groups.
Interviewer
Last question. Are your colleagues able to rely on you?
Aitana
Definitely, I'm very reliable. I'm never late, and I'm always calm under pressure. I believe honesty matters most in this job, so I'm always honest with visitors, even when I don't know an answer.
Interviewer
Wonderful. And are you usually a cheerful person?
Aitana
Always. I love making people smile, especially the children.
Interviewer
That's exactly what we're looking for. Thank you, Aitana. We're deciding by Friday.
Aitana
Thank you. I really hope I get it, this job sounds perfect for me.
Interviewer

Aitana
Very. I'm not usually nervous, but right now, with someone else listening too, I'm feeling a little nervous.
Marina
Don't worry, I always get nervous before interviews as well, it's completely normal.
Interviewer

Aitana
I'm sociable and hard-working. My students say I'm patient too, even on a difficult day.
Marina
I'm exactly the same, sociable and hard-working. I think we'd both do well here.

I know it well too, I visit every weekend, I'm always learning something new there.

Extremely. I'm always careful with the children in my class, and I never lose things. I have three years of experience working with groups.
