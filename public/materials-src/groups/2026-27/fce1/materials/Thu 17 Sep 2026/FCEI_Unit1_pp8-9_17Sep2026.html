TASK 4What you do, and what you are doingpart A, on your own
Student's Book page 9. The eleven uses the book lists are all here, in order of appearance.
Present simple for scientific facts and general truths, permanent situations, habits and repeated actions, timetabled and scheduled events, and narratives. Present continuous for what is happening at the moment, temporary situations, fixed arrangements for the future, annoying habits with always or forever, changing situations (often with a comparative), and describing a picture. Stative verbs such as hate, want, know and seem are not normally used in the continuous at all.
Put each verb in brackets into the present simple or the present continuous. There are thirteen.

Hi Aidan,

Thanks for the photos. Shrewsbury (look) tiny next to Valencia, but I (love) that river. In the second photo you (stand) outside a school, so I suppose that is the new one.

Normally I (get) the metro at half past seven, but they (repair) the line all this month, so I (walk) instead. It (take) forty minutes and my English (get) better and better, because I listen to podcasts the whole way.

My sister (stay) with us until December. She (always, leave) her wet towels on my bed.

Our train to Barcelona (leave) at 7.40 on Friday and we (meet) my cousin there at lunchtime, so I will not be online that morning. I (want) to hear about the new school before then.
