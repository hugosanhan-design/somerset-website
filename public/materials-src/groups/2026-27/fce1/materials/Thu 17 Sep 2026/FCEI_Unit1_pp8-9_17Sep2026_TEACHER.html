Unit 1, pages 8 and 9, done off the book

What changed, and why

This replaces the booklet of 15 September for pages 8 and 9. That one was built from the teacher's-book notes alone, before the Student's Book was on disk. Now that it is, three things were wrong with it and are fixed here.

One. Its Task 2 drilled the -ed against -ing contrast in a three-column grid. The book never sets that exercise. Page 8 exercise 2 is simply noun to adjective, and seven of the ten adjectives have no -ing form at all. The contrast survives here as a fast-finisher box on page 1, labelled as not being in the book.

Two. Its personality list had cheerful and gentle, which are not in exercise 4, and was missing arrogant and childish, which are. Page 3 now carries the book's ten exactly.

Three. It skipped exercise 3 entirely, the five expressions: in agony, on the edge of your seat, get under your skin, lost for words, with regret. Those are the highest-value items on the page for the exam and they are now Task 2.

Martina and Víctor. Unchanged from Tuesday. Víctor gets closed answer sets and written-out questions so he cannot dry up; if he stalls, ask a prepared question rather than waiting. Martina's work is the spelling traps on page 1 and the register box on page 2, not the speaking, which she finds easy.

Task 2, the five expressions  Student's Book p8 ex 3 · TB key 1b 2e 3a 4c 5d

ExpressionKeyMeaning, and the book's example
1in agonybfeeling a lot of pain. My tooth hurt so much, I was in agony.

All five are in the Vocabulary reference on page 95, so they are testable in the End of Unit Test. Number 4 is the one they will actually use; number 5 is the one that earns marks in Writing Part 2.
