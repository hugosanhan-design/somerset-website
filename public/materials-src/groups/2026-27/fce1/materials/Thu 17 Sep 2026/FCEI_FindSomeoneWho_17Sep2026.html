Find your common ground

Ten questions about the things you actually like. Ask the other two, write short notes on what they say, then work out together what you all have in common.
MARTINA’S COPY
STEP 1Interview the other twoabout 12 minutes
Take turns asking each other these ten questions — don’t just wait for your turn to talk. Listen properly and jot down a short note on what Víctor and Isabella answer, in their own words if you can.
QuestionVíctor saysIsabella says
1What kind of music do you actually listen to, when nobody else is choosing the playlist?
2What’s the last series you got hooked on — the one you couldn’t stop watching?
3Is there a food you could eat every single day and never get tired of?
4What do you usually do on a lazy Saturday with absolutely no plans?
5Have you got a favourite place to hang out in Valencia?VALENCIA
6If you had two weeks off and enough money, where in the world would you go?
7Do you play a sport, or is there one you’d love to try one day?
8Is there a public figure — an athlete, singer, actor — you genuinely admire? Why?
9What’s something you’re weirdly good at that would surprise people?
10Would you rather spend a free evening with friends, alone, or somewhere in between? Why?
Step 2 · Common ground, about 5 minutes As a group of three, compare your notes out loud. Agree on two or three things that are true for all three of you — or at least two of you — and write them below.
