One finding worth a second look

All three, same wrong answer

Item 8 — "When I was small, we ___ spend every March at my grandmother's house for Fallas." Key: would (repeated past action). All three of them wrote are used to instead, with no exceptions. That's not three separate mistakes — it's one shared gap: would for a repeated past habit hasn't landed at all yet, even though used to and be used to (items 9 and 10) are more mixed. Worth opening Unit 2 with this contrast specifically, not assuming it'll fall out of general teaching.

Also worth flagging

Item 1 — "Nuria ___ about her results at the moment, though she is normally calm." Key: is worrying (stative verb pushed into the continuous for a temporary state). All three chose worries or has worried instead — a 3/3 miss on a single item, in the one unit that's supposedly already half-taught. Either it's a genuinely advanced exception nobody's covered explicitly yet, or worth a second look at whether the item is landing as intended.
