Listening · Question 1 of 8

How does she describe her phobia of dentists?

  • Aembarrassing
  • Bsevere
  • Clogical
She says she feels ‘a bit silly’ and looks stressed — not that it’s serious (severe), and she explicitly says there ISN’T a reason (rules out logical).
Listening · Question 2 of 8

What job does the boy like the least?

  • Awashing the dishes
  • Bwatering the plants
  • Cloading the dishwasher
‘Why do I have to do the washing-up every evening? You know how much I hate it!’ He says he doesn’t mind watering the plants, and wouldn’t mind loading a dishwasher.
Listening · Question 3 of 8

What is the head teacher worried about?

  • Athe number of classes the student has missed
  • Bthe student's problems with concentration
  • Cthe student's poor academic results
‘I want to talk to you about your grades... they’ve fallen quite dramatically.’ Missed classes and concentration are things she asks ABOUT, not the stated worry itself.
Listening · Question 4 of 8

What is the man anxious about before his driving test?

  • Aneeding to take more lessons
  • Bfailing the test
  • Cnot turning up for the test
‘I’m pretty sure I’d pass the test’ — he’s confident about the driving itself. What scares him is getting cold feet and not showing up.
Listening · Question 5 of 8

What is her opinion of the horror film she saw last night?

  • AIt was very amusing.
  • BIt was very scary.
  • CIt was disappointing.
‘I was absolutely terrified! It was so frightening!’ — unusual for her, since horror films normally just make her laugh.
Listening · Question 6 of 8

Why is the woman talking to her daughter?

  • Ato say that she is annoyed
  • Bto cheer her daughter up
  • Cto show a lack of confidence in her daughter
‘It’s not the end of the world!... Try to be positive... you should be very proud of what you’ve achieved.’ Reassuring and supportive throughout.
Listening · Question 7 of 8

How is the man feeling on the plane?

  • Aashamed
  • Brelieved
  • Cangry
‘I actually feel bad about it... I guess it’s too late to apologise.’ He regrets losing his temper with the check-in guy — not currently relieved or angry, but ashamed.
Listening · Question 8 of 8

How does he feel when he comes home to an empty house?

  • Alonely
  • Bfrightened
  • Ccalm
‘I do miss them and their company when I come home to a cold, dark, empty house. That’s when I realise how alone I am.’
