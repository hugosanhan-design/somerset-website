Unit 3 — Role models

Review Play · FCE I

Two classmates comparing notes on their "role model" presentations.

Past perfect simple & continuous · success & fame

Ruby
Have you finished your role model presentation, Ethan? Mine's a disaster.
Ethan
Almost. I chose Joaquín Sorolla, actually, the Valencian painter.
Ruby
Good choice. By the time he was twenty, he had already won a national painting prize, hadn't he?
Ethan
Exactly. He'd been studying art in Valencia for years before that prize even happened.
Ruby
That's dedication. What had he achieved before he became internationally famous?
Ethan
Well, he'd already sold paintings across Europe before people in America even knew his name.
Ruby
Wow. Had he always wanted to be a painter, or did he try other things first?
Ethan
I think he'd been drawing since he was a small child. His family had actually pushed him toward a "safer" career.
Ruby
Interesting. Success doesn't always come fast, does it?
Ethan
No, and fame definitely didn't come easily. He'd been struggling for recognition for a long time before his big breakthrough.
Ruby
My role model is different. She'd already broken three records before she turned eighteen.
Ethan
Who is she?
Ruby
A swimmer. She'd been training twice a day for years before anyone noticed her talent.
Ethan
That's intense. Had she ever thought about giving up?
Ruby
Apparently, yes. She'd nearly quit the year before her first big win.
Ethan
See, that's the part people forget. Everyone only sees the fame, not the failure before it.
Ruby
True. By the time she became successful, she'd already failed more times than most people even try.
Ethan
That's actually a great line for your presentation.
Ruby
Thanks! Maybe I'll mention Sorolla too, since he'd struggled the same way.
Ethan
Good idea. Success and struggle always seem to go together, don't they?
