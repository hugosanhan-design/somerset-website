Unit 2 — Global culture?

Review Play · FCE I

Break time, comparing notes on last week's Fallas parade.

Past simple & past continuous · used to / would · traditions & festivals

Leo
So how was the Fallas parade last week? I heard it was chaos.
Maya
It was amazing, actually. We were standing in the crowd when the fireworks suddenly started.
Leo
The mascletà? That's so loud, isn't it?
Maya
Incredibly loud. Everyone was covering their ears while the ninots burned in the square.
Leo
When I was a kid, we used to watch it on TV instead of going in person.
Maya
Really? We always used to go every year with my grandmother. It's a real family tradition for us.
Leo
My family didn't use to celebrate festivals like that at all. We moved around too much.
Maya
That sounds hard. Did you have any traditions growing up, though?
Leo
We used to celebrate a harvest festival back home, actually. Everyone would dress in traditional clothes.
Maya
That sounds beautiful. What happened at it?
Leo
Well, one year, while my dad was setting up the decorations, a goat ate half of them.
Maya
No way! What did everyone do?
Leo
Everyone just laughed. It became a family joke after that; we still talk about "the goat incident."
Maya
That's brilliant. Traditions like that are what make a culture special, I think.
Leo
I agree. So did you dress up for Fallas too?
Maya
Of course. I wore a traditional Valencian dress, the falla dress, while my brother lit little firecrackers outside.
Leo
That sounds like a proper celebration. Did anything go wrong?
Maya
Actually, yes. While we were walking to the bonfire, it started raining.
Leo
Typical! What did you do?
Maya
We just danced in the rain anyway. Nobody wanted to go home early.
Leo
I wish I'd been there. Next year, will you take me?
Maya
Definitely. You used to miss out on festivals, but not anymore.
