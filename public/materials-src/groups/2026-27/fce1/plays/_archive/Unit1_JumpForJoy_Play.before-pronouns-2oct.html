Unit 1 — Jump for joy

Review Play · FCE I

1. Choose how many students are acting (buttons above). 2. Type their names.

Each name appears everywhere in the play. Until you type one, the part shows as Student A, B or C. Not saved: names clear when you reload.

Backstage at the school talent show, minutes before curtain-up.

Present simple & present continuous · emotions & personality

Student A
Student B, why are you pacing like that? You're making me nervous too.
Student B
I'm pacing because I'm absolutely terrified. I always get nervous before shows, but tonight is worse.
Student A
You never usually panic, though. You're normally so confident on stage.
Student B
I know, but look, my hands are actually shaking right now.
Student A
That's just excitement. You practise in the Turia Gardens every single afternoon. You never miss a session.
Student B
True, I love rehearsing there, with the fountains going behind us. It usually calms me down.
Student A
See? You're one of the most hard-working people I know. You don't give up easily.
Student B
You're just saying that to calm me down. What about you? You don't look nervous at all.
Student A
I am nervous, I'm just better at hiding it. My leg is bouncing under this chair right now.
Student B
Ha, you're right, it is! You're usually so laid-back about everything.
Student A
Not tonight. Student C just texted; he says the judges are already taking their seats.
Student B
Don't tell me that, I feel sick again!
Student A
Sorry. Listen, you're a brilliant dancer and everyone here knows it.
Student B
Thanks. I actually feel a bit calmer now, believe it or not.
Student A
See, you're getting your confidence back already.
Student B
Maybe. I still think you're more stubborn than nervous, because you never admit when you're scared.
Student A
Fair point. Come on, they're calling our names.
Student B
Wait, I'm feeling anxious all over again!
Student A
You'll be fine, you always are.
Student B
Okay... okay. I'm actually excited now, not just terrified.
Student A
That's the Student B I know. Let's go jump for joy out there.
Student A
Student B, why are you pacing like that? You're making me nervous too.
Student B
