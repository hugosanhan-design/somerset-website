Unit 5 — Emerging technologies

Review Play · FCE I

Borrowing a laptop, and arguing about screen time, after school.

Modals: permission / obligation / ability / probability · technology & compound nouns

Finn
Can I borrow your laptop? Mine won't turn on, the battery life is basically zero now.
Zara
You can, but you have to give it back by six, I've got homework.
Finn
Deal. Do you know if I'm allowed to install a new app on it, or is that against the rules?
Zara
You should ask first, but it's probably fine. Just don't download anything weird.
Finn
I won't! Have you seen the new robot exhibition at the Museu de les Ciències, by the way?
Zara
No, but I've heard it's amazing. Apparently the robots can actually recognise faces now.
Finn
That might be true, but I'm not sure I believe everything I read online.
Zara
Fair. Anyway, you must be careful with your screen time, you know. You're always on that phone.
Finn
I know, I know. I can't help it, though, it's basically a compound noun for "addiction" at this point.
Zara
Ha! Speaking of which, my smartphone updated itself overnight and now nothing works properly.
Finn
That happens. You could try restarting it, that usually fixes a software update problem.
Zara
I already tried that. It must be something else, then.
Finn
Maybe it can't handle the new voice assistant feature. That thing uses a lot of memory.
Zara
You might be right. Could you take a look at it for me later?
Finn
Sure, I should be able to fix it, I fixed my mum's touchscreen last week.
Zara
Thanks. Oh, and you have to remember your own charger this time, not mine.
Finn
I will, I promise. Anyway, are we allowed to bring phones on the school trip?
Zara
I don't think so. We might be able to use them for photos only, I heard.
Finn
That could be annoying. I can't imagine three hours with no wifi.
Zara
You'll survive. Right, give me my laptop back, you've had it for ages.
