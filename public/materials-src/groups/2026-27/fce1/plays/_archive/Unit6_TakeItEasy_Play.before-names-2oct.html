Unit 6 — Take it easy

Review Play · FCE I

Deciding what sport to try this term, after the last exam of the week.

-ing form & infinitives · sports

Kit
I've decided to take up a new sport this term. I'm bored of just going to the gym.
Wren
Really? What are you thinking of trying?
Kit
I fancy trying padel, actually. Everyone in Valencia seems to be playing it lately.
Wren
I love watching it, but I've never managed to hit the ball properly myself.
Kit
It can't be that hard. Do you want to come and try it with me on Saturday?
Wren
I'd love to, but I promised to help my dad with something that day.
Kit
Fair enough. Do you fancy going running instead, one evening this week?
Wren
I hate running, to be honest. I always end up hating every minute of it.
Kit
Come on, it's good for your stamina. I've started training for the Valencia Marathon next year.
Wren
You're actually going to run a marathon? That's ambitious.
Kit
I know, it's a bit mad. I keep avoiding the longer runs, though, if I'm honest.
Wren
You need to stop avoiding them, then, or you'll never manage to finish the race.
Kit
You're right. Anyway, would you consider joining a team sport instead? Less boring than running alone.
Wren
I wouldn't mind trying volleyball, actually. My cousin plays for a local team here.
Kit
You should give it a go. It's great for meeting new people too.
Wren
True. I do enjoy playing with a team more than exercising by myself.
Kit
Same here. I refuse to spend my whole weekend at the gym, it's so boring.
Wren
Fair. Right, I'd better get going, I need to finish tidying my room before dinner.
Kit
Okay, let me know if you decide to try padel with me sometime.
Wren
I will. Enjoy your run, if you actually manage to go.
Kit
Very funny. See you tomorrow.
