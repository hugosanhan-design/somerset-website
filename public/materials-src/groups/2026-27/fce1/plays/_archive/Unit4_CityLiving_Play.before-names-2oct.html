Unit 4 — City living

Review Play · FCE I

Two friends debating university cities versus staying in Valencia.

Future forms: will / going to / present continuous · cities

Ollie
Have you decided where you're applying for university yet?
Pippa
Not completely, but I think I'm going to apply to Manchester. It's meant to be an amazing city.
Ollie
Really? I'm meeting my cousin this weekend, actually; she lives there. I'll ask her what it's really like.
Pippa
Thanks! I've heard the public transport is brilliant, but the cost of living is really high.
Ollie
Everywhere's expensive these days. I'm going to stay closer to home, I think. Valencia suits me fine.
Pippa
Won't you get bored, though? Small cities can feel a bit quiet after a while.
Ollie
Valencia isn't that small! And it's not overcrowded like some capitals.
Pippa
True. I do love the beach here. I'm not sure a big city will have that.
Ollie
Some do. Anyway, are you going to visit before you decide?
Pippa
Yes, we're flying up in October actually, it's already booked.
Ollie
That's soon. What will you look at while you're there?
Pippa
The university area, and probably the suburbs too, to see if the commute is manageable.
Ollie
Good plan. I bet the traffic will be terrible compared to here.
Pippa
Probably. Valencia's public transport is honestly underrated, isn't it?
Ollie
Definitely. I'm going to miss the Metrovalencia if I ever move away.
Pippa
Same. Anyway, I promise I'll send you photos of the skyline when I'm there.
Ollie
You'd better. I'll visit you at some point, don't worry.
Pippa
You will? That would be amazing.
Ollie
Of course. I'm not letting you disappear into some huge city without me.
Pippa
Deal. Right, I'm meeting my mum for dinner in ten minutes, I should go.
Ollie
Okay, good luck deciding. I think you'll make the right choice either way.
