# B1 Unit 1 · Personality lesson · ten illustrated people

For ChatGPT only (never ElevenLabs). Generate each image and save it **in this folder** with the **exact filename** in the table. When all ten are here, tell Claude and they get wired into the drag-and-drop lesson and logged in `images.json`.

Each picture shows the person **already being** the adjective. In the lesson the picture first appears faded and blurred, then comes to life in full colour when the student drops the right word on it.

## Style (identical for all ten: paste this before every prompt)

> Warm flat editorial illustration, gentle paper grain, limited palette of soft natural colours with small fresh-green (#6BAE2E) accents, realistic proportions (not cartoon, not chibi, not anime). Head and shoulders, the person centred, facing the viewer, with clear space all round the head so the image can be cropped into a circle. Plain warm cream background (#F7F3EA). The emotion must be obvious at a glance on a small phone screen. No text, no logos or brands, 3:2 landscape format.

**Tip:** make the first one, then for the other nine start the prompt with "Same illustration style as the previous image." That keeps the set looking like one family.
