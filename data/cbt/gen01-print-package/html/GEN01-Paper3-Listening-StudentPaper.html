Part 1

You will hear people talking in eight different situations. For questions 1–8, choose the best answer (A, B or C). You will hear each recording twice.

1  You hear a woman talking about a course she took.
What does the speaker say about the pottery course?
A   A family member encouraged her to try it.B   She found it easy from the very first lesson.C   She has decided not to continue with it.
2  You hear a man talking about moving house.
How did the speaker find the flat he eventually bought?
A   He had been searching for it online for months.B   A colleague told him about it.C   He saw it advertised in a local shop window.
3  You hear a teenager talking about a school trip.
What does the speaker say about the school trip?
A   He had never visited the museum before.B   His enthusiasm grew once he was there.C   He was disappointed by the exhibition.
4  You hear a woman talking about her job.
What does the speaker say about her career change?
A   She regrets the drop in income.B   She still finds the job exhausting.C   She prioritised job satisfaction over salary.
5  You hear a man talking about a sports injury.
What does the speaker say about his recovery?
A   He is following his physio's advice despite his impatience.B   He returned to playing sooner than the physio recommended.C   He disagreed with the physio's assessment of his injury.
6  You hear a woman talking about learning a language.
What has the speaker decided to do about her Portuguese?
A   Spend more time using the language app.B   Stop learning the language for now.C   Start attending a class with other people.
7  You hear a man talking about a restaurant.
What does the speaker say about the restaurant?
A   The food did not live up to his expectations.B   He would avoid returning because of the slow service.C   The meal took much longer than he had planned.
8  You hear a woman talking about volunteering.
What does the speaker say surprised her most about volunteering?
A   How much she enjoyed being around animals.B   How close she became to the other volunteers.C   How long she has continued doing it.

Part 4

You will hear an interview with a wildlife photographer called Claire Byrne. For questions 24–30, choose the best answer (A, B or C).

24  Why did Claire start photographing urban wildlife?
A   She had deliberately planned the project for years.B   An unplanned delay in London led her to notice foxes nearby.C   A publisher specifically commissioned the project.
25  What does Claire say about photographing animals in cities compared to somewhere like the Amazon?
A   It required less patience than she expected.B   It was more physically demanding overall.C   It was harder in ways people don't expect.
26  How does Claire feel about the photograph of the fox family?
A   It is her proudest image, but not her personal favourite.B   It is both her proudest and her favourite image.C   She now regrets including it in the book.
27  What does Claire's favourite photograph in the book show?
A   A dramatic scene of foxes at night.B   A heron standing still on a rooftop.C   A busy street full of urban wildlife.
28  What message does Claire want the project to communicate?
A   Wildlife photography requires travelling to remote places.B   Wildlife exists close to us if we look for it.C   Urban animals are becoming increasingly rare.
29  What advice does Claire give to people starting out in wildlife photography?
A   Invest in the best equipment available before beginning.B   Focus on spending time outdoors observing animals.C   Avoid urban locations until gaining more experience.
30  What does Claire say about her future plans?
A   She has a confirmed project starting in Spain.B   She intends to keep working without a break.C   She has an unconfirmed idea for a project near Valencia.
